# Doctor Who — Test Menu, opto theory and playfield-adjustment passages

Transcribed from `Bally_1992_Doctor_Who_Manual.pdf` (PDF pages 3, 4, 34-39, 66 and 70-72, printed pages
`DOCTOR WHO` front-matter warning, `1-9`, `1-15` to `1-20`, `1-47`, `1-51` to `1-53`). The pages are
image-only 300 dpi scans; the text below was taken from a Windows OCR pass over the renders, with obvious
character errors corrected by the curator against the page images for the T.1, T.4, T.13 and T.14 passages
and the opto-theory paragraph. This excerpt is mostly prose that the curator has not re-read word for word
against every page, so it is marked `mixed` and not `reviewed`.

## Front-matter warning (PDF page 3)

"Keep hands away from mini-playfield. Movement of the mini-playfield can cause SERIOUS PHYSICAL INJURY. Keep
fingers and loose clothing away from ALL moving parts. As a safety feature, during normal game play (and
attract mode) the mini-playfield will not move unless the coin door is closed and the playfield glass is on.
During the mini-playfield test, both flipper buttons must be pressed before it will move." (The page repeats
the warning in German and French.)

## Menu system and Test Menu (PDF pages 28 and 34)

The Test Menu lists T.1 Switch Edges, T.2 Switch Levels, T.3 Single Switch, T.4 Solenoid Test, T.5 Flasher
Test, T.6 General Illumination, T.7 Sound & Music Test, T.8 Single Lamps, T.9 All Lamps, T.10 Lamp & Flasher
Tests, T.11 Display Test, T.12 Flipper Test, T.13 Trap Door Test and T.14 Mini-playfield Test. "During any
test, press the Start button (Help) to obtain the wire color, driver number, connector number, and fuse
location."

"The switch matrix, on the left side of the display, shows the state of all switches. A dot indicates the
switch is open, and a square indicates the switch is closed. The numbers assigned to each switch indicate
where the switch is located in the matrix. The number on the left indicates the column, and the number on the
right indicates the row. Example: Switch 23 is 2nd column, 3rd row."

"T.1 Switch Edges. Press each switch one at a time. The name and number of the switch is shown in the
display." "T.2 Switch Levels. This test automatically cycles through all switches that are detected closed."

## T.4 Solenoid Test and T.5 Flasher Test (PDF page 35, printed 1-16)

"The Solenoid Test has three modes: Repeat, Stop, and Run ... After entering this test, Solenoid 1 shows in the
display, and the corresponding solenoid activates. Press the Up or Down button to cycle through the solenoids,
one at a time." "T.5 Flasher Test. This tests the flashlamp part of the solenoid circuit exclusively. ... After
entering this test, the name and number of the first flashlamp circuit will show in the display and the
corresponding bulb(s) flash."

## T.6, T.8, T.9, T.10, T.12 (PDF pages 36-37)

T.6 General Illumination tests all general-illumination circuits and then each circuit by name and number.
"The number assigned to each lamp indicates the lamp's position in the matrix. The number on the left
indicates the column. The number on the right indicates the row. Example: Lamp 23 means 2nd column, 3rd row."
T.12 Flipper Coil Test pulses one flipper coil at a time.

## T.13 Trap Door Test (PDF page 38, printed 1-19)

"The Dot Matrix display (left side) shows a side view of the trap door mechanism. ... when the 'TRAP DOWN'
switch is closed, the door will be down. Coils: when the coil is fully activated (PULL-IN), it is 100% filled.
When the coil duty is cycled (HOLD-IN), it is filled. ... NOTE: This test will automatically enter a
'COOLING' state when the coil starts to get warm." The test has the modes RUNNING (automatically cycles the
sub-tests), REPEAT and STOPPED, selected with the Enter button.

## T.14 Mini-playfield Test (PDF page 39, printed 1-20)

"The Dot Matrix display (left side) shows a front view of the Mini-playfield. The top line (right side)
identifies the eleven sub-tests. ... When a switch is considered 'active', its area is filled in. A switch is
active when it is 'closed'. An opto is the opposite and is active when the beam is broken, making the switch
'open'. If all opto switches are abnormally 'active' or 'open' look for a loss of power, switch column, and
opto common wires (for the emitters and receivers)." "During the test, the operator must press and hold both
flipper buttons to allow the mini-playfield to move." The status line shows the current "moving", "kicking",
"flashing" or "error" state. The sub-tests are selected with the + and - buttons and the same three modes apply.

## Error messages (PDF page 65)

"Trap Door Down Error / Trap Door Up Error: The Trap Door did not get to its intended position. Go to 'T. 12
Trap Door Test' [sic] and check the 'TRAP DOOR DOWN' switch, the Trap Door linkage, and the coils for proper
operation." "Mini-playfield Error - C.W. Direction Reversed / C.C.W. Direction Reversed / No C.W. Movement / No
C.C.W. Movement: The Mini-playfield has a problem positioning itself. The abbreviations C.W. is clockwise
direction, C.C.W. is counter clockwise as viewed from the front of the game. If both reversed errors occur,
the DC motor is wired backwards. If only 1 reversed message occurs, the motor direction driver is stuck
on/off (loose wire?). No movement in both directions can be a fuse or loose wire from the motor." "Examine Left
Jet Bumper Switch / Examine Right Jet Bumper Switch / Examine Bottom Jet Bumper Switch: The game detected an
unusually low number of hits on the indicated jet bumper."

## Opto theory (PDF page 66, printed 1-47)

"The opto receiver (detector) should be approximately 0.1-0.7 volts when the opto beam is unblocked and
approximately 11-13 volts when the opto beam is blocked. The opto transmitter (emitter or L.E.D.) should
always be approximately 1.4 volts. Note, the transmitter (L.E.D.) is larger than the receiver (it protrudes
further from its case)."

## Playfield adjustments and mini-playfield service (PDF pages 70-72, printed 1-51 to 1-53)

"PLAYFIELD GLASS SWITCH: The playfield glass switch should be adjusted so that when the glass is inserted, the
switch closes when the front of the glass is in line with the front of the cabinet. Use Test Menu, T.1 Switch
Edges, switch #82, to check adjustment." "MINI-PLAYFIELD BALL EJECT: To adjust ejects from kicking the ball
down the center of the playfield to the outhole ... The ball, when ejected, should hit the lower playfield
flippers." "BALL ROLLOUT FROM HANG ON LANE ..." "RIGHT WIRE RAMP BALL DROP: To prevent the ball from dropping
into the drain lane from the Police Box wire ramp ..." "TO REMOVE THE MINI-PLAYFIELD ASSEMBLY (Bottom of
Playfield): Mini-playfield should be in the down position ... Remove (2) nuts, and lift off Door Release
Bracket. When reassembling, position mini-playfield in the middle position (use diagnostics). Then, mount
Release bracket against flip-up target reset lever, so that the targets drop." "TO REMOVE MOTOR BRACKET
ASSEMBLY: Remove E-ring and Roller from Cam. Remove lower Opto (LED, white) Assembly, via the access holes ..."
The page drawings label a Reset Lever, a Door Release Bracket and Access Holes on the mini-playfield.
