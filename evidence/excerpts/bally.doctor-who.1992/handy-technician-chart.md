# Doctor Who — Handy Technician's Chart

Transcribed from `Bally_1992_Doctor_Who_Handy_Technician_s_Chart_includes_Fuse_List.pdf`, its single page
(titled `DOCTOR WHO (16-20006)`), a born-digital one-page chart that prints the fuse list, the
solenoid/flasher table, the lamp matrix and the switch matrix side by side. Read from a 220 dpi render. It
is a separately compiled printing of the same machine data as the operations manual: its numbers largely
agree with the manual's tables, but it is not independent of the manual's data and carries its own
printing differences, recorded below. Its switch matrix is the only printing in the retained set that shades
opto cells (legend, bottom edge of the matrix: a yellow box followed by `= OPTO, TYPICALLY CLOSED`).

No cell is printed in the Cabinet columns of this table for any solenoid. Differences against the manual's
Solenoid/Flasher Table (`solenoid-flasher-table.md`): this chart prints 02, 04, 05 as `Low Power` (the manual
prints them `High Power`), 03 as `High Power` (same as the manual), 06, 08, 14 and 23-24 as `Flasher`, and
25-28 as `Gen. Purpose`; it prints sol 07's `AE-23-800` in the backbox column. The manual prints the
function `Opto Popper` for 03; this chart prints `BALL POPPER`.

connector list (`power-driver-board-connectors.md`) prints those same pins as `White-Yellow/Green/Violet,

as `Playfield A/Insert A`, `Playfield B/Insert B` and `Playfield C/Insert C` with both a J120 and a J121
return connection.

## Lamp matrix

Same 8 x 8 grid, wires and transistors as the manual's lamp matrix, with column 8 printed `J137-9`. Cell text
(upper case as printed): row 1 `(E)-S-C-A-P-E`, `RIGHT RETURN LANE`, `5 X 3 TOP LEFT 1`, `5 X 3 MIDDLE LEFT 1`,
`5 X 3 BOTTOM LEFT 1`, `(R)-E-P-A-I-R`, `DOCTOR 4 (2 LAMPS)`, `(W)-H-O`; the other seven rows follow the
manual's cell text with these wording differences: 17 `LEFT OUTLANE`, 18 `LEFT RETURN LANE`, 21 `RIGHT RETURN
LANE`, 22 `RIGHT OUTLANE`; 23 `DOCTOR 7 (2 LAMPS)`, 36 `DOCTOR 2 (2 LAMPS)`, 48 `DOCTOR 1 (2 LAMPS)`, 67 `DOCTOR
5 (2 LAMPS)`, 72 `DOCTOR 6 (2 LAMPS)`, 82 `DOCTOR 3 (2 LAMPS)`; 56 `MINI PLAYFIELD LEFT LOCK`, 57 `MINI
PLAYFIELD RIGHT LOCK`, 58 `MINI PLAYFIELD TARGET`; 86 `BALL TRANSMAT & ADVANCE BONUS X`; 87 `BALL LAUNCH`; 88
`START BUTTON`.

## Switch matrix

Dedicated D1-D8 and the columns and rows (J206 / J208 pins, U20 / U18 / U19 ICs) as the manual's switch
matrix. Shaded cells (yellow, legend `= OPTO, TYPICALLY CLOSED`): 31 `POPPER OPTO`, 32 `MINI-PLY HOME OPTO`, 23
`TICKET OPTO`, 33 `ENTER TOP RAMP OPTO`, 71 `MINI-PLY 5 BANK RIGHT 1 OPTO`, 72 `MINI-PLY 5 BANK RIGHT 2
OPTO`, 73 `MINI-PLY 5 BANK MIDDLE OPTO`, 74 `MINI-PLY 5 BANK LEFT 2 OPTO`, 75 `MINI-PLY 5 BANK LEFT 1 OPTO`,
76 `MINI-PLY LEFT EJECT OPTO`, 77 `MINI-PLY RIGHT EJECT OPTO`, and the flipper cells F2 `Lower Right Flipper
Opto`, F4 `Lower Left Flipper Opto`, F6 `Upper Right Flipper Opto` and F8 `Upper Left Flipper Opto`. Not shaded:
every other cell, including 78 `MINI-PLY LITES LOCK`, 82 `PLAYFIELD GLAS`, 38 `MINI-PLY DOOR, MIDDLE`, 57 `TRAP
DOOR DOWN`, 58 `TRANSMAT AWARD`, and the flipper end-of-stroke cells F1, F3, F5 and F7. Unshaded cell text
that differs from the manual's: 11, 12, 81, 83-87 `NOT USED`; 13 `START BUTTON`; 17 `SHOOTER LANE`; 34 `LAUNCH
BALL`; 64 `LEFT OUTLANE`, 65 `LEFT RETURN LANE`, 66 `RIGHT RETURN LANE`, 67 `RIGHT OUTLANE`; 38/68/88 `MINI-PLY
DOOR, MIDDLE/LEFT/RIGHT`.
