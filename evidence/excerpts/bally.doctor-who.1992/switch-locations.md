Notes on the transcription:

- Items 68 and 71-75 are joined by a printed brace in the left margin and another around their
  descriptions. The row for 68 carries its own switch number (`5647-12693-13`) and assembly (`A-12238`);
  the single `A-15431 (Trans) & A-15432 (LED)` assembly cell is printed once, across the lines of items 72
  and 73, and applies to the five items 71-75; the printed `---` switch-number cell is likewise printed
  once, level with item 73. Both are repeated above, once per item, to give each its own row.
- Item 31's, 32's and 33's switch-number cell is two lines (`A-14231 (LED)` over `A-14232 (Trans)`); the
  same two-line cell is printed for 76 and 77 in the Assembly column, in the order `A-14231 (LED)` then
  `A-14232 (Trans)`.
- The flipper rows F5 and F6 (upper right) are not printed; the page has F1-F4 and F7-F8 only. Matrix
  positions 11, 12, 81 and 83-87 are printed Not Used; item 23 prints `Not Used` as its assembly with
  the description `*Ticket Opto`. Items 11 and 12 are printed as the single row `11-12`.
