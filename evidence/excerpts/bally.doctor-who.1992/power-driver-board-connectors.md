# Doctor Who — Power Driver Board connector list

Transcribed from `Bally_1992_Doctor_Who_Manual.pdf`, PDF pages 143-145, printed pages `DOCTOR WHO 3-20`,
`3-21` and `3-22`, the `POWER DRIVER BOARD A-12697-1` connector list (`POWER DRIVER BOARD`, `POWER DRIVER
BOARD CONTINUED...`, `POWER DRIVER BOARD CONTINUED...`). Read from the rendered pages (300 dpi image-only
scans), not from the OCR text. The page legend reads `J1-J6 Coin Door Interface Board`, `J1xx Power
Driver Board`, `J2xx CPU Board`, `J5xx Audio Board`, `J6xx Dot Matrix Controller Board`, `J9xx Fliptronic II
Board`. Each connector pin is `J<connector>-<pin>` followed by the wire colour and its destination.

## What this list shows about locations

The list prints a playfield-lamp set (J133 rows 1-8, J137 columns 1-8), a speaker-panel set (J135 rows 1, 2,
3, 6, 7, 8 and J138 columns 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8) and a cabinet-lamp set (J134-9 row 8 and J136-3 column 8) of
matrix connectors. The flasher drives 17-24 appear twice, once toward the playfield (J126) and once toward the
insert board (J125); Sol 6 and Sol 8 appear toward the insert flasher connector J131 (Sol 6 also at J130-7
toward the playfield), and Sol 14 only at J128-2 "to backbox". G.I. return pins are split between playfield
(J120), insert (J121) and coin door (J119).
