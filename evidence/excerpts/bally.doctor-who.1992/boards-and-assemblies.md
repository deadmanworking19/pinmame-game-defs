# Doctor Who — backbox, board and mechanism assembly pages

Transcribed from `Bally_1992_Doctor_Who_Manual.pdf`, PDF pages 77, 91-109, 113, 115-117 (printed pages
`DOCTOR WHO 2-4`, `2-19` to `2-36`, `2-40`, `2-42`, `2-43`): the Backbox Assembly page, the flipper, outhole,
shooter-lane, slingshot, jet-bumper, knocker, trough-switch, Tardis, mini-playfield, popper, motor, bracket,
cover, 3-door, Dalek, trap-door, cap-ball-popper and target assembly pages, the Back Panel Assembly cable list
and the playfield parts lists. Read from the rendered pages (300 dpi image-only scans); part-number cells on
the mechanism pages were read from the OCR text and spot-checked against the renders, so this excerpt is
marked `mixed` and not `reviewed`. Only what the definition relies on is transcribed.
