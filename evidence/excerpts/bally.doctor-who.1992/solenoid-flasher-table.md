# Doctor Who — Solenoid/Flasher Table

Transcribed from `Bally_1992_Doctor_Who_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 128, printed page `DOCTOR WHO 3-5`, the
`SOLENOID/FLASHER TABLE`, with its general-illumination and flipper-circuit rows and the footnote `J1XX =
Power Driver Board, J9XX = Fliptronic II Board`. Read from the rendered page (300 dpi image-only scan), not
from the OCR text. The page prints no column headings for the two connection cells after the transistor;
the Handy Technician's Chart (`handy-technician-chart.md`) prints the same data under the headings
Playfield / Backbox / Cabinet.

## Handy Technician's Chart comparison (different printing, same data)

The Handy chart (`handy-technician-chart.md`) prints the flipper circuits as numbered rows 29-36 with the
voltage connector `J907-1 (RED-GRN)`, `J907-4 (RED-BLU)`, `J907-6 (RED-VIO)` and `J907-8 (RED-GRY)`, the
connections `J902-13/-11`, `J902-9/-7`, `J902-6/-4` and `J902-3/-1`, and the wires `YEL-GRN`/`ORG-GRN`,
`YEL-BLU`/`ORG-BLU`, `YEL-VIO`/`ORG-VIO` and `YEL-GRY`/`ORG-GRY`; 33/34 are printed `NOT USED`. Those
wire colours and J902 pins differ from the row text on this page (for example `Gry-Yel` and `J902-11, 12`
for the lower right flipper here). Both readings are kept.
