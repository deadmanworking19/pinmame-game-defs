# Doctor Who — Lamp Locations

Transcribed from `Bally_1992_Doctor_Who_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 118, printed page `DOCTOR WHO 2-45`, the
`LAMP LOCATIONS` parts list. The PDF is an image-only 300 dpi scan with no text layer; every cell was read
from the rendered page (a Windows OCR pass was used only to find the page). The right half of the page is
a playfield drawing whose numbered balloons carry the same item numbers (the committed crop `lamp-locations.webp`
shows the whole page: table and drawing).

Header columns: Item No. | Bulb No. | Lamp Assy. No. | Description. A second line under an item number
(printed with a blank Item cell) is a second bulb of the same item and is transcribed here as the same
item's second row, labelled "(second row)" below. Every one of the 64 matrix positions 11-18, 21-28, 31-38,
41-48, 51-58, 61-68, 71-78 and 81-88 carries a description; no address on this page is marked "Not Used".

Notes on the transcription:

- Item 86 prints its description across two lines ("Ball Transmat &" / "Advance Bonus X") with a
  bulb pair on the same two lines: the first line carries 24-6549 / A-11754 and the second 24-8768 /
  A-11271. The two bulbs are therefore read as two bulbs of one lamp address, not as two lamps.
- Item 88 prints no bulb number (a literal `---`); its assembly cell reads `20-9663-1`, the same part
  number the Switch Locations list prints for the Start Button (item 13). Item 87's bulb cell reads
  `20-9663-B-2`.
- The printed "Doctor 7 / 2 / 1 / 5 / 4 / 6 / 3" rows (23, 36, 48, 67, 71, 72, 82) each say "(1 playfield)"
  plus a second bulb on a "speaker panel" (23, 36, 48, 71, 72, 82) or a "back panel" (67).
- The page prints no per-lamp bulb-count marker for any other item.

## What the drawing shows (read from the rendered page)

- The drawing prints the item number of every lamp inside a black balloon at the lamp's place on the
  playfield outline; the balloon shape follows the insert (circles, rectangles for the 5X3 matrix, arrows and
  diamonds for the REPAIR arrows and the multiplier lamps).
- A row of seven rectangular balloons `48 36 82 71 67 72 23` is drawn above the playfield outline, outside
  it. A separate teardrop balloon `67` is drawn at the playfield's top right edge. The Doctor lamps 23, 36, 48,
  71, 72 and 82 also have a balloon on the playfield itself (23 above the 5X3 matrix, 36 at lower left, 48 at
  lower right, 71 and 72 among the multiplier diamonds, 82 on the diagonal 81-85 lamp array).
- Balloons 87 and 88 are drawn below the playfield outline, outside it. Balloon 86 appears twice, once as
  an arrow at the upper left and once as a circle at the left side.
- Balloons 56, 57 and 58 are drawn inside the mini-playfield outline at the top of the playfield.
