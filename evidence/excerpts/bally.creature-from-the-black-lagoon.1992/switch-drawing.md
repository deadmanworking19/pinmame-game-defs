# Creature from the Black Lagoon — Switch Locations drawing

The playfield drawing on `Creature_From_The_Black_Lagoon_OPS.pdf` PDF page 101, printed page 2-41, beside the Switch
Locations parts list (`switch-locations.md`). The crop `switch-drawing.webp` is the drawing alone. Every balloon on it
was transcribed with its pixel by a reader working only from the drawing, on an earlier crop with the same origin and
resolution that cut the frame's right edge; the curator widened the crop to the right, read the frame's top-right corner
on it, and verified every read on an overlay. The reads and corrections are in
`tools/seeds/bally/creature-from-the-black-lagoon-1992-callouts.json` (page `2-41`).

What the drawing shows, as printed:

- Balloons 15-18, 25-28, 33-38, 41-48, 51-58 and 61-66, most with pointer wedges ending on the switch they name.
- Balloon 16's pointer ends on the centre hole and balloon 17's on the left hole. The game's own rules put the K-I-S-S
  (Taillight Park) award on the left hole and the Snack Bar on the centre hole, and the ROM awards K-I-S-S for switch
  16 and the Snack Bar for switch 17 (runtime evidence `creature-cftbl_l4-subway-awards.json`): the drawing transposes
  the two callouts.
- Balloons 55-58 sit in a row without pointers along the trough, each one ball position up the trough from the switch
  it names, 55 on the outhole end.
- Balloon 38 sits without a pointer on the lift ramp at the head of the left ramp.
- Balloon 13 below the outline at the lower left, without a pointer: the Start button in the cabinet. The tilt, slam
  tilt, coin door and Fliptronic switches carry no balloon.
