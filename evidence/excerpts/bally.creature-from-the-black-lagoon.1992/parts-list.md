## Reading notes

- The swirl ramp carries 24 #86 chase bulbs on three flexible eight-lamp boards, A-16089 ("lt-1"), A-16088 ("lt-2")
  and A-16087 ("lt-3"), and the bowl eight more on A-15672: 32 chase bulbs in all.
- A-16087 carries both a seven-pin cable header and a seven-pin strip socket, and A-16088 a seven-pin strip header:
  A-16088 plugs into A-16087, and the pair takes one cable. A-16089 has its own cable header.
- The ramp's two cables use the two Chase Light II groups' colours: H-16213 "front chase lite cable" the J3 group
  (White-Green common with Orange-Green, -Blue, -Violet, -Gray; G.I. string 04, public G.I. 3, lamps 95-98) and
  H-15871 the J4 group (White-Brown common with Orange-Brown, -Red, -Black, -Yellow; G.I. string 01, public G.I. 0,
  lamps 91-94). Neither entry says which board it plugs into.
- The bowl cable H-15870 carries the J3 group's five colours (and green/white for the bowl switch 65 on its two-pin
  plug), so the bowl's eight bulbs are on public G.I. 3 and lamps 95-98.
- The manual's cable list adds H-16214 "Center Chase Light Cable", which the ramp and bowl assemblies do not list.
