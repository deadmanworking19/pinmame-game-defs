# Creature from the Black Lagoon — Lamp Locations drawing

The playfield drawing on `Creature_From_The_Black_Lagoon_OPS.pdf` PDF page 100, printed page 2-40, beside the Lamp
Locations parts list (`lamp-locations.md`). The crop `lamp-drawing.webp` is the drawing alone. Every balloon on it was
transcribed with its pixel on this crop by a reader working only from the drawing, and the curator verified each read
on an overlay; the reads, the control points and the curator's corrections are in
`tools/seeds/bally/creature-from-the-black-lagoon-1992-callouts.json` (page `2-40`).

What the drawing shows, as printed:

- Black balloons 11-17, 21-24, 27, 28, 31-38, 41-48, 51-58, 61-68, 81-87 with pointer wedges ending on the inserts and
  sockets they name. Balloons 25 and 26 are not printed. The swirl ramp's and the bowl's chase bulbs carry no balloon.
- Balloon 28 twice (left outlane and right outlane), 37 with two pointers (the two right-lane inserts) and 44 with two
  pointers (the two left-lane inserts).
- Balloons 55-58 point at the four round hatched sockets of the board in the centre of the swirl; 63-65 at the three
  sockets of the board at the upper left; 66-68 at the three sockets of the board right of centre.
- Balloon 18 above the playfield outline at the top right, its pointer ending in a shaded box drawn above the frame:
  the "Admit One" lamp sits on the back panel, not on the playfield.
- Balloon 88 below the outline at the lower left, with no pointer: the Start button lamp in the cabinet.
- No balloons for 71-78, which the parts list marks `*Located on backbox insert`.
