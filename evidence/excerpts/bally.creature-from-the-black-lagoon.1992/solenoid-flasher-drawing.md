# Creature from the Black Lagoon — Solenoid/Flasher Locations drawing

The playfield drawing on `Creature_From_The_Black_Lagoon_OPS.pdf` PDF page 103, printed page 2-43, beside the
Solenoid/Flasher Locations parts list (`solenoid-flasher-locations.md`). The crop `solenoid-flasher-drawing.webp` is
the drawing alone, with its printed legend "Square indicates coil or P.C.B." and "Circle indicates flasher". Every
symbol on it was transcribed with its pixel by a reader working only from the drawing, and the curator verified each
read on an overlay; the reads are in `tools/seeds/bally/creature-from-the-black-lagoon-1992-callouts.json` (page
`2-43`).

What the drawing shows, as printed:

- Circles 02, 08, 09 (twice: the two top corners), 10, 16, 17, 18, 19, 22 and 25 (twice: the left and right lane
  walls) with pointers ending on the flasher domes they name. Circle 22 is printed over the rectangular outline of a part,
  so at low resolution it can look like a square; a 600 dpi render of the page shows a circle balloon.
- Squares 01, 03, 05, 06, 13, 14 and 15 on the poppers, slingshots and jet bumpers.
- Square 04 at the trough's exit end beside the shooter lane, its pointer ending on the trough; square 12 below the
  trough's outhole end.
- Squares 20 and 24 one above the other right of the left ramp, where the Chase Light II board sits under the
  playfield; square 21 on the hologram assembly in the middle of the playfield; squares 23 and 26 one above the other on
  the lift ramp at the head of the left ramp; square 27 inside the hologram window's oval and square 28 near the top
  centre, although the parts list marks both "Located in cabinet bottom".
- No symbol for 07 (`*Knocker`, marked Not shown), 11 (the insert-only Creature flasher) or the flipper coils (marked
  Not shown).
