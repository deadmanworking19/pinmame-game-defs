# Gold buyin output: firmware state and factory omission

Source: Pinside topic “Addams Family Gold - Extra Ball Lamp,” complete 26-post capture `parent-pinside-buyin-lamp-capture.json`, photographs and URL/hash manifest `parent-buyin-images.json` in the retained task review folder. https://pinside.com/pinball/forum/topic/addams-family-gold-extra-ball-lamp . Owner photographs 3 and 4 were visually inspected by the curator, including the DMD diagnostic text.

Gold factory manual 2-42 and 3-2 print lamp 41 as Not Used. The thread separates the factory omission from a later owner modification:

- LTG, post 2: the buyin lamp was not wired at the factory; diagnostic lamp test nevertheless identifies Buy In. In post 19 he explains that he uncrated his machine new; post 23 identifies Gold 0972.
- Owner todds411, post 3, identifies lamp 41 and the red-brown column / yellow-black row. His diagnostic photographs explicitly show BUY IN, LAMP41, RED-BRN and YEL-BLK.
- Post 24 reports successful added wiring and diode. That retrofit is not evidence of a factory-fitted lamp.

The canonical address is therefore a physical lamp-matrix output (`kind: lamp`, `availability: unused`) with no factory wired load. Its normal lamp-matrix binding remains 41. `Unused` describes factory fitment, not an absence of meaningful ROM state: the diagnostic photographs identify that state as Buy In. The optional retrofit is described in prose rather than counted as an original emitter. The Gold VPX script does not bind a factory buyin lamp. The manual and first-person original-owner account settle factory omission while the photographs explain the firmware state.
