# Gold web research and authority boundaries

Retained complete browser captures/downloads reside in the task's external review folder; their individual byte digests and URLs are pinned by the Gold source records. These observations are secondary context, not replacement physical/socket evidence.

Pinside Game Archive/details, Gold Special Collectors Edition: Bally, October 1st 1994, 1000 units, manual plunger, buyin, four flippers, five bumpers, four magnets. Its generic Fliptronics 2 classification does not identify the actual controller circuitry. The Gold manual 3-10/3-19 and owner board photographs control that question. Owner forum topic 360770: LTG post 8 corrects the buyin location to front-right below the shooter rod; aeneas post 11 links his Flippers.be comparison. The photo article and that forum link share authorship, not independent corroboration.

Flippers.be comparison, by Aeneas (with photos credited to Johan DM, Michel O, Erik): board photograph shows Fliptronics I without on-board bridge; cabinet photograph shows optical button board, two coin-door switches and buyin loom; power-driver photo shows later revision without former flipper relay. Factory manual independently establishes optical button A-17316 and interlock A-17077. Cosmetic gold versions of 1992 TAF are not proof of 1994 Gold identity.

Action Pinball ROM-conversion technical article: conversion replaces U6, changes U18 and adds U15 sound ROM. It states that the original machine can run Gold rules after those ROM changes, with buyin unavailable unless its button is fitted. This supports shared rules compatibility, not equivalence of the two cabinets or socket geometry.

Keefer, Guide V1.1.1, 10-Jun-1995, complete original guide mirrored by GameFAQs faqs/1447. Original pinball.org endpoint failed; retained mirror contains both preformatted blocks and original distribution/contributor notices. Source assertions, paraphrased: Cousin It's hidden items occur in mansion rooms; 3M also awards another room; 6M has extra dialogue/animation; Super Seance has 10/15/20M ramp awards (random likelihood explicitly uncertain); starting Jackpot varies; Dirty Pool can award a Super Jackpot while leaving it lit; locked balls normally purge at game end (adjustable); buyin preserves score/progress and allows 0–99 bought balls. These are the author's gameplay observations, not newly measured ROM behavior. Contributors: Jon Biavati, Mark Phaedrus, Mark Valentine, David Stewart. No random probability or undocumented timing is promoted as a physical constant.

Williams' official archived Ask Uncle Willy #16, mirrored by Planetary Pinball at `https://www.planetarypinball.com/mm5/Williams/archives/willy16.htm`, distinguishes the gold-plated cabinet made to commemorate the production record from the later Special Collectors Edition. The complete historical page is retained with its digest. It supplies an identity distinction, not a device map or production-date substitution.
