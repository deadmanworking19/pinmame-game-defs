The drawing places the magnet coil/core in the moving hand and the two limit optos beside its motor-driven interrupter. It is not a magnet fixed below the saucer. Service test1-21 separately displays the opto limits and cycles hand pickup/drop, returning the ball to the playfield. The Bookcase test toggles the motor and displays its mechanical limits. Physical switches exist even when the emulator synthesizes their public states.
