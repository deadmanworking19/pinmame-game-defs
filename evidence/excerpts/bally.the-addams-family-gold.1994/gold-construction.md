# Gold construction and edition facts

Gold manual PDF3, New Game Features, visually checked against page-003.png. Factual digest of the complete enumerated change list:

1. Gold-coloured cabinet side armour, front hand protector, legs, Thing box, bookcase, centre ramp wire form, bottom arch artwork and cabinet artwork; numbered engraved front plaque and certificate.
2. More Gomez/Morticia speech from original studio tapes; expanded sound/music memory.
3. Buy-In button, allowing an additional ball and continued progress toward Tour The Mansion.
4. Cousin It's Hideout, with hidden objects found through mansion rooms; Wednesday/Pugsley's secret passage from the3-million room to another randomly awarded room; added DMD sequences/game memory.

PDF4, Important Notice, visually checked against page-004.png. A-17077 is a bracket holding the existing memory-protect switch below and an added interlock above. Opening the coin door opens the interlock, disconnecting the transformer+50V/+20V secondary. The actuator supplied for service holds that upper switch closed. This is not another switch-matrix address. Parts labelled on the drawing: service actuator01-11791, interlock5643-09112-00, memory protect5643-09268-00, bracket01-11790. Interlock harness drawing: WHT-RED(92B/92C), BLK-YEL(04C/04B), two separately drawn normally-open contact sections.

PDF108/110,2-41/2-43: Gold playfield topology remains four flippers, five jets, three under-playfield Power magnets, a separate Thing-hand electromagnet, bookcase/vault, three-ball lock and trough, manual shooter. Changes are recorded by Gold's own parts tables; shared geometry is not inferred from the ROM clone tree.
