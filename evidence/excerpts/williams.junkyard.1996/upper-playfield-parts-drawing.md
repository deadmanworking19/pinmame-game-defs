# Upper Playfield Parts drawing, top of playfield (printed page 2-31)

Source: Williams Junk Yard (1996) Operations Manual, PDF page 107, the drawing that goes with the
Upper Playfield Parts list on PDF page 106 (`upper-playfield-parts.md`). The rendered crop beside
this file (`upper-playfield-parts-drawing.webp`) covers the top of the playfield only.

The drawing is a plan view of the upper playfield with numbered callouts, each joined by a leader
line to a drawn part. The numbers are item numbers of the Upper Playfield Parts list. At the top
centre a channel runs up the playfield with a large arrow in it and a circle drawn near its lower
end. A semicircular arc of targets surrounds the top of that channel.

Leader endpoints in this crop, as drawn:

| Callout | Parts-list item | Where the leader ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 24 (upper-left margin) | A-18530-4 Red Standup Target | At a target on the upper left of the arc |
| 24 (top, left of 16) | A-18530-4 Red Standup Target | Runs down the left edge of the centre channel and ends at the top of the arc |
| 23 | A-21215 Wrecking Ball Mech. Assembly | At the top of the centre channel, near the top rail |
| 21 | A-21247 Wrecking Ball Target Assembly | On the right-hand part of the arc |
| 14, 15, 16 | A-21351-1/-2/-3 Decal & Standup Target | Targets on the right-hand part of the arc |
| 27, 28, 29 | A-21351-4/-5/-6 Decal & Standup Target | Targets on the left-hand part of the arc |

Item 24 is called out twice, matching the two switches the Switch Locations page prints with part
`A-18530-4`. The other callouts in the crop are not used by the definition.

Reading the individual targets is limited by the scan; which side of the channel each red standup
is on is better read from the Switch Locations drawing (`switch-locations-crane-area.md`).
