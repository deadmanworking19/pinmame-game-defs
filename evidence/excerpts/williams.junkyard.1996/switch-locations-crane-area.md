# Switch Locations drawing, crane area (printed page 2-35)

Source: Williams Junk Yard (1996) Operations Manual, PDF page 111, printed page **2-35**. The table
on the same page is transcribed in `switch-locations.md`; this excerpt covers only the top of the
playfield drawing, retained as a crop (`switch-locations-crane-area.webp`) because the evidence is
where leader lines end.

The callout numbers are switch numbers. At the top centre a channel with a large arrow runs up the
playfield, with a circle drawn in it below the arrow and a `⊻`-like mark at its top. A semicircular
arc of targets surrounds the top of the channel. A large round assembly sits at the top left.

Leader endpoints in this crop, as drawn:

| Callout | Switch (from the table) | Where the leader ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 44 | Past Crane | At a small switch body on the lane at the top left, between the large round assembly and the arc |
| 28 | Crane Down | On the bar along the top rail, above the centre channel |
| 38 | Top Right Crane | At the target at the top of the arc, right of the channel's arrow |
| 15 | Top Left Crane | At the target at the top of the arc, left of the channel's arrow (the leader is dashed) |
| 63, 62, 61 | Upper Right 3-Bank | Targets on the right-hand part of the arc |
| 65, 65, 64 | Upper Left 3-Bank | Targets on the left-hand part of the arc; two callouts read `65` at this resolution, as `switch-locations.md` notes |

The other callouts in the crop are not used by the definition. The five car targets (46-48, 53, 54), which the table footnotes `***ABOVE CRANE`, have no callout on
this drawing.
