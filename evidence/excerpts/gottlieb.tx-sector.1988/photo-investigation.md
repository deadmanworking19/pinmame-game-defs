They are visible as white bulb bases emerging from separate metal holders; wires partly obscure the right one. Their visible white tip reads are (930,310) and (1022,310), and are recorded separately because the bulbs project above the socket/board plane. `915063-detail-grid.jpg` reproduces these approximate manual reads. The insert identification follows the manual and its distinctive adjacency to the energy ladder; the original shows the two separate holders. This establishes the second physical assembly in photo space. It does not establish which side corresponds to the existing script's single `L29` render center (0.4585,0.489), or the normalized locations of either holder. No second table binding is invented and the table light is not duplicated.

## Flashes, GI, trough

Manual wiring page-47.png printed 41 assigns each of the four multiplexed drives 1/2/5/6 to a hole-kicker branch and a separate pair of #67 flash bulbs: left dome, right dome, left ramp and right ramp, respectively. Photos 915027/028/035 and 915041–052 show ramp and dome context, but these views do not unambiguously expose and trace both bulb-holder axes for each pair to its corresponding S-relay branch. The row on the ramp is independently described by the manual as three #86 lamps per circuit 15–19; it must not be relabeled as these #67 flash pairs. A dome, visible aperture, or table flasher sprite cannot by itself identify two socket positions. Exact eight holder centers and their one-to-one circuit assignments remain unavailable from the checked views.

The underside 915062/063/064/068/069 shows many real bulb/holder assemblies. Some are recognizable as backs of controlled inserts, so their appearance alone does not establish GI membership. In stripped top view 915051 the metal-bottom openings near (505,895) and (1890,865) are concrete candidates for follow-up, but neither a lamp holder nor a trace to the T-relay GI supply is established in the image; clear threaded plastic supports are posts and cannot be counted as sockets. The controlled-GI supply on manual page 41 and script's `UseGI=0` do not locate or count those sockets. Therefore no GI socket coordinates or count are proposed. Follow the visible common supply wiring on the original underside photographs and verify each corresponding aperture before classifying it.

In 915064 the large release mechanism is at the apron end, and a slender mechanism occurs near (1430,450); the latter is only an approximate region read, not an identified switch contact. Neither it nor the nearby hardware is demonstrably the manual's trough contact #2 / switch 56: wiring and actuation are obscured, and the table drives the logical trough count without a physical contact object. No switch-56 point is assigned. A close, unobstructed view tracing the actual trough contact and wiring, or a reliably identified assembly diagram, is needed.

## Reproducible fit and its limit

The 915018 full-playfield top photo has seven independent, manually read controls in `fit-915018.json`: three bumper cap centers, two lower flipper pivots and the centers of the two lower inserts. They were read from the image grid before fitting, not manufactured from table coordinates. Their table counterparts come from the retained extracted objects and bounds. The control y coordinates span 0.725 normalized, greater than half the playfield. The convex hull in native pixels is [(844,1066), (925,282), (1070,240), (1230,1066)]; no target socket in a different photo has a valid in-hull classification against it. Topside rear points are at the image top, apron points at the bottom. Cap centers sit above the board, several are partly hidden by rails, and pivot/insert center reads are approximate: these same-part offsets and perspective parallax are uncorrected. The socket holder axes in 915063 also project above the board and do not directly read a planar intersection.

`fit_photo.py` uses least squares for an affine transform and an eight-parameter projective homography, and refits after removing each control. It records coefficients, each full-fit prediction, each residual and each leave-one-out prediction/error. Affine maximum residual is 0.0469 and maximum leave-one-out error is **0.0767**; homography maximum residual is 0.0519 and maximum leave-one-out error is **0.1493**, with Bumper2 and Bumper3 both exceeding 0.07 under homography LOO. These numerical discrepancies and the height/occlusion problems make this fit insufficient for precise new placements. It belongs only to 915018, whereas the target socket reads are in 915063; transferring its coefficients to a different view would be invalid. No independent, sufficiently spread set of unambiguously corresponding planar features around the target was established between 915063 and a wider underside view. The control hull therefore surrounds no independently calibrated target on that photo, and extrapolation/fit quality remain unresolved.
