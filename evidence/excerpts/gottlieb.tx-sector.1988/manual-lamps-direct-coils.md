## Uncertainty markers

- The ground-return connector block uses alphabetic `Z` and `U`, distinct from numeric driver pins. Its two faint lower glyphs remain `[S or 5; ambiguous]` and `[G or C; ambiguous]`; neither is normalized into structured wiring.
- The pin glyph by L3 on P/O A3J3 appears as `C` in the native image but is not certain; it is marked `C?` rather than normalized to a numeric pin.
- The A3J3 connector uses printed numeric and alphabetic pin marks; the alphabetic marks Y, X, V, W, K, T, D, F, P, M, R and N are preserved literally. The L43 pin glyph cannot be distinguished reliably between E and 2 and is explicitly ambiguous in its row.
- The A9P6/J6 pin positions are tightly packed beside the right-ramp lamp block. Pin labels 1–6 and 7–8 are recorded as printed, but no unprinted return/load relationship is supplied.
- The number of flipper/kicker/bumper symbols in this schematic is stated only as a count of drawn rows. No count is derived from an unlabelled or partially hidden mark.

## Board provision versus game-harness readings

Native PDF 48 / printed 42 clearly prints A3J3 pin **22**, wire **322**, for L21 MEGA LEVEL 2. The shared E-20915 board drawing on PDF 33-34 / printed 27-28 instead connects Q22/public 21 to A3J3-20 and Q15/public 14 to A3J3-22. The L21 table cell is retained literally; it suggests a fitted bulb on pin 22 but does not settle which public branch the actual game harness uses. The structured record for 14 describes only the Q15 board provision; its fitted load remains unknown. Lamp 21 has no structured harness pin because these readings are not independently reconciled.

For L43 EXTRA BALL, RIGHT SPOT TARGET, the native PDF 48 cell beside wire **744** is **E or 2; ambiguous**. Comparing other numeric and alphabetic pin marks does not justify silently choosing either. The board drawing positively connects Q44/public 43 to A3J3-2 and Q49/public 48 to A3J3-E. An E reading would suggest a fitted bulb on the latter branch; a 2 reading would agree with Q44, but neither is asserted as the resolved game-harness pin. Structured 48 wiring describes only the Q49 board provision and fitment remains unknown; lamp 43's harness pin remains unset. Public runtime bindings 21/43 identify logical outputs, not independently measured connector pins. These wiring-detail observations do not add blocking physical conflicts or settle physical polarity.
