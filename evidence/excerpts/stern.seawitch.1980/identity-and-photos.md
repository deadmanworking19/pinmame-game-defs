# Seawitch identity and reviewed physical photographs

Retained IPDB page 2089, acquired 2026-10-15T08:45:47.604Z: Seawitch, Stern Electronics, May 1980, model 123, M-200 MPU, four players. Its listed four flippers, three pop bumpers, two slingshots, four stand-ups, two four-target banks, one three-target bank, two star rollovers and one spinner match the factory parts/topology sheets. Firmware revision years do not change the physical release year.

The primary curator inspected retained IPDB images 19809 (upper playfield), 19810 (middle), 19811 (lower), 52422 (underside, courtesy Mike Halse) and 19828 (behind backglass). They corroborate the physical three-bank/four-flipper layout and separate insert-panel lighting. No measured coordinate, socket count or connector identity is inferred from these photographs. Image 52422 has apron at top and is mirrored relative to player view; no raw photo pixel was normalized into VPX space. Originals remain external and copyrighted to their photographers.
