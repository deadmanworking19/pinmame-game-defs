# Seawitch cabinet contacts and lighting

Source: schematics PDF page 7, CABINET and FRONT DOOR wiring. The drawing crop retains all named cabinet switches, flipper buttons, service controls, knocker, lockout and door-light branches. Generic unlabelled contacts in other columns do not establish game-specific fitment.

| Return | MPU pin | Wire | Name |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I0 | A4J3-9 | BLU | CHUTE #1 |
| I1 | A4J3-10 | BRN-W | CHUTE #2 |
| I2 | A4J3-11 | R-W | CHUTE #3 |
| I3 | [blank] | [blank] | [blank] |
| I4 | [blank] | [blank] | [blank] |
| I5 | A4J3-14 | BLU-W | CREDIT |
| I6 | A4J3-15 | BLU-O | TILT, two drawn contacts |
| I7 | A4J3-16 | Y | SLAM |

ST0 A is A4J3-2 (R-Y); ST1 B is A4J3-3 (R-G). No further cabinet strobe connector labels are printed.

TEST SWITCH: A4J3-1 (R) to A3J2-7 (Y-R).
S33 MEMORY CLEAR: A4J3-5 (W) to the same return.
LEFT FLIPPER: A3J2-2 (BLU).
RIGHT FLIPPER: A3J2-1 (R).
Flipper-button common: A2J2-9 (O).

COIN LOCKOUT: A2J2-2 (G) to A3J2-8 (Y-W).
KNOCKER: the same G bus to A3J2-5 (B-Y).
FRONT DOOR LITES: A2J2-1 (Y-B) to A2J2-5 (G-R).

Mains switch and service-outlet wiring are cabinet power distribution. Literal note: n/u = not used; all diodes are 1N-4004. The front-door-jack table is outside this crop and is not used to infer extra devices.
