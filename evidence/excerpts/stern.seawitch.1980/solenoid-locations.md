# Seawitch solenoid location drawing

Source: manual PDF page 21, SOLENOIDS SEAWITCH. Complete playfield callouts refer to SDU Q positions: 1 left thumper, 2 top thumper, 9 right thumper; 4 center reset, 8 left reset, 13 upper-right reset; 6 left sling, 7 right sling; 14 out-hole; 15 at all four flippers. Separate cabinet list: 3-KNOCKER; 19-LOCKOUT.

The four 15 callouts denote flippers enabled by one relay, not four numbered CPU coil outputs. The out-hole callout ends under the apron and cannot validate the table's shooter-lane ballrelease object as a physical out-hole coil position. No quantitative control fit has been applied.
