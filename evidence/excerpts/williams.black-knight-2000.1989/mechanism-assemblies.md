The page's schematic draws three channels, Opto 1, Opto 2 and Opto 3, each an LED (current-limited
through a 470 Ω resistor from +12 V to Gnd) facing a phototransistor that, with a 27 KΩ pull-up,
feeds the + input of one LM339 comparator section; U1's fourth section drives the column line.
J1 pin 4 is row 1, 5 is row 2, 7 is row 3, 1 is the column, 6 is Key, and 2 and 3 are not
labelled on the schematic; J2 carries +12 V (pin 1), Key (2), Circuitry Ground (3) and N.C. (4).

Transistor, TIP36C; 3 5010-09160-00 R1 Resistor, 220Ω, 1/4w, 5%; 4 5070-09054-00 D1 Diode,
1N4004, 1.0A; 5 5705-09172-00 Heatsink, 6072B; 6 5791-10862-06 J1 Connector, 6-pin Hdr Sq Pin .156;
7 5791-10862-05 J2 Connector, 5-pin Hdr Sq Pin .156; 8 4406-01128-00 Nut, 6-32 KEPS; 9
4006-01017-08 Mach. Screw, 6-32 x 1/2. The page's "High Current Driver - Magna Save™ Circuit"
schematic shows three harness wires, RED-WHT from 5J11-12, BRN-VIO from 1J12-8 and BLK from 5J11-7,
through a three-pin connector to the board's J1: RED-WHT lands on J1 pins 5 and 6 (tied together),
BRN-VIO on pin 1 and BLK on pins 3 and 4 (tied together). The Magna Save coil is drawn across J2
pins 1-2 (tied) and 3-5 (tied), with Q1 (TIP36C), R1 (220 Ω) and D1 (1N4004) between them.
