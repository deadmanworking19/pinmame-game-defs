# Black Knight 2000 — Switch-Matrix Table (printed page 67)

Source: `Williams_1989_Black_Knight_2000_Operations_Manual.pdf` (102 pages), PDF page 71 (printed
"Black Knight 67"; the switch-matrix wiring drawing is printed above the table on the same page).
Read from a 345 dpi render of the native ~345 ppi bilevel scan; the PDF has no text layer. Every cell
is transcribed; a cell that prints only its number is written "(blank)". A circled digit inside a
cell is the table's "Item Identifier" and is written "(item n)".

Two printed details worth keeping. The U-Turn cells carry item identifiers 1, 2, 3 and 4 on
addresses 49, 50, 51 and 52 (the fourth circle sits in the row-4 cell at 52); the R 3-Bank Dr
Target cells carry identifiers 1-3 and the Left UPF/Right UPF Loop End cells carry 1 and 2. The
table prints no shaded or opto-marked cell, and has no opto legend.

Legend printed under the table: "UPF = Upper Playfield   BL = Bottom Left   BR = Bottom Right
(n) = Item Identifier".

The matrix's own flipper cells, "Flipper Right" at 57 and "Flipper Left" at 58, are the cells the
switches list (printed 66) prints as "R Flipper Lane Change" and "L Flipper Lane Change".

The unnumbered foldout on PDF page 101 prints the Lamp-Matrix Table and the Switch-Matrix Table
again; both were read against this transcription cell by cell and every cell agrees.
