# Black Knight 2000 — Solenoids/Flashers lists and location drawings (printed 71 and 73)

Source: `Williams_1989_Black_Knight_2000_Operations_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 77 (printed "Black Knight
73", Lower Playfield Solenoids/Flashers above Rubber Parts) and PDF page 75 (printed "Black Knight
71", Upper Playfield Parts above Upper Playfield Solenoids/Flashers). Read from 345 dpi renders of
the native ~345 ppi bilevel scan; the PDF has no text layer. Every list row is transcribed.

the upper middle of the playfield, near the right three-bank drop target, where the list
puts the R Drop Target Flashers (05C).
