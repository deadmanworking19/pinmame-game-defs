## Playfield drawing callouts (PDF 111, printed 2-49)

The drawing is a top view of the playfield with circled two-digit callouts and leader lines, plus a separate tall drawing to its right labelled `Race Track Assy.` (printed in three lines: `Race` / `Track` / `Assy.`). Callout numbers read from `d1.png`, `d2.png`, `d3.png`.

- Left margin of the playfield, top to bottom: `05`, `16`, `26`, `02`, `04`, `25`, `15`, `23`, `28`, `09`, `03`.
- Right margin of the playfield, top to bottom: `27`, `08`, `11`, `33`, `34`, `13`, `21`, `12`, `22`, `28`, `10`, `24`, `01`.
- Race Track Assy. drawing (lower end): `18` and `19` (two adjacent circles sharing one leader).

Total: 26 circled callouts; the number `28` appears twice (once on each side of the playfield); every other number appears once, giving 25 distinct numbers: 01 02 03 04 05 08 09 10 11 12 13 15 16 18 19 21 22 23 24 25 26 27 28 33 34.

List items with NO callout on the drawing: `06` (Not Used), `07` (Knocker), `14` (Not Used), `17` (*Race Direction), `20` (*Tenth Corvette). Everything else in the list (01-05, 08-13, 15, 16, 18, 19, 21-28, 33, 34) has a callout. Items 18 and 19 point to the Race Track Assy. drawing rather than the playfield view.

## Uncertain readings

None. All cells were legible in the cited crops; the only interpretive step is the reading of the second-line `24-8802` entries as a second Coil/Flasher No. value belonging to the item above it (they are printed directly beneath, in the same column, with no item number).
