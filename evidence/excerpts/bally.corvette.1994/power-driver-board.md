# Corvette — Power Driver Board Connectors

Transcribed from `Bally_1994_Corvette_Manual.pdf`: the `Power Driver Board` / `A-12697-3` drawing (PDF page 143, printed folio `3-29`, upper part of the page) and its connector pin list, which runs over three printed pages: `3-29` (PDF page 143, under the drawing, two text columns; J101-J112), `3-30` (PDF page 144, two text columns; J113-J126) and `3-31` (PDF page 145, two text columns; J127-J138). The pages print the pin list as plain text lines of the form `Jnnn-n <wire colour> <function/destination>` with the wire colour spelled out in full (for example `Blue-White`, `Gray-Green`) and no comma after the wire colour and no column separators; connectors without pin rows are printed as one line such as `J103 Not Used`. Each line is given here as one table row, with everything printed after the pin designator kept literally in the `Printed text` column; misspellings are reproduced as printed. Blank lines between connectors on the page are not rows. Read from the rendered page (300 dpi scan), not from the OCR text. The pages carry no heading above the pin list. Column layout: page `3-29` left column `J101`-`J105`, right column `J106`-`J112`; page `3-30` left column `J113`-`J120`, right column `J121`-`J126`; page `3-31` left column `J127`-`J134-3` (connector `J134` is split: pins 1-3 end the left column), right column `J134-4`-`J138`.

Fuses printed: none. LEDs printed: none. Test points printed: none. Other printed labels: none. The drawing has no printed notes, no part numbers other than `A-12697-3` and no pin function labels.

## Uncertain readings

No pin list text is uncertain; all lines were read from legible crops (`p143-L1.png`, `p143-L2.png`, `p143-R1.png`, `p143-R2.png`, `p144-L0.png` to `p144-L3.png`, `p144-R0.png` to `p144-R3.png`, `p145-L0.png` to `p145-L2.png`, `p145-R0.png` to `p145-R2.png`). Notes on literal printing, not readings that are in doubt:

- Misspellings reproduced as printed: `J115-2` prints `White-Borwn`; `J117-2` prints `Gray-Yelow`; `J127-6` prints `playfioeld`.
- `J102-9` prints `Black Yellow` with a space rather than a hyphen, unlike `J102-5` and `J102-6` (`Black-Yellow`).
- The drawing prints the designator `J112` twice (a 5-pin header at the top edge and an 11-pin header in the bottom row); the pin list has an 11-pin `J121` and no drawn connector is labelled `J121`. Crops: `p143-b1.png`, `p143-t1.png`.
- Connectors drawn with pins but listed as a single `Not Used` line: `J103` (4 pins drawn), `J108` (3), `J109` (7), `J110` (9), `J111` (5), `J123` (5), `J128` (5), `J129` (5), `J131` (5), `J132` (5), `J133` (9), `J137` (9), `J105` (5). The pin list is transcribed as printed.
