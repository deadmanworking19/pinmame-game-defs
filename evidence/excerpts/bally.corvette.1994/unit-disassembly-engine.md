# Corvette — Unit Disassembly For Repair 16-9887, engine ramp page

Transcribed from `Bally_1994_Corvette_Unit_Disassembly_For_Repair_16_9887.pdf` (August 29, 1994), PDF page 6, read from a
200 dpi render. The page carries a numbered procedure, two drawings (a playfield detail with callouts 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8,
and an exploded view of the engine with its LT-5 cover, callouts 1, 7, 9, 10a and 10b) and a two-column repair table with
no column headings. Text is copied literally, including its spelling.

## REMOVE RAMP ONLY FROM ENGINE ASSEMBLY

```
1. Remove plastic LT-5 cover from engine mech. (2 screws)
2. Remove play field plastics on both sides of engine. (6 screws)
3. Remove garage popper wire. (2 screws, 1 nut)
4. Remove two screws holding racetrack ramp brackets in back panel.
5. Remove Left wire. (1 screw, 1 nut)
6. Remove right side plastics mounting bracket. (2 screws)
7. Remove screws on each side of engine, by slotted plates. (4 screws) Pull sides of
   engine up and off of mechanism.
       Use a right angle screwdriver
8. Remove screws in engine ramp. (3 screws)
9. Remove screws in ramp assembly. (2 in front, 1 in side rail in back, 1 in ramp guard)
10. Unplug all connectors.
       a. Four from front of ramp.
       b. One from gate at back of ramp.
11. Remove ramp from game.
12. Reasemble in reverse order
```
