# Corvette — Lamp Locations

Transcribed from `Bally_1994_Corvette_Manual.pdf`. The Lamp Locations material occupies two PDF pages:

- PDF page 106, printed page `2-44`, lower part (below the Lamp Matrix), headed `LAMP LOCATIONS`: a two-column parts
  list, left column items 11-28 and right column items 31-48 (no drawing on this page).
- PDF page 107, printed page `2-45`, headed `LAMP LOCATIONS (continued)`: a single parts list for items 51-88 with
  the bulb and bulb-sleeve footnotes, and, at the right, a playfield plan-view drawing with a numbered balloon for
  every lamp 11-88 and a small inset captioned `Race Tree Lamps`.

No separate backbox or insert-panel list is printed on either page; the two cabinet lamps (items 87 and 88) are marked
`**Located on Cabinet` in the footnote.

Header columns on both lists, as printed: `Item No.` | `Bulb No.` | `Lamp Assy. No.` | `Description` (the header
words wrap onto two lines: `Item` / `No.`, `Bulb` / `No.`, `Lamp` / `Assy. No.`). There is no Socket column. `---` is
the literal printed dash string for a blank cell (items 87 and 88 in the Lamp Assy. No. column). Read from the rendered
pages (300 dpi scan), not from the OCR text.

numbering (numbers 19, 20, 29, 30, ... 89 are not used).

Observations (not corrections):

- The Description of item 25 is `R Outer Loop Arrow` and of item 31 is `L Outer Loop Arrow` (abbreviated); the Lamp
  Matrix cells print `Right Outer Loop Arrow` and `Left Outer Loop Arrow`.
- Item 35 prints `ZR-1 Ramp Lock`; the Lamp Matrix cell 35 prints `ZR-1 Ramp`.
- Item 74 prints `Right Out Lane`; the Lamp Matrix cell 74 prints `Right Our Lane` (suspected typo there).
- Items 87 and 88 print a bulb number in a different series (`20-9663-18`, `20-9663-2`) and `---` in Lamp Assy. No.; the
  footnote marks them `**Located on Cabinet`.
- Only items 56, 57, 58, 71-75 use bulb `24-6549` (#44); all others use `24-8768` (#555), except items 87 and 88.

## Drawing on printed page 2-45 (PDF page 107)

A plan-view line drawing of the playfield (apron at the bottom, outhole and flippers lower centre) with circled
balloons joined to the lamp positions by leader lines ending in arrowheads. A separate inset below the playfield,
captioned `Race Tree Lamps` (bold), shows a rectangular panel with eight round lamp positions.

Balloon numbers visible, in two groups.

Playfield drawing: 11, 12, 13 (top, right of the top-arch circles); right edge, top to bottom: 57, 21, 22, 23, 24,
14, 15, 16, 25, 26, 27, 28, 17, 18, 66, 67, 73, 74, 65, 64, 63, 58, 87; left edge, top to bottom: 68, 53, 56, 75, 78, 77,
38, 76, 37, 31, 32, 46, 47, 48, 72, 71, 61, 62, 88; inside the playfield: 36, 35, 33, 34 (circle balloons beside the
stack of arrow inserts at the lower centre-left) and the nine hexagonal balloons 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, 51, 52, 54, 55
(the `Corvette` 1-9 cluster, drawn as a hexagon honeycomb with 55 in the middle). Items 87 and 88 are drawn as leader
arrows pointing off the lower edge of the playfield (cabinet).

Race Tree Lamps inset: 84 (two leaders to the top pair), 83 (two leaders to the second pair), 86 and 85
(left and right of the third row), 82 and 81 (bottom-left and bottom-right).

Every list item 11-88 has a balloon (64 of 64); no list item lacks a callout, and no balloon appears that is not a list
item. Balloons 81-86 appear only in the Race Tree inset; 87 and 88 appear on the playfield drawing's lower edge with
off-playfield arrows. The drawing carries no lamp colour marking and no legend.
