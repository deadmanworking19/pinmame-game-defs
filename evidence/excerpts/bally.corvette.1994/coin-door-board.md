# Corvette — Coin Door Interface Board Connectors

Transcribed from `Bally_1994_Corvette_Manual.pdf`: the `Coin Door Interface P.C.B. Assembly` / `A-17051-1` drawing and its connector pin list on PDF page 149 (printed folio `3-35`, drawing in the upper part, pin list under it in two text columns), and the `Coin Door Interface P.C.B. Schematic` on PDF page 150 (printed folio `3-36`). The pin list is printed as plain text lines of the form `Jn-n <wire colour> <function/destination>` with the wire colour spelled out in full and no comma after it; connectors without pin rows are printed as one line such as `J4 Not Used`. Each line is given here as one table row, with everything printed after the pin designator kept literally in the `Printed text` column. Blank lines between connectors on the page are not rows. Read from the rendered pages (300 dpi scans), not from the OCR text. The pin list has no heading line of its own. Column layout: left column `J1`-`J4`, right column `J5`-`J9`. The schematic page prints no part number under its title and has no pin list.

## Uncertain readings

- `S5` fifth right-hand terminal is printed `1`, where the sequence 1, 2, 3, 4, ?, 6, 7, 8 would suggest `5`. The crop reads `1`; it is recorded as printed and not corrected. Crops: `s150-s5-zoom4.png`, `s150-s5.png`.
- Diode attachment to specific nets was not traced beyond the connector line each symbol sits on. Crops: `s150-j3.png`, `s150-j7.png`, `s150-lower.png`.
- `J7-8` and `J7-11` print identical text (`White-Orange switch row 3 to cabinet`) in the pin list, while the schematic labels `J7` pins 8 and 11 both `SW. ROW 3`, pin 9 `SW. ROW 2` and pin 10 `SW. ROW 1` although the list prints `J7-9` and `J7-10` as `Not Used`; recorded as printed, not reconciled. Crops: `d149-R1.png`, `s150-j7.png`.
- The schematic labels `J1` pins 1-4 `DIG. SW4`, `DIG. SW3`, `DIG. SW2`, `DIG. SW1` and pins 5-8 `COIN 4`, `RT. COIN 3`, `CN COIN 2`, `LT. COIN 1`, while the pin list prints `J1-1` to `J1-8` as `dedicated row 8` to `dedicated row 1`; recorded as printed, not reconciled. Crops: `d149-L0.png`, `s150-j1.png`.
- All pin-list lines were read from legible crops (`d149-L0.png` to `d149-L2.png`, `d149-R0.png` to `d149-R2.png`, `d149-draw1.png`, `d149-draw2.png`); no pin-list text is uncertain.
