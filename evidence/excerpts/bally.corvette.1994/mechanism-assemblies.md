# Corvette — Mechanism Assemblies (parts lists)

Provenance. Literal transcription of the printed parts lists in the Bally "Corvette" (1994) service manual, section 2 (assembly pages, folios 2-2 to 2-35), and of Amendment 16-9890 (printed folios 10, 11 and 13). Source images are the 300 dpi grayscale renders under `manuals/by-machine/bally.corvette.1994/rendered-300/m-NNN.png` (NNN = PDF page) and `rendered-300-amendment/a-NN.png`; each cell was read from a zoomed crop of the render (Windows OCR was used only to locate text). Spelling, abbreviations, capitalisation, punctuation and part numbers are copied as printed; units and symbols (Ω, µ) as printed. A hyphen cell printed as "-" is kept. Drawing descriptions state only which numbered callouts and which printed labels are visible; they are not interpretations. Transcribed by a delegated worker; not yet checked by a curator (`reviewed` not set).
