## Printed lamp location drawing (printed page 23)

The drawing places 41, 42 and 43 in a row of three circles drawn above the playfield's top arch, outside the playfield outline: the back panel. It locates every other used lamp on the playfield plan except 44, 63 and 64, which appear nowhere on the page; 56 is not used. 47 and 48 are drawn as two separate circles one above the other at the lower center.
