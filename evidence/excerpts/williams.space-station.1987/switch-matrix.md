# Space Station — Switch-Matrix Table (printed page 51)

Source: `Williams_1987_Space_Station_Operations_Manual_and_Test_Procedures.pdf` (86 pages, SHA-256
`71e3212599e733ed432cdc533190ead6a925ff7a5d62862d7c09897566aaddbc`), PDF page 55 (the footer prints
`SPACE STATION 51`; Section 1 and 2 folios are PDF page minus 4). Read from the page's embedded 150 dpi
scan rendered at 300 dpi, cropped and enlarged in strips; the scan has no text layer and the Windows OCR
pass was used only to find pages. The page carries the switch-matrix wiring drawing at the top and the
"SPACE STATION Switch-Matrix Table" below it. Every cell is transcribed. In each table cell the switch
number is printed at the lower right and the name above it. The table prints no shaded cells and no
footnote.

## Matrix wiring drawing (top of page)

A single connector block, printed as a boxed column of arrow-shaped pins at the left edge, with the wire
and column/row label beside each line (no pin numbers are printed in the drawing). From top to bottom:
`Grn/Gry Column 8`, `Grn/Vio Column 7`, `Grn/Blu Column 6`, `Grn/Blk Column 5`, `Grn/Yel Column 4`,
`Grn/Orn Column 3`, `Grn/Red Column 2`, `Grn/Brn Column 1`, then `Wht/Brn Row 1`, `Wht/Red Row 2`,
`Wht/Orn Row 3`, `Wht/Yel Row 4`, `Wht/Grn Row 5`, `Wht/Blu Row 6`, `Wht/Vio Row 7`, `Wht/Gry Row 8`.

The drawing is an 8 x 8 grid of crossing lines; each crossing carries a slanted switch mark labelled with
a two-digit switch number. Column 1 holds `01` to `08` (rows 1 to 8), column 2 `09` to `16`, column 3 `17`
to `24`, column 4 `25` to `32`, column 5 `33` to `40`, column 6 `41` to `48`, column 7 `49`, `49`, `51`,
`52`, `53`, `54`, `55`, `56` and column 8 `57` to `64`: the row 2 crossing of column 7 is labelled `49`
a second time where the table prints `50`. A small symbol at the right, labelled `Column` (top), `Row`
(bottom) and captioned `Typical Switch Circuit`, shows a switch in series with a diode between the column
and row lines.

## Copies on PDF page 34 (printed "SPACE STATION 30") and PDF page 85

PDF page 34 ("TEST/DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURES (Continued)", "SWITCH TESTS. 1. Switch Levels.") prints the same
table under the Switch Levels text, which reads in part: "If, however, a switch *is* actuated (possibly
stuck closed), the player 3 display shows that switch's number, while the player 1 and 2 displays indicate
the switch's name." and "For *SPACE STATION*, switch numbers can range from 01 through 63." PDF page 85
(Section 3, no folio) prints it a third time under a copy of the Lamp-Matrix Table. Both copies have no
wiring drawing. Read cell by cell at the same enlargement, all 64 cells, the eight column headers and the
eight row headers agree with page 55 in every printed word and digit. Copies of one table are one source.

Normalization: none beyond writing each cell as "name (parenthetical) number" in row-major order, joining
lines printed one under another with a single space, and writing the two-line wire `WHT-` / `BRN` as
`WHT-BRN`.

Uncertain cells: none. The drawing's second `49` in column 7 is a misprint of the drawing, not a reading
uncertainty; the table and both copies print `50`.
