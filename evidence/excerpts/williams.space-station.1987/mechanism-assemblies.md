## Ball Shooter Lane Feeder p/n C-9638-3 and Ball Trough Switches (PDF 49, printed 45)

Ball Shooter Lane Feeder associated parts include `B-9362-R-1 Coil & Bracket Assembly` and `AE-23-800 Coil
Assembly`. Ball Trough Switches ("viewed from underside of playfield to show parts locations"):
`5647-09957-00 Ball Trough Switch; Center & Left`, `A-11660 Ball Trough Switch, Right` with `5647-12073-08
Submin. Switch`, and `5070-06258-00 Diode, 1N4001`; the drawing labels the switches `5647-09632-00
MICROSWITCH` and `5647-12073-08 MICROSWITCH`.

The Maintenance Information page (PDF 38, printed 34) says: "Figure 3 shows the two main lubrication points
of the Ball Shooter Lane Feeder (also the Right Eject Hole device,which utilizes the same mechanism)." and
"Lubrication to ensure proper operation also applies to other devices on SPACE STATION, such as the shaft of
the Ball Poppers, and the kickbigs."

## Amendment schematics (Amendments and Additions, page 2)

The amendment sheet adds `Schematic, Space Station Opto Positioner PC Board, C-11872` (two opto
interrupters `OPTO 1` and `OPTO 2`, `OPTO1 = OPTO2 = p/n 5490-10159-00`, each switching a 2N3906 PNP
transistor, `Q1` and `Q2`, whose outputs reach header `J1` pins labelled `R2`, `C1-C2` and `R1`, with `+12V`
and `Ground`), `Schematic, 3-Bank Drop Target Opto Switch Assembly, C-11318-1` (three optos, each switching
a 2N3906 onto its matrix row/column pair `R1/C1`, `R2/C2`, `R3/C3`) and `Schematic, 1-Bank Drop Target Opto
Switch Assembly, C-11903-L, -R` ("J1 used on C-11903-L; J2 used on C-11903-R"), with the "Single-Bank Drop
Target Assembly p/n C-11212-2" drawing and parts list (item 5 `AE-23-800 Coil Assembly`, item 14
`C-11903-R Opto Switch Assembly`).
