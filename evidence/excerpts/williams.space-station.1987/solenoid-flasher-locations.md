The fold-out's labels disagree with the Solenoid Table in four places, each of which the ROM's own Coil
Test settles in favour of the table: 03C is drawn as `Left Dock Kickbig` (table `Right Side + "AC"
Flashers`, ROM `RIGHT FLASHER`), 07A as `3-Bank Dr Tgt - Raise` with wire `Blk-Vio` (table `Knocker (or
Ticket Dispenser)` `Vio-Blk`, ROM `INSERT KNOCKER`), 07C as `Flame + "TI" Flashers` (table `Station
Flasher`, ROM `STATION FLASHER`), and 05C as `Top P/f + "TA"` (table `P'fld Top Panel + "TA"`, ROM `PANEL
FLASHER`). Sol. 21 is drawn as `CENTER JET BUMPER` where the table prints `Left Jet Bumper` and the ROM
`LEFT JET`. The amendment sheet corrects this page's `SPECIAL SOLENOIDS (25V)` supply wire to `RED-WHT`.

Uncertain cells: the Sol. 15 block title is split across the fold (`TO`... on page 81, `P PLAYFIELD` /
`EL FLASHERS` / `(Q14)` on page 82); it is read as `TOP PLAYFIELD PANEL FLASHERS (Q14)`.
