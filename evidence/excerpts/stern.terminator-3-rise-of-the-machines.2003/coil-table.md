# Terminator 3 — Coils Detailed Chart Table

Read from `Stern_2003_Terminator_3_Rise_of_the_Machines_Pinball_Game_Service_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 8, the front-matter
drawing printed `DR. 6` ("COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE"). Every cell was read from a 200 dpi render. The `Driver Ouput
Board` column prints one merged cell per group, `I/O Power Driver` with up and down arrows, and the auxiliary group
prints `Solenoid Expander Auxiliary`; it is omitted from the table below. The last column prints the coil gauge-turns
over the coil part number, or the bulb or relay type over its part number; both are joined with ` / ` here. Headings
keep the page's spelling (`Ouput`). The banner above the table shows the Portals path DIAG → COIL → TEST "SINGLE COIL
TEST", with the side note "In COIL MENU also select: CYCLING COIL TEST".

Page notes:

- Under row #8: "Q4 Note: Power Line Color is GRY~3A Fuse~RED-YEL and GRY-GRN~3A Fuse~YEL-RED".
- Under row #24: "Diode On Terminal Strip (if noted)"; no coil row carries a DOTS tag.
- Under row #32: "Note: In Test Flash Lamps Menu ("Flash" Icon), Flashers tested are all Flash Lamps located between
  Q1-Q32 (This Game: Q26-Q32)".
- Under the auxiliary rows: "Coil Note: Either -00B or -00T can be used for coil replacements. B/T listed is preferable
  for easier diode access & may differ on game."
- The control-line connectors skip J8-P2 and J9-P3 (row #2 is J8-P3, row #11 is J9-P4); no row prints either pin.
- The auxiliary group's last heading reads `Coil GA-Turn`; the other groups read `Coil GA-Turn or Bulb Type`.
- The page gives no legend for the grey bands. The four NOT USED rows (#6, #7, #19, #25) carry dark grey name cells and
  blank power cells; row #24 and the UK-only auxiliary group carry the light band.
