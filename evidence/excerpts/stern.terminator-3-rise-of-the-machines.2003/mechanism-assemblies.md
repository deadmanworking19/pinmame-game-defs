# Terminator 3 — major assemblies, the RPG backbox test and the shipping note

Read from `Stern_2003_Terminator_3_Rise_of_the_Machines_Pinball_Game_Service_Manual.pdf`: the front page (PDF page 2),
the RPG Backbox Test (PDF page 41), the playfield switch part table (PDF page 84) and Section 4 Chapter 2 "Drawings for
Major Assemblies & Ramps", PDF pages 94-116 (the printed page number is the PDF page less 18). Each assembly page is an
exploded drawing with a parts list whose part numbers and quantities are set in a re-encoded font; they were decoded from
the text layer by a character shift and spot-checked against renders, not read cell by cell, so this excerpt is not
marked reviewed. Only the items that bear on a controller device, its sensor or its actuator are listed; quoted text is
the page's own.

## Front page (PDF page 2)

- The page requires four pinballs, and: "Remove Shipping Post Ref. A from the Backbox Insert under the RPG Cannon before
  Powering Up".
- It names an optional Tournament Kit (TRPS).

## RPG Backbox Test (PDF page 41)

The test exercises the BACKBOX MOTOR RELAY (Q20), the BACKBOX KICKER (Q5) and the BACKBOX SWITCHES (the Stand-Up Target
switches 41-45). RUN cycles the motor through one full cycle; the Gun Trigger (or the optional Auto Launch Button) fires
the RPG kicker; the display draws a box per target and the test lights the Insert Lamps 65-69. "The Gun Trigger (Sw. 55)
is not part of the test, however, if it is not functioning, the Gun Trigger will not fire the Kicker Coil."

## Curator's observations (not printed)

- The coil chart (DR. 6) names Q21 BACK PANEL DIVERTER; the assembly that Q21's location on DR. 7 marks on the back panel
  is the Kicker (Ball Stop) Assembly 500-6219-01, whose text says it holds the ball in the back-panel trough.
- The bumper page labels switches 49 (Left), 50 (Right) and 51 (Bot.), matching DR. 4; DR. 6 names three bumper coils
  Q9-Q11. Its "#555 Wedge Base Bulb" is the bumper G.I. bulb that the G.I. wiring page counts among circuit V's #44
  bulbs.
- The switch grid (DR. 4) prints 515-6027-08 for the backbox targets 41-45. Page 113 orders that part as the target's
  "Switch & White Rectangular Target Sub-Assy." for individual parts, so the grid names the switch subassembly and the
  assembly page the whole target (four 500-6698-08 and one 500-6698-08-79).
