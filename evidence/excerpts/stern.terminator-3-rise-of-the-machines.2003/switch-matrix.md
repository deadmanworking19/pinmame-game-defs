## Side note (printed vertically at the right edge)

"Switch Part Note: ¥ Yen Coin Switch is 180-5091-00. Part numbers which start with 515- or 500- include the bracket,
target, and/or housing. Sw. 14, 15 & 33 Part Note: Transmitter & Receiver OPTO PC Boards are used for Switches 14, 15
(Trans: 515-0173-00; Rec: 515-0174-00) and 33 (Trans: 515-7307-00; Rec: 515-7308-00). Switch 56 Part Note: The Switch is
comprised of a Hanger Bracket (535-5319-00) and Contact Wire (535-7563-01) located in the Cabinet. Some Switch Diodes may
be located under the playfield, in the Cabinet or Backbox on Terminal Strips or Diode Boards and not on the assemblies.
DOTS: Diode On Terminal Strip. See Section 5, Chapter 2, Playfield Wiring."

## Location drawing (lower half of the page)

The playfield is drawn on its side with the back panel to the left (legend "Playfield ▲ / ◀ Backpanel / Backbox Insert
▶"): the left edge of the drawing is the rear of the playfield, the right edge the front, the top edge the playfield's
right side and the bottom edge its left side. Legend: white box = "Switches above Playfield.", black box = "Switches below
Playfield.", grey box = "Switches not on Playfield." Every box was read from the 200 dpi render; positions below are
given in playfield terms (rear/front, left/right).

- Rear (left edge of the drawing): 52 (grey) outside the rear rail near the right side; 40 (white) at the rear rail right
  of centre.
- Rear, right half: 46 and 24 at the right orbit near the rear right corner; 32 (white) at the right ramp; 39, 38 and 37
  down the top lanes; 50, 49 and 51 at the three bumpers.
- Rear centre: 36 at the VUK.
- Rear, left half: 33, 34 and 35 stacked at the lockup; 31 (white) beside them nearer the rear rail; 29, 30 (white) and
  28 at the left orbit and left ramp near the left rail.
- Centre: 23 at the captive ball; 25 at the drop target left of centre; 10 at the standup left of centre, midway down.
- Mid-field: 19, 18 and 17 at the left 3-bank; 20, 21 and 22 at the right 3-bank.
- Front right: 60 and 61 at the right outlane and return lane; 62 twice at the right slingshot; 16 at the shooter lane;
  14 and 15 each drawn twice (transmitter and receiver) at the trough's right end; 13, 12 and 11 down the trough.
- Front left: 57 and 58 at the left outlane and return lane; 59 twice at the left slingshot.
- Flippers and cabinet: DS-4 (grey label) at the right flipper, DS-2 (grey label) at the left flipper; DS-3 and DS-1
  (grey) at the cabinet's right and left front corners; 53 (grey) with the boxed note "Optional Tournament Kit Required"
  at the front centre, and 54 (grey) at the cabinet's left front.
- Back panel inset (lower left): a row of ten small circles, four bumper-like discs, a trough drawing marked
  "From Playfield", 52 (grey) with an arrow, and the note "Switch 52 located behind the Back Panel, under the Back Panel
  Trough."
- Backbox insert inset (lower right): 41, 42, 43, 44 and 45 (grey) stacked beside a fan of five targets on the edge of
  the cannon's arc.
- Not drawn: 1-9, 26, 27, 47, 48, 55, 56, 63, 64, DS-5 to DS-8.
