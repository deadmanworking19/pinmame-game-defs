## Side note (printed vertically down the left edge)

"Lamp Part Note: #555 Bulb Clear = 165-5002-00. #44 Bulb Clear = 165-5000-44. See Section 4, Chapter 1, Parts
Identification & Location, Pages 72-74 for more details on bulbs and corresponding sockets. Some Lamp Diodes may be located
under the playfield, in the Cabinet or Backbox on Terminal Strips and not on or with the Lamp Socket. DOTS: Diode On
Terminal Strip See Section 5, Chapter 2, Playfield Wiring." The note runs across the page fold; the words split by the
fold were joined.

## Location drawing (lower half of the page)

The playfield is drawn on its side with the back panel to the left, as on DR. 4 (the left edge of the drawing is the rear
of the playfield, the top edge its right side). Legend: white box = "Lamps above Playfield.", black box = "Lamps below
Playfield.", grey box = "Lamps not on Playfield." Every numbered box is black except 53, 54 and 65-69 (grey). The three
pop bumpers carry "GI" labels. Positions are given in playfield terms.

- Rear, right of centre: 40, 39 and 38 down the top lanes; 71 beside them; the three pop bumpers (each labelled GI)
  just in front of them.
- Right side, from rear to front: 49, 50 and 51; 16, 15 and 14 at the right 3-bank; 36 and 35 at the right outlane and
  return lane.
- Rear left: 64 at the T-X figure; 45, 46 and 47 and 57, 58 and 59 along the T-X arm cannon.
- Centre: 55, 56, 48, 62, 61 and 60 in a cluster; 31, 32 and 52 behind the value arc 30, 29, 28, 27, 26, 25 and 77; the
  right security levels 13, 12, 11, 10 and 9 and the left security levels 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1 in two arcs; 22, 21, 20, 19,
  18, 17 and 76 in a third arc in front of them.
- Left side, from rear to front: 41, 42 and 43; 23, 24, 72 and 44 inboard of them; 8, 7 and 6 at the left 3-bank; 34,
  33 and 37 at the left return lane, outlane and kickback.
- Front centre: 78, 75, 73, 74 and 80 between the flippers, 80 nearest the front.
- Cabinet: 54 (grey, with the boxed note "Optional Tournament Kit Required") at the front centre and 53 (grey) at the
  cabinet's left front.
- Back panel inset (lower left): a row of ten small circles and four bumper-like discs, the note "Backpanel Top Row and
  Playfield Pop Bumpers are General Illumination (GIs). No Controlled Lamps on Backpanel.", and a trough marked
  "From Playfield".
- Backbox insert inset (lower right): 65, 66, 67, 68 and 69 (grey) stacked along the edge of the cannon arc, with the
  note "Small Lamps (not numbered) are Gen. Illum. (GIs), Large Lamps (not numbered) are Flash Lamps (see next page),
  Center RPG Area X8 Small Lamps are Switched GIs (see next page)."
- Not drawn: 63, 70, 79.

The grid prints lamp 53 START BUTTON and 54 TOURNAMENT BUTTON; the drawing puts 53 at the cabinet's left front and 54 at
the front centre beside the tournament-kit note, the same places as switch 54 START BUTTON and switch 53 TOURNAMENT START
on DR. 4.
