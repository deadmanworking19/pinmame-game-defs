# Terminator 3 — Coil & Flash Lamp Locations

Read from `Stern_2003_Terminator_3_Rise_of_the_Machines_Pinball_Game_Service_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 9, the front-matter
drawing printed `DR. 7` ("COIL & FLASH LAMP LOCATIONS"). Every box was read from a 200 dpi render. Unlike DR. 4 and DR.
5, the playfield is drawn upright with the rear at the top ("Playfield ▲"). The back panel is drawn above it ("◀
Backpanel") and the backbox insert to its right ("Backbox Insert ▼"). Legend: white box = "Coils / Flash Lamps above
Playfield.", black box = "Coils / Flash Lamps below Playfield.", grey box = "Coils / Flash Lamps not on Playfield.", and a
boxed word = "Color of Mini-Mars of Flash Lamp Bulb."

## Back panel (drawn above the playfield)

- 30 (grey) four times across the top row: two at the left and right ends and two in the middle, with "Red" mini-mars
  labels beside each.
- 21 (grey) on a coil drawn upright in the middle of the back panel trough, and 21 (grey) a second time on the
  playfield's rear edge below it.

## Playfield

- Rear left: 26 (white) twice, labelled "Blue", at the T-X figure; 22 (black) between them; 14 (black) in front of them.
- Rear centre: 23 (black) behind the bumpers; 13 (black) at the VUK beside the bumpers.
- Bumpers: 9 (black, left), 10 (black, right), 11 (black, bottom).
- Left centre: 8 (black) and 3 (black) stacked at the drop target.
- Mid-field: 31 (white) twice, labelled "Red", at the left and right side; 29 (black) at the centre.
- Lower playfield: Aux. 1 (black) at the left outlane and Aux. 3 (black) at the right outlane; 17 and 18 (black) at the
  slingshots; 32 (white) twice, labelled "Red", at the slingshots' outer ends; 15 and 16 (black) at the flippers; Aux. 2
  (black) between the flippers; 12 (white) at the left outlane kickback; 1 (white) at the trough's right end; 2 (white)
  at the shooter lane.

## Backbox insert (drawn right of the playfield)

- 27 (grey) four times down the left side of the insert.
- 28 (grey) four times down the right side.
- 4 (grey) in the centre of the cannon's arc, with arrows to eight small lamps around the arc.
- 5 (grey) at the cannon's base on the right.
- 20 (grey) twice: once beside a part labelled "Relay" at the top right and once beside a part labelled "Motor" at the
  bottom right, joined by a dotted line.

## Not drawn

6, 7, 19, 24 and 25.

## Typical wiring panel

The right half of the page prints "Typical Switch Wiring & Schematic" (a switch with N.C., N.O. and COM. terminals, WHT
and GRN wires and a 1N4001 diode; "(Column: Switch Drive Wire) GRN-XXX", "(Row: Switch Return Wire) WHT-XXX", a "Blocking
Diode"), "Dedicated Switch Schematic" ("Dedicated Sw. Inputs GRY-XXX" to an N.O. terminal, "Ground BLK" to COM., no
diode), "Typical Lamp Wiring & Schematic" ("YEL-XXX 18V COL", "RED-XXX GND ROW", a 1N4001 diode) and "Typical Coil
Wiring" (a 1N4004 diode, "Anode (Non-Banded Side)" to the "Drive Transistor" and "Cathode (Banded Side)" to the "Power
Supply Voltage"). The left margin carries the same diode note as DR. 4 and DR. 5, reading "Some Coil or Flash Lamp Diodes
may be located under the playfield, in the Cabinet or Backbox on Terminal Strips or Diode Boards and not on the
assemblies. DOTS: Diode On Terminal Strip See Sec. 5, Chp. 2, Playfield Wiring."
