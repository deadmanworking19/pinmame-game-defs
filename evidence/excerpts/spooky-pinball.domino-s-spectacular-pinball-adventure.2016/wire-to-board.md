# Domino’s wire-to-board chart (DOMINOS-WIRE-TO-BOARD.pdf)

Transcribed by a Sonnet 5.5 worker from the chart's text layer (the RZ chart from its raster), every pin aligned by its tick; the Opus 5.5 curator spot-checked the coil banks, the cabinet header and the GI_1 header (at 300 dpi) against the render, not every pin. Uncertain readings are listed at the end.

Pins are listed in tick order as drawn on the chart (connector header lines run left to right as printed, vertical headers top to bottom). '(blank)' means no wire text is printed at that tick; '(unlabeled tick)' means a tick with no pin label.

## Uncertain readings

- OPTOS box vs connector headers: the box prints 'Opto - 4' over blue/purp, black/purp, blue/orange, black/orange (Scoop Emitter/Receiver) and 'Opto - 3' over blue/red, black/red, blue/black, black/blue (Noid Loop Emitter/Receiver). The connector headers print the opposite: header Opto3 carries blue/purp.. black/orange and header Opto4 carries blue/red.. black/blue. Both are transcribed as printed; the chart is internally inconsistent about which opto number is Scoop and which is Noid Loop. (RZ's chart has the same headers but its box agrees with them.)
- GI_1 colour alignment: the wire texts sit directly under ticks 12, 13, 14, '.', 5V and the unlabeled 10th tick: 'brown/White - Right Flasher' under 12, 'brown/Purple - Left Flasher' under 13, 'brown/Green - GI' under 14, 'brown/yellow - Oven Flasher' under '.', 'brown - POWER' under 5V, and 'key' under the unlabeled 10th tick. Ticks 8-11 have no text. The '.' marker (which elsewhere marks the key pin) carrying a wire is odd; alignment could be off by one tick in the artwork. Read from tick x positions at 220 dpi.
- Typo kept verbatim: 'whtie/red' (RGB Com Out, 5v pin).
- Light Column pins 6 and 7 have no wire colour printed (the lamp matrix does print YELLOW VIOLET / YELLOW GRAY for columns 6/7); transcribed as blank.
- Switch Column pins 7, 6, 5 have no wire colour printed (the switch matrix prints GREEN GRAY for column 5, and nothing for columns 6/7); transcribed as blank.
- Sol Bank 0 coils 0 and 1 (Knocker/Shaker, both '(option)'), coil 7, Bank 1 coils 13-15 and Bank 2 coils 21-23 have no wire colour and no name ('-' only); transcribed as blank.
- Leading unlabeled tick on Switch Row, Switch Column, Light Row and Light Column, and an extra unlabeled tick after 5V on GI_1, plus two unlabeled ticks after the 'key' tick on Cabinet RGB: counted from the artwork, they carry no text.
- Fuse leader lines (F1->Sol Bank 0, F2->Sol Bank 1, F3->Sol Bank 2) read from the drawn lines; not stated in text.
- Switch matrix header: COLUMN 7 and COLUMN 6 carry no wire colour (COLUMN 5 GREEN GRAY ... COLUMN 0 GREEN WHITE). Lamp matrix columns 7/6 contain no labels at all; lamp COLUMN 7..0 header colours are YELLOW GRAY, YELLOW VIOLET, YELLOW BLUE, YELLOW GREEN, YELLOW BLACK, YELLOW WHITE, YELLOW RED, YELLOW BROWN.
- Lamp cells 0, 1, 14, 15 are single letters 'O', 'V', 'E', 'N' (the OVEN letters) and 22 reads 'Battle Noid N' (the 'N' is printed as the third line); kept as printed. Switch cell 25 reads '5 Lane' (the digit 5 is part of the label, not the cell number 5).
- Switch cell 1/2/3 labels end in the digits 1/2/3 ('Trough Ball 1'), part of the label; the cell numbers are the small top-right ones.
