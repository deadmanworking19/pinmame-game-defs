# Bram Stoker's Dracula — Fliptronic II upper-flipper circuits on other machines

excerpts print it. Each block quotes the rows verbatim from the named excerpt, whose SHA-256 is given as
it stood when this record was written. Dracula's own 3-8 table pairs 33/34 with "Up Lt. F." (Q1/Q5,
J902-3/J902-1) and 35/36 with "Up Rt. F." (Q2/Q7, J902-6/J902-4); see `solenoid-flasher-wiring.md`.

## Reading

- Twilight Zone, the one machine here with both upper flippers fitted and a known-working script, wires its
  upper-right flipper on Q2/Q7 (J902-6/J902-4) and its upper-left on Q1/Q5 (J902-3/J902-1); its script drives
  the upper-right flipper from `SolCallback(sURFlipper)`, core.vbs constant 34, and the upper-left from
  `sULFlipper`, 36 (`machines/partial/bally/twilight-zone-1993.json`, solenoids 33-36).
- Every other table above prints each of these rows it carries on the same pin: 33 on J902-6, 34 on J902-4, 35
  on J902-3 and 36 on J902-1. Their transistor columns agree too (33 Q2, 34 Q7, 35 Q1, 36 Q5), except The
  Shadow's main table, which prints Q2 beside J902-3 on its 35 row and Q7 beside J902-1 on its 36 row while
  its own flipper rows print Q1 and Q5 for those pins. Corvette prints only rows 33 and 34, and World Cup
  Soccer no 36 row.
- Pinned PinMAME's `src/wpc/core.h` names 33/34 `sURFlipPow`/`sURFlip` and 35/36 `sULFlipPow`/`sULFlip`, and
  the WPC-Fliptronic controller profile states the same mapping.
