## Facts established from this page

- **Lamps 58 and 61-63 are on the playfield's back panel, not in the backbox.** The Lamp Locations list
  (printed 2-44) gives lamps 61, 62 and 63 (L./M./R. Skill) lamp assembly A-16337 and lamp 58 (L. Ramp
  Diverted) B-12224, and draws all four in an inset box labelled `A-16399 Back Panel Assy.` above the
  playfield. This assembly is the upright panel at the rear of the playfield, carrying a playfield plastic,
  the 3-lamp PCB A-16337-1 and the B-12224 single lamp assembly.
- The panel also carries two single flashlamp assemblies (item 13), one near each end of the face view.
  The page does not number them; the solenoid drawing (printed 2-45) prints callouts 20 (T.L. Corner) and
  17 (T.R. Corner) in the playfield's top corners, where the retained table puts F20_Dome and F17_Dome on
  the rear edge, which is consistent with them.
