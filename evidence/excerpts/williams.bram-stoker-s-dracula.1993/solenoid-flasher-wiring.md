## Facts established from this page

- **No blank/populated-transistor-but-no-function row exists** anywhere in 1-28 or
  33-36 (unlike Monster Bash's solenoids 4 and 7). Every printed row is a real device.
- **Only two flipper rows exist** ("Lower Left Flipper", "Lower Right Flipper"); there is
  no "Upper Left/Right Flipper" row, confirming this machine has no upper-flipper coils.
- **Solenoids 33-36 are printed under their real function names** (Up/Down Post
  Diverter, Right Gate, Castle Release Post, Left Gate Actuator) and driven through the
  upper-flipper power/hold driver-transistor pairs this machine does not need for flippers —
  printed "Up Lt. F." for 33/34, "Up Rt. F." for 35/36.
- **The printed pairing of 33-36 with a side is the outlier.** `src/wpc/core.h` defines
  `sURFlipPow`/`sURFlip` at `CORE_FIRSTUFLIPSOL`(33)/34 and `sULFlipPow`/`sULFlip` at
  35/36, so PinMAME calls 33/34 the upper-**right** circuit and 35/36 the upper-**left**
  one, the opposite of this page's labels; Twilight Zone's known-working script and manual
  and twelve other Fliptronic II and WPC-95 solenoid tables agree with PinMAME on the pins
  (33/34 on J902-6/J902-4; 35/36 on J902-3/J902-1) and, except The Shadow's main table, on
  the transistors (Q2/Q7; Q1/Q5): that table prints Q2 and Q7 beside J902-3 and J902-1 on
  its 35/36 rows while its own flipper rows print Q1 and Q5 for those pins
  (`fliptronic-ii-upper-circuits.md`). The printed rows above are kept
  verbatim. The structured definition gives each address the circuit that evidence settles,
  with this page's pin, wire colour and voltage pin for that circuit. The device functions
  and addresses are not in doubt; `conflict.upper-flipper-circuit-side-naming` is ignored as
  a wiring detail.
- **The lower-flipper drive cells are merged.** `J902-7,9` and `J902-11,13` each cover a
  flipper's power and hold windings, told apart only by the two wire colours; the 3-18
  Fliptronic II Flipper Circuits page (`fliptronic-flipper-circuits.md`) gives the pin of
  each colour: power `J902-9` Blue-Gray and `J902-13` Blue-Violet, hold `J902-7`
  Orange-Blue and `J902-11` Orange-Green.
- **GI strings 3 and 4 have no playfield voltage/drive connection at all** — only
  backbox (04) and backbox+cabinet (05) columns are populated — matching the retained
  script's `GIUpdate2`, which drives only GI addresses 0-2 through real playfield light
  collections and treats 3/4 as single `gibg4`/`gibg5` bulbs.
