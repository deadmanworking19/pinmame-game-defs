# Star Gazer — playfield switch location drawing

Transcribed from `Stern_1980_Star_Gazer_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 19 (printed page 18), the drawing captioned "STAR GAZER / SWITCHES" together with the list at its upper left headed "SWITCHES NOT ON PLAYFIELD:". The page is a scan of a plain outline drawing of the playfield; the number beside each switch symbol is the manual's own switch number, the same numbers as the switch-identification table. The drawing was checked at four-times enlargement of the native scan.

## Numbers printed on the playfield outline

Reading the outline with the playfield upright (shooter lane at the right):

- Top-left arch of stand-up targets, left to right: `10`, `11`, `17`, `18`.
- Top-right arch of stand-up targets, from the top of the arch down the right wall: `20`, `19`, `21`, `31`, `32`, `38`. The label `20` sits nearest the apex of the arch and `19` just to its right.
- Three circles, the bumpers, numbered `12` (top left), `13` (top right) and `14` (centre, below the other two).
- `9` at a spinner drawn at the upper-left lane mouth; `4` at a spinner on the left wall; `5` at a spinner at the lower end of the right-hand lane.
- Upper-left drop-target bank, three dashes numbered `25`, `26`, `27` left to right, with the bank label `1B`.
- Right drop-target bank, three dashes numbered `28`, `29`, `30` from top to bottom, with the bank label `1A`.
- Left drop-target bank, three dashes numbered `24`, `23`, `22` from top to bottom, with the bank label `1C`.
- Two stand-up targets on the lower right wall, `39` above `40`.
- `35` at the left outlane and `34` at the right outlane, each drawn as a small elongated symbol beside its lane.
- `37` (left) and `36` (right) beside two star-shaped circles drawn above the flippers; the manual's table names these the left and right roll-over buttons.
- `15` beside the upper end of the right slingshot. No number is printed at the left slingshot, although the table lists `16 LEFT SLINGSHOT`.
- `33` between the flipper bays, at the bottom of the drawing, where the outhole is.

Beside each outlane guide post the drawing prints three letters, `L`, `M`, `C` (left side, top to bottom `C`, `M`, `L`; right side `C`, `M`, `L` mirrored); they label the post's adjustment positions and are not switch numbers.

The drawing is not to scale and is not dimensioned. It prints no coordinates, bulbs or lamp numbers.
