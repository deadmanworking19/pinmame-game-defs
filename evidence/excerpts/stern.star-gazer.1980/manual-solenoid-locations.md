# Star Gazer — playfield solenoid location drawing

Transcribed from `Stern_1980_Star_Gazer_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 21 (printed page 20), the drawing captioned "STAR GAZER / SOLENOIDS" together with the list at its upper left headed "SOLENOIDS NOT ON PLAYFIELD:". It is a plain outline of the playfield; each number is the manual's solenoid number, the same numbers as the solenoid-identification table.

## Solenoids not on the playfield

| number | printed location |
| ---: | --- |
| 3 | KNOCKER |
| 19 | COIN LOCKOUT |

## Numbers printed on the playfield outline

With the shooter lane at the right:

- `1` beside the lower-left slingshot and `2` beside the lower-right slingshot.
- `4` beside the left wall, level with the left drop-target bank.
- `5` beside the upper-left lane mouth at the end of the upper-left drop-target bank, and `6` beside the lower end of the right-hand lane at the right drop-target bank.
- `7` inside the top-left circle, `8` inside the centre circle and `9` inside the top-right circle; the three circles are the bumpers.
- `10` at the bottom centre, between the flipper bays, where the outhole is.
- `15` printed twice, once at the base of each lower flipper.

The drawing prints no `3` or `19` on the playfield, which agrees with the list above it, and prints no number for the positions the manual's table calls OPEN.
