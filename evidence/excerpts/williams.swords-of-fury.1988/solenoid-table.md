## Notes printed below the table

1. `Wire colors, except flipper Orn-Vio and Orn-Gry, are ground connections (to coil terminal with unbanded end of diode). Flipper Orn-Vio and Orn-Gry wires connect from CPU Board to flipper switch.`
2. `Flipper connections shown in braces are from flipper switch to flipper coil.`
3. `"A" coils are pulsed, when Sol. 12 is de-energized; "C" coils are pulsed, with Sol. 12 energized. Wire colors in brackets are those from respective A and C terminals corresponding to the J1- terminal connection listed for the Aux Power Driver Bd., which controls the device pulsing by Sol. 12.`
4. `Relay is mounted on Relay Bd. p/n C-11998-1.`
5. `Relay is mounted on Aux Pwr Driver Bd. D-12247 in the backbox.`

## Explicit uncertainty

The page-32 `03A`/`03C` connector print overlays the text `(2P12-13` beside the two rows; its
graphic grouping is not sufficiently clear to assert which individual row owns it. The exact text
and position are retained above; assignment is deliberately unresolved. The duplicate flipper
identifier mismatch is likewise a source-layout discrepancy, not a basis for a hardware decision.
