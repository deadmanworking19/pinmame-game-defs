# Playfield Terminal Strips, Fuses & Misc. Wiring Descriptions & Locations

Transcribed from `Lord_of_the_Rings_Manual-2022.pdf`, PDF page 123 (printed Section 5, Chapter 2, page 105). The page is fully vector: a bottom view of the playfield `shown as if leaning up against the Backbox` (front edge at the top), with every below-playfield assembly drawn and labelled. Legend as printed: `S = Switch Matrix Number (Below P/F Only)`, `L = Lamp Matrix Number (Below P/F Only)`, `Q = Coil / Flash (see Page 101)`, `DTS / TS = ( Diode ) Terminal Strips (A-E)`. The accompanying image keeps the drawing.

Labels the definition uses, as printed, with what each labels:

| Printed | Labels |
| --- | --- |
| S11, S12, S13 | the three trough positions along the ball trough |
| S14 (twice), S15 (twice) | the dual opto transmitter and receiver boards at the trough's eject end; each board carries both beams |
| S16 | the shooter-lane switch |
| Q01, Q02 | the trough up-kicker and the auto launch coil assemblies |
| DS2, DS4 | the end-of-stroke blade switches drawn at the outer end of the left and right flipper mechanisms |
| Q15, Q16 | the left and right flipper coil assemblies |
| 19-LED PCB | board 520-5242-00 between the flippers, drawn with its connector along its right-hand edge |
| Q03, Q04, Q05 | the left, top and right vertical up-kicker coils |
| Q09, Q10, Q11 | the three pop-bumper coils, each with a `Q25` flasher label |
| Q13 | the orbit pin coil |
| Q17, Q18 | the slingshot coils |
| Q19 | the top saucer coil |
| Q20 | the Balrog motor relay board 520-5066-00 |
| Q21 | the sword lock release coil |
| Q22, S31, S32 | the Balrog motor bracket, with the Balrog Open microswitch S31 on its left and the Balrog Closed microswitch S32 on its right |
| ( Q7 ) | the right tower coil, circled at the rear right |
| Q09 (second) | printed beside `LOOP DIVERTER FUSE` at the loop diverter assembly; the bumper coil carries its own `Q09`, so this second one names the loop diverter Q08 |
| GI (20 labels) | G.I. sockets drawn with green rings |

Printed at the top of the page: `Please Note: Terminal Strip(s), Diode Board(s) and/or Fuse Holder(s) locations shown, represent the general location (your game may differ slightly).` The note qualifies those three item classes only; the switch, coil and board assemblies are drawn in place.

Fuses printed on the page: `LOWER LEFT FLIPPER FUSE` (RED-YEL / GRY-YEL, 3A S.B.), `LOWER RIGHT FLIPPER FUSE` (RED-YEL / BLU-YEL, 3A S.B.) and `LOOP DIVERTER FUSE` (YEL-VIO / GRY-YEL, 3A S.B.), with `All fuses are rated: 3A 250v Slo-Blo Do Not Over-Fuse`. Terminal strips A-E: A `Switch: 09 LEFT VUK` (ORG-BRN to GRN-RED, WHT-BRN), B `Switch: 30 RIGHT VUK` (ORG-BLU to GRN-YEL, WHT-BLU), C `Switch: 25 RIGHT RAMP ENTER` (WHT-BRN, ORG-BRN to GRN-YEL), D `Jumper for Flash Q25 Pops X3 in the Bumpers (1 Flash per Pop)` (BLACK, ORANGE, `NO DIODE REQUIRED (JUMPER)`), E `Coil: 07 RIGHT TOWER` (BRN-VIO from BLK lead, YEL-VIO from RED lead).
