## PDF 126: UK Post Save (complete boxed note)

> 2 Extra Cabinet Buttons for the Post Save Feature are used. The Left Button operates the Left Outlane Ball Deflector. The Right Button operates the Right Outlane Ball Deflector. Both buttons pushed together operate the Center Up/Down Post. Both buttons are located under the Flipper Buttons.

The note is printed between dashed rules next to `UK ONLY:`. The coil chart (DR. 6) names the three outputs `AUX 1: LEFT UP/DOWN POST`, `AUX 2: CENTER UP/DOWN POST` and `AUX 3: RIGHT UP/DOWN POST`.
