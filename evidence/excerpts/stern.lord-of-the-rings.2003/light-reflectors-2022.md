## PDF 83: the drawing

`Playfield - General Parts, ...Rails... & Switches (Above) Continued` is a raster playfield with vector item callouts. Two black `5` callouts point with arrowheads at the return-lane plastics: the left one at the lower end of the left return lane, beside the left slingshot plastic, and the right one at the top of the right outlane plastic, above the right Helm's Deep flasher dome (`6a`). The photograph on the same page carries the same two `5` callouts at the same places. Item 6a is `Mini-Mars Lite Cover (Snap-In) Yellow`, item 35 `Ball Guide (Plastic, Long Clear)`.
