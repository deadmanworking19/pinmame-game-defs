# Playfield General Parts (Below)

Transcribed from `Lord_of_the_Rings_Manual-2022.pdf`, PDF page 84 (printed Section 4, Chapter 1, page 66). The page pairs this parts table with a raster bottom view of the playfield, front edge at the top. The drawing marks each part with the table's item: black boxes for the numbered items, white boxes for the lettered switches and boards. The drawing callout check reads its letter boxes A (trough switches 11-13), B (the two flipper EOS switches) and G (the three pop-bumper switches).
