# FunHouse — Lamp Locations drawing (November 1990 handbook, printed page 7)

Source: `Williams_1990_Funhouse_Operator_s_Handbook_November_1990_OCR_searchable.pdf`, PDF page 8 (printed "7"), the full-page plan drawing of the playfield titled "Lamp Locations", committed at its native 600 dpi as `operator-handbook-lamp-locations.webp`. It is the same artwork as the 2-36 drawing of the operations manual, printed larger.

The drawing prints every lamp number it shows in one of three ways:

- Inside the insert it names (round, triangular, arrow or kidney insert, or a jet-bumper cap): 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 27, 28, 51, 52, 53, 61 (twice), 63, 64, 65, 66, 67, 68, 72, 73, 81, 82 (twice), 83, 84, 85, 86, 87 and 88.
- Beside the round insert or socket it names, with no leader: 21, 24, 25, 26, 31, 32, 33, 34, 38, 46 and 47 around the clock, and 54, 55, 56 and 57 on and beside the Steps track.
- At the end of a straight leader: 22, 23, 35, 36, 37, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45 and 48 into the clock's arrow inserts, and the fan of six leaders 71, 74, 75, 76, 77 and 78 that converge on the Magic Mirror.

61 (Rt. Flipper Lanes) and 82 (Special Outlanes) are each drawn twice, once on each side. 88 (Start Button) is drawn in the cabinet area below the playfield. 58 (Rt. Gangway Overhead Lamp) and 62 (S-T-E-P "S") do not appear on the drawing.

The three Steps lamps are drawn as small round sockets on and beside the Steps track's left rail, each with its number printed just left of it, 56 at the top, 55 below it and 54 lowest: not in the vertical stack on the sign beside the track where the retained table lights them.

Every number's read position, the controls each page was fitted on and the checks made against the definition are in `tools/seeds/williams/funhouse-1990-callouts.json`.
