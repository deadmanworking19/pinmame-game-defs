# FunHouse — Solenoid Locations drawing (November 1990 handbook, printed page 5)

Source: `Williams_1990_Funhouse_Operator_s_Handbook_November_1990_OCR_searchable.pdf`, PDF page 6 (printed "5"), the full-page plan drawing of the playfield titled "Solenoid Locations", committed at its native 600 dpi as `operator-handbook-solenoid-locations.webp`. It is the same artwork as the 2-40 drawing of the operations manual, printed larger. The page has no leader lines.

The drawing prints every solenoid number it shows in one of three ways:

- Inside the part it names: 9, 10 and 11 in the three jet bumpers; 4 in the tunnel kickout block; 18 in the round Dummy flasher dome; 19 in the clock centre; 20 in the kidney-shaped Superdog insert; 24, 23 and 17 in the three diamond inserts beside the Steps (top to bottom); 17, 23 and 24 in the three round flasher domes on the right side wall (top to bottom).
- Beside the part it names, with no leader: 8 on the lock-release box beside the lock; 12 and 13 at the slingshots; 14 under the Steps gate's pivot post on the left outlane guide.
- In open space or along an edge, with no leader: 22, 17, 23 and 24 in a row along the top rail (the back-panel parts); 1 below the bottom arch; 2 beside the ramp diverter's rail; 3 beside the hideout kicker; 5 and 6 on the trap door plate; 15 in the lower right; 16, 25, 26, 27 and 28 inside Rudy's head outline; 21 at the top of the jaw's guide pair.

7 (Knocker, a cabinet part) does not appear.

Every number's read position, the controls each page was fitted on and the checks made against the definition are in `tools/seeds/williams/funhouse-1990-callouts.json`.
