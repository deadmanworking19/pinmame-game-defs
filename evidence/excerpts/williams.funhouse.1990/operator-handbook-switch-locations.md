# FunHouse — Switch Locations drawing (November 1990 handbook, printed page 9)

Source: `Williams_1990_Funhouse_Operator_s_Handbook_November_1990_OCR_searchable.pdf`, PDF page 10 (printed "9"), the full-page plan drawing of the playfield titled "Switch Locations", committed at its native 600 dpi as `operator-handbook-switch-locations.webp`. It is the same artwork as the 2-38 drawing of the operations manual, printed larger.

The drawing prints every switch number it shows in one of four ways:

- At the end of a straight leader from the page margin: 15, 17, 26, 36, 42, 43, 47, 52, 54, 57, 61, 62, 64, 66, 71 and 75.
- Inside the part it names: 18, 68 and 77 in the three jet bumpers, 58 in the tunnel kickout block, 67 in the drop hole, 76 on the trap door plate and 33 on the gangway roll-under gate.
- Beside the part it names, with no leader: 41 and 53 at the slingshots, 44 under the wind-tunnel hole.
- In open space or along an edge, with no leader and no part drawn at it: 16 and 55 (ramp switches) along the top rail; 25, 27 and 28 along the lock slot; 32, 34 and 37 beside the three Superdog targets; 35, 38, 45, 46, 48, 51, 56, 63, 65, 72, 73 and 74.

11, 12, 13, 14, 21 and 22 are printed in the cabinet area below the playfield. 23, 24, 31, 78 and 82-88 do not appear. A number printed "81" sits on a round insert beside the S-T-E-P "P" target, where no other 31 is printed; the switch matrix marks 81 Not Used, so the definition reads nothing from it.

The three Steps switches are drawn as three wire slots across the Steps track, close together near its upper end. Leaders 36, 26 and 15 end on the left ends of the top, middle and bottom slot.

Every number's read position, the controls each page was fitted on and the checks made against the definition are in `tools/seeds/williams/funhouse-1990-callouts.json`.
