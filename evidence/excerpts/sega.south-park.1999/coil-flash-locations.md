# South Park — coil and flash lamp location drawings

Read from `Sega_1999_South_Park_Manual_English.pdf`, PDF page 5 (right half), the front-matter drawing printed `DR. 7`:
"COIL LOCATIONS" (left playfield) and "FLASH LAMP LOCATIONS" (right playfield). The crop `coil-flash-locations.webp`
renders both at the scan's native resolution, rear at the top. Legend: "= Coils/Flashes mounted above playfield."
(white box) and "= Coils/Flashes mounted below the playfield." (black box). The UK-only coils 21, 22 and 23 are drawn
in grey boxes.

Coil drawing:

- 4 (black) at the top centre, behind the upper loop.
- 13, 6 and 5 (black) at the toilet, upper left: 13 above, 6 and 5 below it.
- 19, 20 and 14 (black) at Kenny, upper right: 19 and 20 either side, 14 in the middle.
- 9 and 10 (black) at the left and right bumpers, 11 at the bottom bumper.
- 3 (black) right of centre beside the right inlane guide.
- 21 and 22 (grey) at the left and right outlanes; 12 and 17 (black) at the left and right slingshots.
- 15 and 16 (black) at the left and right flippers; 23 (grey) between the flippers; 1 (black, a long box) along the
  trough and 2 (black) at the shooter lane, lower right.

Flash lamp drawing:

- 18 (white) at the top centre over the upper loop; F6 F6 (black) side by side at the top right, on the back panel.
- F7 F7 (white) at the toilet, upper left; F6 (white) on Kenny, upper right.
- F2 (black) twice: below the toilet and in the centre. F4 (black) twice: right centre and lower centre right. F3
  (black) twice: centre and lower centre. F5 (black) twice: right of centre and lower right. F1 (black) twice: left of
  centre and lower left.
- 7 (black) four times around the bumpers, and F8 (white) at the super VUK on the right.

Under the drawings: "Note: All Coils require diodes. Some diodes are located on Terminal Strips (under playfield) &
not on the coil itself."
