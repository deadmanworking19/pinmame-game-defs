Trough Dual OPTO boards, printed page 89: transmitter 520-5173-00 and receiver 520-5174-00 (assemblies 515-0173-00 and
515-0174-00). Theory of operation: with light on the receiver its output transistor Q2 is off and "acts as an OPEN
SWITCH"; when the beam is blocked it conducts and "acts as a CLOSED SWITCH".
