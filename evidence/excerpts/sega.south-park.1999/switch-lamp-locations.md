# South Park — switch and lamp location drawings

Read from `Sega_1999_South_Park_Manual_English.pdf`, PDF page 4 (right half), the front-matter drawing printed `DR. 5`:
"SWITCH MATRIX GRID LOCATIONS" (left playfield) and "LAMP MATRIX GRID LOCATIONS" (right playfield). The crop
`switch-lamp-locations.webp` renders the two playfield drawings at the scan's native resolution. Both playfields are
drawn upright, rear at the top and the flippers at the bottom. Legend under the drawings: "= Switches/Lamps mounted
above playfield." (white box) and "= Switches/Lamps mounted below the playfield." (black box).

Switch drawing, read box by box:

- Top: 46 (black) at the top left of the upper loop; 26 (white) at the rear end of the right ramp.
- Toilet area, upper left: 22, 23 and 24 (black) in a row in front of the toilet, 43 (black) under 22.
- Left side: 47 (black) in the left orbit at the far left; the left 5-bank 21, 20, 19, 18, 17 (black) top to bottom
  along the left inner guide.
- Kenny area, upper right: 42 (black) twice beside the Kenny opening; 25 (white) at the right ramp entrance; 44
  (black) below and left of the Kenny area.
- Right side: 36, 35, 34 (black) top to bottom; 48 and 45 (black) right of centre at the playfield hole and the super
  VUK.
- Bumpers: 49 and 50 (black) at the left and right bumpers, 51 at the bottom bumper; 38 and 37 (black) at the two
  bumper targets.
- Lower playfield: 59 twice and 62 twice (the slingshot stacks); 57 and 58 at the left outlane and return lane; 61 and
  60 at the right return lane and outlane.
- Trough, lower right: 14 and 15 side by side, then 13, 12, 11 and 10 stepping down toward the apron; 16 at the
  shooter lane.

Lamp drawing, read box by box:

- Top: 8 and 5 (black) right of the toilet; 46 and 48 (black) at the toilet seat; 30 (white) on Kenny; 23 and 22
  (black) at the Kenny ramp entrance.
- Centre: 7 and 6 near the top centre; 4, 3, 2, 1 down the centre; 21, 20, 19, 18, 17 (Kenny) down the centre right; 13,
  12, 11, 10, 9 (Stan) down the centre left; 29, 28, 27, 26, 25 (Kyle) down the right; 37, 36, 35, 34, 33 (Cartman) down
  the lower right; 15 and 24 at the bottom centre above the flippers.
- Left: 45, 44, 43, 42, 41 (the left 5-bank) top to bottom; 62, 61, 60, 59, 58, 57 (Mr. Hankey) beside them; 38, 39 and 40
  on the bumpers; 64 and 63 at the bumper targets; 54 and 53 at the left return lane and outlane.
- Right: 52, 51, 50 (the right 3-bank) top to bottom; 16 right of 27 in the right centre; 14 at the right, below 36 and
  37; 55 and 56 at the right return lane and outlane.
