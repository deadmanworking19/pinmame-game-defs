# High Speed — 2026-10-30 physical photo corroboration

Physical photographs of one production High Speed, used to corroborate the identity, count and order of
the Ramp Stoplight bulbs (lamps 42, 43 and 44), whose coordinates this definition measures on the
factory lamp-locations drawing. The photographs supply no coordinate: every one is a perspective view,
and the traffic light stands on a mount above the playfield.

The photographs stay external, under the working root's `review-artifacts/high-speed-1986/physical-photos/`,
pinned by that folder's canonical manifest (`manifest.json`, SHA-256
`aef01aca68c2676434a0532f01b999f768e0b77946b592a2a6c4b42b22a7fe8d`), with the page they were published on
and each file's URL in the folder's `urls.tsv`. Copyright in each photograph stays with its
photographer, and in the machine artwork with Williams Electronics; rights and license are
`NOASSERTION`. This excerpt records only what the photographs show; it does not copy any of them.

## What they show

- **One traffic light, at the upper left.** `pf1.JPG` and `pf2.JPG` show the whole playfield from the
  front. A single black box-shaped traffic light stands at the upper left, left of the upper-left jet
  bumper, with the left wireform ramp passing in front of it. The other traffic-light shapes on the
  playfield are printed insert graphics with round inserts, flat in the playfield.
- **Three lamps in one row, red, amber, green from left to right.** `upper1.JPG` (a close view of the
  upper left) and `mid2.JPG` (the upper playfield, the stoplight lit) show the box's front face, turned
  towards the player, with three round lenses side by side in one horizontal row: red on the left, amber
  in the middle, green on the right. This matches the lamp drawing (printed page 33), which prints `42`
  at the housing's left, `43` in its middle and `44` at its right, and the Lamps list, which names 42
  the Red, 43 the Yellow and 44 the Green Light (Ramp Stoplight).
- **The box stands beside the upper-left stoplight bank, not in front of it.** In both close views the
  box is left of the yellow traffic-light insert graphic whose three round inserts (red at the top) are
  lamps 17-19, with its right side clear of that graphic's left edge; in `mid2.JPG` the "Bayshore
  Freeway" spin target sign stands to its left and nearer the player. That is where the lamp and switch
  drawings draw the housing outline.
