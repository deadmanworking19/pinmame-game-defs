## Notes printed beneath the table

1. Wire colors other than flipper ORN-VIO and ORN-GRY identify the coil ground connection at the unbanded-diode terminal. Those two flipper colors run from the CPU board to the cabinet flipper switch.
2. Bracketed flipper connections run from the switch to the flipper coil.
3. The source says A circuits pulse with Sol.12 de-energized and C circuits with Sol.12 energized; parenthetical wire colors come from the corresponding A/C terminals and J1 connection on the Aux Power Driver Board.
4. It places the relay on a Relay Board: (4a) C-11998-1; (4b) C-11902-1.
5. It places a relay on Aux Power Driver Board D-12247 in the backbox.

These note descriptions preserve all technical information but paraphrase the sentences. They are visibly reused System11 A/C text beneath a WPC table; they do not establish a WPC multiplex relay or override the WPC runtime and driver evidence. The source supplies no explanation for the starred connector references. Triac function/quantity cells are genuinely blank. The Wht-Vio/Q10, Wht-Org/Q16 and Wht-Grn/Q12 pairings above are literal source readings, not corrections to the usual board wiring.
