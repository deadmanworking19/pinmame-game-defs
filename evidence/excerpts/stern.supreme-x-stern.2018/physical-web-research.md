# Supreme factory label, display, rules and research limits

Method: manual visual inspection of original retained photographs; factual paraphrase
of the complete instruction-card region. The sources remain outside Git under
`review-artifacts/stern.supreme-x-stern.2018/session-20261026/`. No image or
community table redistribution permission is implied.

## Complete factory instruction-card region: factual paraphrase

Pinside ad 124610, OCPinballs, uploaded 2021-11-17, photo 2639645:
<https://pinside.com/pinball/forum/topic/for-sale-supreme-1>.
Retained `pinside-2021-2639645.jpeg`, SHA-256
`fd6f0408c7c6baf8ece115b19ce2cc021fbc5b5e4dbf6c386ffc5a491a57ba4c`.
The original imgproxy download URL, uploader and dimensions are retained in
`pinside-market-2021.json`; photograph dimensions 1983 by 2643 pixels, card crop
rectangle `(60,2180,620,2570)`. All ten instruction bullets were read:

1. Lightning targets qualify Supreme shots.
2. Making Supreme shots advances their scoring.
3. Spinner hits grow Meteor Shower scoring.
4. Completing the three top-lane stars starts timed double scoring.
5. Completing all seven Lucky Stars advances the bonus multiplier.
6. Completing any two Supreme shots lights extra ball.
7. Pop-bumper hits grow Supreme values and move the Supreme shots.
8. Freeing the trapped ball starts Galaxy multiball.
9. The target above the trapped ball adds a ball during multiball.
10. Ramp sequences and other successive shot combinations award additional points.

The photographed factory card does not state electrical wiring, contact polarity,
GI quantities, a gate-coil direction, a double-scoring duration or a complete
mechanism inventory. None is supplied from those omissions.

## Tutorial lead, explicitly separate from verified photographic facts

Colorado Pinball Collective, *Supreme pinball with Dean Grover*:
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47VnHAlFke8>.
Retained browser ASR transcript `programmer-segments.json`, SHA-256
`cc22d8ff5ff2b720fb375edb6345911a33569b0f1cc75256c12cc305c3144798`.
The video's description identifies the game's programmer. Captions are an
unverified transcription lead; audio and speaker attribution were not checked.
At 2:53-3:49 they describe passive ball capture, replacement serve, the
drop-bank/sign multiball and an escape path because the bank cannot lower itself
electrically. At 4:02-4:26 they describe a 60-second final multiball after all
four Supreme shots. At 12:31-12:46 they describe an open-sided capture with a
second ball that tends to roll away. At 49:08-49:25 they describe linked slings.
These leads do not validate gate direction, wiring or physical contact polarity.

## Search scope and unresolved evidence

The retained research covers both Supreme club pages, all 15 launch-thread
pages, Pinside production ads 124610 and 192007, IPDB 6609 and its photos,
the current Stern manuals index, and focused web/manual searches. Direct Pinside
forum searches for `supreme manual`, `supreme schematic`, `supreme wiring`,
`supreme gate`, and `supreme opto` produced no relevant technical documentation;
the complete result captures are retained in `pinside-specific-searches.json`.
No Supreme factory manual or wiring pages were found. This is a report of the
searched sources, not a claim that no such document exists anywhere.

The VPX's control-gate coil-on direction is explicitly guessed. Its GI and insert
lights are render/lightmap objects rather than observed sockets. Cabinet
coin/ticket fitment, complete trough/jam hardware, physical contact states, gate
default/action and complete emitter counts/positions remain unresolved. A
Supreme factory wiring/parts set or direct inspection is needed to settle them;
another title's manual, absent VPX handlers or ROM activity cannot settle fitment.
