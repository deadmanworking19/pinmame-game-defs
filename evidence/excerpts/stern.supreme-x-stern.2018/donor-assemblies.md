The donor's cabinet access/construction differs from Supreme's photographed
coin-door/manual-plunger cabinet. Equivalence of the listed trough and launch
assemblies has not been established. The donor's supplied
four balls do not establish which numbered trough positions are occupied. Its
board quantities do not count Supreme emitters, and the list does not establish
a dedicated electrical output for every listed physical assembly.
