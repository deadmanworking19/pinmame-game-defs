## Location drawing legend (PDF 31, complete)

Heading dagger: "The switch locations correspond with the Switch N° in the table below and the Switch Maxtrix Grid." (spelling as printed). Legend: an open box = "Switches located above playfield"; a filled box = "Switches located below playfield". "The following switches are located in the cabinet and are not noted in the diagram above: 1 2 4 5 6 7". "The following switches are not used: 9 10 17 31". Drawn outside the playfield outline: 3 and 8 below the left front, 49 below the right front, 63 at the left edge and 64 at the right edge, both level with the apron. Open boxes (above playfield): 30, 44, 45, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56, 60, 61, 62. Every other drawn number is a filled box (below playfield): 11-16, 18-29, 32-41, 42, 43, 46-48, 50, 51, 57-59, plus 3, 8, 49, 63 and 64 outside the outline. The trough runs diagonally under the right apron, 11 at its lower left rising to 14, with 15 and 16 at its upper right beside the shooter lane; two filled boxes printed `X` continue that line below and left of 11. The parts list prints `(X)` before NOT USED on rows 9 and 10; the drawing gives no number in either box.

## Typical switch schematic (PDF 30)

"(Column: Switch Drive Wire) GRN-XXX" to the N.O. terminal; "(Row: Switch Return Wire) WHT-XXX" through a blocking diode 1N4001 to COM.; N.C. labelled separately. The side view shows WHT and GRN on the N.C./N.O./COM. lugs with "Diode 1N4001".

## Trough up-kicker opto (PDF 111, printed 103)

"As light from the Transmitter falls on the Receiver LED, it generates a Positive Bias Voltage (0.7v to 1.5v) which is applied to the gate of Q1, turning Q1 off. When Q1 is held off, no current flows through Q2's Base, the transistor is off acting as an OPEN SWITCH. When the light is interrupted (BLOCKED) R1 bleeds the gate voltage off of Q1 allowing it to conduct, switching Q2 on, which acts as a CLOSED SWITCH." Receiver 520-5125-00: pin 1 SWITCH ROW / RETURN WHT/XXX WIRE, pin 2 SWITCH COL / DRIVE GRN/XXX WIRE. Transmitter 520-5124-00. Troubleshooting: "OPEN OPTO (Light Falling on LED) = SWITCH OPEN"; "CLOSED OPTO (Light Blocked) = SWITCH CLOSED".
