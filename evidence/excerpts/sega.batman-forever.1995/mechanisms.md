## Rules text bearing on mechanisms (PDF 15-19, printed 9-13)

- Gotham Events (PDF 15): "Collect flashing Gotham Event in "?" logo by shooting Upper Left Eject Saucer when "GOTHAM EVENT" is lit." Change Gotham Event: "Hitting the Turbo Bumpers change the lit Gotham Event. Pull the Gun Trigger to lock/unlock the currently lit Gotham Event."
- Forever Mode (PDF 17): "When FOREVER starts, all balls will be kicked into play and the Batwing Cannon will begin to oscillate." "Players must shoot the Left Ramp and catch a Ball in the Batwing Cannon AS IT PASSES THROUGH THE HOME POSITION to collect the current value of the FOREVER JACKPOT."
- Skill Shot (PDF 17): "Pull the Gun Trigger to select the award & launch the ball into play."
- Batwing Cannon (PDF 17): "Completing the required number of shots through the U-Turn, Middle, and Right Ramps will lite ARM BATWING on Left Ramp. Shoot the Left Ramp to load the ball into the Batwing Cannon. Pull the Gun Trigger to shoot the ball at the flashing targets."
- Multiball Ready (PDF 18): "Complete the 4-Bank Drop Targets (C • A • V • E) during regulation play to lite locks at the VUK. Shoot the VUK to lock balls in the BATCAVE. Lock 3 balls to start Multiball. Lock the 3rd ball from a Cannon shot to start Multiball with increased base Jackpots and play 4-Ball Multiball."
- Super Jackpot (PDF 19): "While keeping at least two balls in play, load the Batwing Cannon by shooting the Left Ramp. Shoot the ball from the Cannon through the U-Turn Mini-Ramp to score the 250M point SUPER JACKPOT."

## Playfield Coil / Flashlamp Wiring Diagram (PDF 108, printed 100)

The coil row, read rotated: LASER KICK 23-800 Q13CPU Q1PPB 22; BATCAVE EXIT KICKER 24-940 Q23CPU Q2PPB 16; BATWING CANNON 23-800 Q24CPU Q3PPB 15; KNOCKER 23-800 Q39CPU 8L; DROP TARGET 23-700 Q40CPU 7L; "NO COIL AT THIS LOCATION Q41CPU 6L" with no wire drawn to it; TOP EJECT 24-940 Q42CPU 5L; VUK 24-940 Q43CPU Q4PPB 4L; AUTO LAUNCH 23-800 Q44CPU Q5PPB 3L; TROUGH UP KICKER 23-800 Q45CPU 2L; 4 BALL LOCK OUT 25-1240 Q46CPU 1L. Relay note: "LEFT/RIGHT RELAY NORMALLY CLOSED SIDE ="N.C." ="L"LEFT ="L" SIDE NORMALLY OPEN SIDE ="N.O." ="R"RIGHT ="R" SIDE N.U. MEANS "NOT USED"".

The CPU CN 12 block draws only three loads, each fed "RED" from "+32 VDC PS CN3-7/6": pin 1 Q30 "BATCAVE BALL LOCK 24-900" BRN/BLK (9); pin 5 Q27 "RAMP DIVERTER 27-1500" BRN/YEL (12); pin 7 Q25 BRN/BLU to the coil (14) of a relay on a board labelled "RELAY BOARD" (its part number is printed too small to read in this scan) whose N.O. contact switches WHT/RED "FROM BR2 28 VAC" through the motor (WHT). The N.C. contact is marked N.U. No load is drawn on CN12-6 (Q26, driver 13).

## Solid State 3-Flipper Board (520-5076-00) Wiring Diagram (PDF 115, printed 107)

SSFB CN1, pin: function (wire): 12 UPPER FLIPPER ENABLE (GRY/VIO); 11 LEFT FLIPPER ENABLE (BLU/GRY); 10 NOT USED (N.C.); 09 LEFT FLIPPER EOS (BRN/GRY); 08 +5v DC (GRY/WHT); 07 RIGHT FLIPPER ENABLE (BLU/VIO); 06 GROUND (GND) (BLK); 05 SWITCH RETURN 7 (WHT/VIO); 04 SWITCH DRIVE 8 (GRN/GRY); 03 SWITCH RETURN 8 (WHT/GRY); 02 KEY; 01 RIGHT FLIPPER EOS (BRN/VIO). SSFB CN2: 01-02 UPPER FLIPPER POWER (BLK/YEL); 03 NOT USED; 04-05 LEFT FLIPPER POWER (GRY/YEL); 06 KEY; 07-08 RIGHT FLIPPER POWER (BLU/YEL); 09-10 +8v AC (GRY, GRY/GRN); 11-12 +50v DC (BLK/WHT). The FLIPPER CABINET SWITCHES are three contacts marked N.O.; the two EOS switches are marked N.C. The coils: "UPPER FLIPPER RIGHT 23-900", "RIGHT FLIPPER LOWER 22-900", "LEFT FLIPPER LOWER 22-1080". The CPU board side shows relay RY-1 (DC6V) switching the flipper grounds to CN19-1/2 ("FLIPPER GNDS", ORG/VIO and ORG/GRY) under "(1-12G)EN.BLNR". No CPU solenoid driver connects to the SSFB.

The Flipper Coils table (coils.md) prints `SSFB CN1-10` for both the lower-left and the upper-right cabinet switches; this diagram puts the left enable (BLU/GRY) on CN1-11, the upper enable (GRY/VIO) on CN1-12 and marks CN1-10 NOT USED.
