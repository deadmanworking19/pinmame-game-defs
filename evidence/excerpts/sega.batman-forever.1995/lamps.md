## Lamp Matrix Location drawing (PDF 37, complete legend)

"The lamp locations correspond with the Lamp Number in the Lamp Maxtrix Grid on the previous page." (spelling as printed). Legend: open box = "Lamps located above playfield"; filled box = "Lamps located below playfield". "The following Lamps are not used: 36 37 38 60". "The following Bulb is used over the ramps & in the Pop Bumpers: #555 Bulb (Wedge) 165-5002-00". "The following Bulb is used in the remainder of the matrix: #44 Bulb (Bayonet) 165-5000-44". Drawing footer: "RAMPS, BATCAVE & BATWING CANNON ARE NOT SHOWN FOR CLARITY."

Open boxes in the drawing: 33, 34, 35, 49, 50, 52, 53, 61. 57, 58 and 59 are printed inside the three turbo bumper caps. 63 and 64 are printed outside the outline below the apron. Every other used number is a filled symbol on the playfield.

The legend's two bulb sentences (#555 over ramps and in pop bumpers, #44 elsewhere) do not agree with the grid's per-cell bulb column, which prints #555 for most playfield inserts (for example 1-6 and 18-21) and #44 for 35, 49, 52, 53 and 61, which the drawing shows above the playfield.
