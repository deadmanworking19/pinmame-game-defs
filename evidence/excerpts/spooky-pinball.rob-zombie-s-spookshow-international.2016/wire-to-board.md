# RZ wire-to-board chart (wiring-board-chart.png)

Transcribed by a Sonnet 5.5 worker from the chart's text layer (the RZ chart from its raster), every pin aligned by its tick; the Opus 5.5 curator spot-checked the coil banks, the cabinet header and the GI_1 header (at 300 dpi) against the render, not every pin. Uncertain readings are listed at the end.

Pins are listed in tick order as drawn on the chart (connector header lines run left to right as printed, vertical headers top to bottom). '(blank)' means no wire text is printed at that tick; '(unlabeled tick)' means a tick with no pin label.

## Uncertain readings

- Source is a 1160 x 877 raster, not a vector PDF; all small text was read from 3-4x enlargements. Wire-colour words are legible, but pin alignment of the dense GI_1 and Sol Bank texts was checked only by tick x position.
- Typos kept verbatim: 'whtie/red' (RGB Com Out 5v), 'Exit Reciever' (OPTOS box).
- RGB Com Out: RGB CLK carries 'white/green' and RGB DAT carries 'white/blue' here, the opposite of the Domino's and Jetsons charts (CLK white/blue, DAT white/green). Read from the enlarged crop; the column lengths ('white/green' is the longer text) are consistent with this.
- Cabinet header: this chart prints 'key' on the 11th line and puts '5V' with 'gray/red' beside the GI_0 header instead of printing '5V - gray/red' in the Cabinet list (Domino's/Jetsons list it in the Cabinet block, AMH too). It is unclear whether the cabinet header genuinely lacks a 5V pin or the 5V line moved.
- GI_1: text columns sit under ticks 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, '.' (key) and 5V (brown); tick 8 and the unlabeled 10th tick carry nothing.
- Switch Column pins 7 and 6 have no wire colour printed; Light Column pins 6 and 7 have none; transcribed as blank.
- Leading unlabeled tick on Switch Row, Switch Column, Light Row and Light Column, an extra unlabeled tick after 5V on GI_1, and two unlabeled ticks after the 'key' tick on Cabinet RGB: counted from the artwork.
- Sol Bank 0 coils 0 and 1 ('(option)'), Bank 1 coils 14-15 and Bank 2 coils 22-23 have no wire colour and no name; transcribed as blank.
- Fuse leader lines (F1->Sol Bank 0, F2->Sol Bank 1, F3->Sol Bank 2) read from the drawn lines; not stated in text.
- Switch matrix: COLUMN 7 and COLUMN 6 carry no wire colour in the header. Cell 32 'RAMP TARGET' and 40 'RAMP' sit in COLUMN 4 / COLUMN 5 respectively. Cabinet cell 13 'Upper PF Opto Spaulding' and 14 'Upper PF Opto (exit)'.
- Lamp matrix: 'Skil Shot 3' (lamp 32) is a misspelling printed on the chart; lamp 17 'Gein'; lamp 41 'Ramp Arrow'; letters/digits cells 24 'C', 25 'H', 27 '1', 42 '2', 13 '3', 14 'O', 15 'P' are printed alone. Lamp 8 reads 'What' (no punctuation).
