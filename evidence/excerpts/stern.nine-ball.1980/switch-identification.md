Source: `Stern_1980_Nine_Ball_Manual.pdf` (IPDB machine 1678, "Manual", 23 pages, each a 72 dpi colour JPEG scan with no text layer), PDF page 18 (printed page 17), stored sideways. Read rotated upright from a three-times enlargement; every cell below was checked against it.

Heading, verbatim, two lines: `SWITCH I.D.`, `NINE BALL`. Legend at the upper left: a `SWITCH SYMBOL` (a plain contact); a contact drawn with a series diode and the label `SWITCH NO.` `15`; the note `SWS. #1, 2, & 3 NO DIODE ALL OTHER SWITCHES USE DIODE IN4004`; and a contact drawn with a diode and a capacitor across it, captioned `SYMBOL IF .05 CAP IS USED`, whose mark in the table is a filled dot on the row's line (on rows 4, 17 and 35).

The strobe lines are drawn as vertical buses at the left of each block, each labelled with its wire colour and MPU jack pin: `R-Y (J3-3)` joins rows 1, 2, 3, 6, 7 and 8; `W-R (J2-1)` joins rows 4 and 5; `BRN-W (J2-2)` rows 9-16; `W-BLU (J2-3)` rows 17-24; `W-Y (J2-4)` rows 25-32; `Y-R (J2-5)` rows 33-40.
