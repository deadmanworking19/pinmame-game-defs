Source: `Stern_1980_Nine_Ball_Schematic_Diagrams_paginated.pdf` (IPDB machine 1678), PDF page 2, `NINE BALL PLAYFIELD`, middle part. The coil wiring is reproduced in `playfield-coil-wiring.webp`; every label was read from enlargements of the page at its native resolution.

Each coil is drawn with a diode across it. Coil labels sit beside the coil they name; the first coil of each group is labelled at its left, the others at their right.

Flippers: a bus `A2J1-6` `(BLU-W)` feeds the `LEFT FLIPPER` and `RIGHT FLIPPER`, each drawn as two windings with a contact across one of them. The left flipper's return is `A3J1-8` `(G)`, the right flipper's `A3J1-9` `(O)`. At the right the `BLU-W` line runs through a fuse symbol marked `1/4 AMP SLO-BLO` to the vertical line marked `(Y)` that feeds the coil groups below.
