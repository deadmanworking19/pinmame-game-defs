Source: `Stern_1980_Nine_Ball_Schematic_Diagrams_paginated.pdf` (IPDB machine 1678), PDF page 2, the hand-lettered sheet titled `NINE BALL PLAYFIELD`, left part. The matrix is reproduced in `playfield-switch-matrix.webp`; every label was read from enlargements of the page at its native resolution.

Strobe columns, left to right, headed `ST0` `A`, `ST1` `B`, `ST2` `C`, `ST3` `D` (the letter is drawn as a reversed C), `ST4` `E`; a mark `#1` follows the `A`. Return rows `I0`-`I7`, each fed from the left by a labelled MPU wire. The strobe wires leave the matrix at the bottom. Every drawn contact has a series diode; three contacts also carry the capacitor symbol (a small capacitor drawn below the contact).

Return wires, verbatim: `I0` `A4J2-8 (BRN)`, `I1` `A4J2-9 (GREY)`, `I2` `A4J2-10 (W-O)`, `I3` `A4J2-11 (W-B)`, `I4` `A4J2-12 (W-G)`, `I5` `A4J2-13 (W-BRN)`, `I6` `A4J2-14 (BRN-Y)`, `I7` `A4J2-15 (O)`.

Strobe wires, verbatim, at the bottom: `A4J2-1 (W-R)` to ST0, `A4J2-2 (BRN-W)` to ST1, `A4J2-3 (W-BLU)` to ST2, `A4J2-4 (W-Y)` to ST3, `A4J2-5 (Y-R)` to ST4.

Below the matrix, verbatim: a lamp symbol `GEN. ILLUM.` between `A2J1-8 (W)` `6 VAC` and `A2J1-1 (R)` `RETURN`. Legend at the lower left: `D.T. = DROP TARGET`, `K.O.H. = KICK OUT HOLE`.

The six unlabelled ST0 contacts sit at the matrix positions the Switch I.D. page assigns to the coin chutes, the credit button, tilt and slam (1, 2, 3, 6, 7 and 8), which that page wires to MPU connector J3; this sheet labels none of them.
