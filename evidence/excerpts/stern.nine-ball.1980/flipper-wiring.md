Source: `Stern_1980_Nine_Ball_Manual.pdf` (IPDB machine 1678), PDF page 22 (printed page 21), lower part. The drawing is reproduced in `flipper-wiring.webp`; its labels were read from a three-times enlargement and are transcribed here. The MPU-200 jumper text in the upper part of the same page is general board information and is not transcribed.

Heading, verbatim: `FLIPPER WIRING`, with the column captions `LEFT FLIPPER` and `RT. FLIPPER`.

Supply: fuse `F4` from `RECT. BRD.` `J1-6` `+43 VDC`, on a `BLU-W` bus across the top to both flippers.

Left flipper: a coil assembly drawn as two windings in series, each with a diode across it labelled `IN 4004 DIODE`; a contact labelled `E.O.S.` is drawn across the first winding. Its return lead is marked `-G-` and runs to `SDU J1-8`, through a contact of the `SDU RELAY`, out on `SDU J2-2`, then `-BLU-` to the `LT. BUTTON`.

Right flipper: the same coil drawing with its contact labelled `E.O.S.` `A`. Its return lead is marked `-O-` and runs to `SDU J1-9`, through the second `SDU RELAY` contact, out on `SDU J2-1`, then `-R-` to the `RT. BUTTON`.

The two buttons share a common marked `-O-` to `RECT. BRD.` `J2-9`, drawn to ground.

Drawn in dashed lines to the right: an `EXTRA FLIPPER` with a resistor across its first winding, an `E.O.S.` `B` contact on a lead marked `BRN-W`, joined to the right flipper's return. The note beside it, verbatim: "Dashed Lines: Sometimes when more than 1 Flipper is used on one side, a 1-OHM 5-WATT Resistor is Used to Reduce Power."

The solid lines draw one flipper per side; the dashed extra flipper is a generic option on this shared drawing, and the parts list (PDF page 16) counts `Flippers (2)`.
