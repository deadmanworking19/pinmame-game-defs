Note, verbatim:

> Note: Each Ball Eater has two switches. The center is #41 & #42 and tells the CPU if a ball is in the Ball
> Eater. The switch to the back and side is #33 & #34 and tells the CPU if the Ball Eater is up or down. The
> Motorized Target also has two switches. The right side switch tells the CPU the target is down, the left side
> switch tells the CPU the target is up.
