# Mousin' Around! — Solenoid Table (printed page "Solenoid 2-30")

Source: `Bally_1989_Mousin_Around_Manual.pdf` (SHA-256
`6dcd83d1485a1b2e9909cc65b07d9ce23e1e7757e87cdf4a002984465b02d9f0`), PDF page 84, footer `Solenoid 2-30`. Read
from the page rendered at 300 dpi and enlarged in three horizontal strips. The page prints the title
`MOUSIN' AROUND Solenoid Table`, one table and its notes. Every row and every column is transcribed.

Column headings, as printed: `Sol. No.` | `Function` | `Solenoid Type` | `Wire Color` | `Connections` split
into `CPU Bd` and `Playfield/ Cabinet` | `Driver Trnstr` | `Solenoid Part Number / Flashlamp Type / d= Display Bd;
p=Playfield`. The last column prints the flashlamp quantity at its right edge (`2p`, `1p`, `1b`); the
heading's legend names `d` and `p` only, and the `b` of `04C` is printed without a legend entry.

How the switched pairs are printed: each `A` row and its `C` row share one driver transistor. The `Wire
Color` column brackets the two rows' wires together with a `)` drawn after the `A` wire. The `CPU Bd` column
prints the pin once, on the `A` row, and a bracketed wire on the `C` row; those brackets are transcribed
exactly, including the ones the scan prints without a closing bracket. Every `Sol. No.` of `01A` to `08C`
carries a superscript `3`, the note number. The note numbers `4a`, `4a`, `4b` and `5` of rows 09-12 are
printed at the right of the part-number column, set slightly above their rows; they are given here on the
row they align with in sequence.
