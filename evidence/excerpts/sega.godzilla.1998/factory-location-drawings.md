# Factory lamp and flasher location drawings

Source: Godzilla manual, PDF pages 7 and 9, printed DR. 5 and DR. 7; SHA-256
`fc71e6584b2a8ad715100f94b5d4d690b0cd3864cd9abd6746c2441ad4d6c1c5`.
Method: primary curator visually read full native-resolution renders and
independent bumper/return-lane controls. The curator checked the drawings and keeps
the source pixel reads and control fit in `tools/data/godzilla/spatial.json`.
The crops are compressed reading aids; measurements were made on the original
1275×1650 renders. Drawing measurements remain observed, with extrapolations
disclosed; they do not independently validate themselves.
