- The three captive balls occupy separate lanes. Striking a left or right
  captive ball knocks the top center ball between its posts; reinstallation
  requires one captive ball in each lane.
- A hard left-orbit shot travels through the Orbit Ramp to the right return.
  A soft Godzilla-ramp shot exits the top hole to the pops. A hard ramp shot
  exits to the left return with the diverter up and through Godzilla's mouth
  with it down.
- Four-ball trough assembly 500-6318-24 has three roller microswitches,
  180-5119-00, a 26-1200 VUK coil, and dual transmitter 520-5173-00 and
  receiver 520-5174-00 boards; the full parts table is in `trough-parts.md`.
  The opto theory describes both receiver channels as open with light present
  and closed with the beam blocked.
- Four magnet assemblies use threaded bushing core 515-6142-01 and coil 22-650,
  090-5042-01. The cabinet-mounted shaker is identified in the coil and
  location charts. Timing in the VPW implementation is table tuning rather than
  measured physical behavior.
