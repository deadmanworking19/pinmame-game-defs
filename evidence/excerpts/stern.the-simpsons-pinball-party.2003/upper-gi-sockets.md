Source: Stern The Simpsons Pinball Party manual, PDF112, printed94, underside
view of the upper playfield. This drawing shows seven small bayonet socket
assemblies: three along the left edge, two at middle-left, one at rear-middle,
and one at rear-right. The six larger sockets on insert openings are matrix
lamps65–70 and are excluded. Screw heads are not emitter centers.
