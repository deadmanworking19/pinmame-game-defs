Source: Stern The Simpsons Pinball Party manual, PDF37, lamp-location drawing.

sign. These are two emitters on one matrix binding, consistent with the lamp
matrix's printed quantity of two. The measured points are leader endpoints,
not the remote numbered balloons or observed filament centers.
