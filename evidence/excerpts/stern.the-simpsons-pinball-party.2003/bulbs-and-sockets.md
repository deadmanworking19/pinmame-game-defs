# Bulb and socket parts tables

Source: Stern The Simpsons Pinball Party manual, PDF90 (printed72) and PDF92
(printed74). Full printed table regions transcribed visually. `blank` means
the quantity cell is genuinely empty; colored-bulb rows repeat the printed
letter. Capitalization and separators are normalized for Markdown. The
adjacent drawings and general notes remain in the retained manual.
