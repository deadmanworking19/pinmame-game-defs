# Frontier - vector drawing for the eject saucer

Source: `Bally_1980_Frontier_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 37 (the last page, unnumbered), a full playfield line drawing with a typed caption beside it. The crop beside this file is the upper half of the drawing with the caption; the lower half repeats the playfield outline and carries no marking.

Caption, verbatim:

> VECTOR DRAWING FOR FRONTIER
> EJECT SAUCER
>
> The ball should pass thru gate as shown.

What the drawing marks, read from the native render: in the upper-right lane, the saucer sits at the lane's lower end; a straight line drawn from the saucer runs up the lane, past a star rollover drawn as a spoked circle just right of the line, and through the gate assembly at the top of the lane into the arch. Both arrowheads on the line, one halfway up and one at its end in the arch, point away from the saucer. A separate short arrow above and left of the gate points down at it from the arch. Three further arrows point at the left, center and right stand-up targets; they mark the same targets Figure V marks with arrows and carry no caption here.

The drawing is an adjustment aid for the saucer's kick direction. It places the star rollover between the saucer and the gate, which is where Figure V prints switch 25 (SAUCER ROLLOVER BUTTON) and solenoid 11 (GATE).
