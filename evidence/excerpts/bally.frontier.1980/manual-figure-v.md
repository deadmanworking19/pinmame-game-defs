Source: `Bally_1980_Frontier_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 22 (printed page 18), captioned "FIGURE V". The crop beside this file is the playfield drawing; the legend printed to the right of it is transcribed below rather than cropped.

The numbers are the page-17 identification numbers (switch self-test numbers in circles, solenoid self-test numbers in squares), not public controller addresses. No number has a leader line; a few carry a short arrow, and each is printed beside or inside the part it names.
