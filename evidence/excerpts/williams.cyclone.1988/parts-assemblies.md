`p-13.png`, `p-14.png`, `p-15.png` and `p-17.png` (200 dpi). The printed folio `CYCLONE n` is PDF page n + 2.
Each listing below is transcribed whole; the exploded drawings beside them are not, apart from the callout named in
the Kicker Arm section. Indented part numbers in the scans (sub-parts of the line above) are written with a leading
`  `. Neighbouring listings on the same pages (the 11-Lamp, insert, interconnect and ramp boards, the standup targets,
the jet bumper above the playfield, the drop target and the outhole kicker) are not transcribed.

## Reading against the operations manual

- Both slingshot kickers are B-9463 assemblies with the AE-26-1500 coil, and the jet bumper coil assembly carries
  the AE-23-800 coil. That agrees with the operations manual's Solenoids/Flashers list (18 and 20 AE-26-1500; 17, 19
  and 21 AE-23-800) and against its Solenoid Table, which prints 20 (Right Kicker) as AE-23-800 and 21 (Bottom Jet
  Bumper) as AE-26-1500.
- The boomerang kickbig's coil is AE-26-1500 and the shooter-lane kickbig's AE-24-900, as both operations-manual
  lists print for 4 and 3.
- The operations manual's Switches list footnotes the Mystery Wheel Opto (5490-10159-00) as `Part of Motor Control
  Assy, D-12046`; this manual lists the 5490-10159-00 `Opto Interruptor Module` (`Opto 1`) on the D-12045 Mystery Wheel
  Opto Positioner board, which the Carousel Assembly lists as `Control Assy, Stepper Motor`, and the Solenoid Table
  prints `14-7948/D-12045` for the stepper outputs 13 and 14.
