Playfield  b = Backglass`), `Solenoid Type`, `Wire 1 Color` (superscript 1), `Connections` over `CPU Bd.` and
`Playfield/ Cabinet`, `Driver Trans.`, `Solenoid Part Number Flashlamp Type` (sub-heading `b = Backbox  p =
Playfield`). Superscripts are written `^n`. `[blank]` is a visibly empty cell. Rows 01A-08C print the A and C rows of
one drive as a braced pair: the wire colours are braced together, the CPU cell of the C row prints a second wire
colour in parentheses, and the playfield cell spans both rows (`8P3-1 (via 5J1-9,` / `Aux Pwr Drvr Bd.)`). The
flashlamp quantity is printed at the right edge of the part cell.

Notes, verbatim: `Notes: 1. Wire colors, except flipper Orn-Vio and Orn-Gry, are ground connections (to coil terminal
with unbanded end of diode). Flipper Orn-Vio and Orn-Gry wires connect from CPU Board to flipper switch. 2. Flipper
connections shown in braces are from flipper switch to flipper coil. 3. "A" coils are pulsed, when Sol. 12 is
de-energized; "B" coils are pulsed, with Sol. 12 energized. Wire colors in brackets are those from respective A and B
terminals corresponding to the J1- terminal connection listed for the Aux Power Driver Bd., which controls the device
pulsing by Sol. 12. 4. Relay is mounted on Relay Bd. p/n C-11998-1. 5. Relay is mounted on Aux Pwr Driver Bd.
D-11813 in the backbox. 6. Relay is mounted on Relay Bd C-11902-1.`

Note 3 says `"B" coils` where the table's rows are labelled C; the A/C relay note on PDF page 33 (excerpt
`operation-and-diagnostics`) uses A and C.

PDF page 32 (printed CYCLONE 28) reprints this table under the Solenoid Test; compared through its OCR text it shows
the same rows, wires, pins, transistors and parts.
