Together with the instruction card (item 2, "Spotting B-I-G G-A-M-E lites letters in eject pocket lane"), the photographs show every letter twice: once at its own lane or target and once in the eject-pocket lane's column. Each letter has one row in the lamp lists, so the two inserts of a letter share one lamp-driver output.
