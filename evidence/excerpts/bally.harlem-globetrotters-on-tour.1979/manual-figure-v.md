# Harlem Globetrotters On Tour - Figure V, switch and solenoid locations

Source: `Bally_1979_Harlem_Globetrotters_On_Tour_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 23 (printed page 18), "#1161-E HARLEM GLOBETROTTERS", captioned "FIGURE V". The crop beside this file is the playfield drawing and its legend at the scan's native resolution; the text the page prints beside the drawing bleeds through from the reverse page and is not part of the figure.

Legend, verbatim:

> ◯ INDICATES SWITCH ASSEMBLY IDENTIFICATION NUMBERS.
> NOTE: CABINET: 07, 16
> DOOR: 06, 09, 10, 11, 16
>
> ▢ INDICATES SOLENOID IDENTIFICATION NUMBERS.
> NOTE: DOOR: 11
> BACKBOX: 12
> CABINET: 02

The numbers are the page-17 identification numbers (switch self-test numbers in circles, solenoid self-test numbers in squares), not public controller addresses. No number on the drawing has a leader line; each is printed beside or inside the part it names.

It draws three flippers: the two lower flippers and a third, upper-left flipper below the left slingshot, above the lower left flipper; on the right a long guide rail runs where a matching flipper would be.
