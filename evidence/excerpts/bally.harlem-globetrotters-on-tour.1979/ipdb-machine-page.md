# Harlem Globetrotters On Tour - IPDB machine page 1125

Quoted from the retained copy of the IPDB machine page for IPD No. 1125 (`ipdb1125.html`, Wayback capture `20240214190623` of `https://www.ipdb.org/machine.cgi?id=1125`), read from the page's HTML text. Only the identity, hardware, feature and note fields are quoted; ratings, the photo list and the site footer are not.

> Harlem Globetrotters On Tour / IPD No. 1125 / September, 1979 / 4 Players
>
> Manufacturer: Bally Manufacturing Corporation (1931-1983) [Trade Name: Bally ]
> Project Date: August 28, 1978
> Date Of Manufacture: September, 1979
> Model Number: 1161-E
> MPU: Bally MPU AS-2518-35
> Type: Solid State Electronic (SS)
> Production: 14,550 units ( confirmed )
> Theme: Sports - Basketball - Licensed Theme
> Notable Features: Flippers (3), Pop bumpers (3), Slingshots (2), Standup targets (6), Kick-out holes (2), Spinning targets (3), 4-in-line drop targets , Right outlane detour gate . One of the standup targets is recessed.
> Design by: Greg Kmiec
> Art by: Greg Freres
> Notes: On the game in the manufacturer's flyer, the four inserts 2X-3X-5X-Special are green, white, white, and green, respectively. Most production games are green, yellow, white, and red. Pictured in this listing is one game from the factory having green, yellow, red, and white, where the 5X and Special insert colors are reversed.
> Also pictured is an example where the G-L-O-B-E inserts are white, not yellow.

> Documentation: 7 MB PDF English Manual [Bally Mfg. Corp.]
> 1 MB PDF German Manual [Bally Mfg. Corp.]

The English manual is the document this record cites; the German manual was not retained. The page names the MPU "AS-2518-35", where the manual's parts list (`manual-self-test-parts-and-assembly.md`) prints "MPU A4 AS-2962-10".
