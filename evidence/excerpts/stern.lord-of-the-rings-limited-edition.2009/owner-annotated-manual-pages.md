# The owner's annotated manual pages 100-106

`Stern-2009-LOTR-LE-Missing-Pages-100-to-106.pdf`, published by the same LE owner as `Pages 100-106 which are missing from Stern LOTR LE Manual 2009`: https://firepowerpinball.com/2ya/lotr/Stern-2009-LOTR-LE-Missing-Pages-100-to-106.pdf (SHA-256 `3745b609874e0f48994101a57067a8a78c0bb62c49cc74338c534c26926c5d74`). Each of its seven pages is a scan of the Lord of the Rings manual's Section 5 pages 100-106 with the owner's corrections laid over it as vector text and boxes.

The scans are the 2003 manual's pages, not Limited Edition ones: page 100 matches the coils chart of Stern's current reissue of the 2003 manual (`Lord_of_the_Rings_Manual-2022.pdf`, PDF 118) cell for cell, still printing #14 FLASH: HELMS DEEP RT and #28 NOT USED, and page 101 is titled `Partial View I/O Power Driver Board 520-5137-01`, the 2003 board. So everything the scans print describes the 2003 machine, and everything the owner changed describes his Limited Edition. His page says these are his own game's wiring and that Stern may have used different wire colours on other LEs.

Below is every overlay, page by page: where it sits, what the scan prints there, and what the overlay prints. The overlay texts were read from the PDF's text layer and each placement checked on the page rendered at 150-220 dpi; boxes, arrows and other drawn marks were read from the rendering. Where an overlay hides the printed value completely, the value given under it is the one the same page of Stern's 2022 reissue prints in that place.
