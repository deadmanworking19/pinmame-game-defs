# Literal transcription — Switch, Solenoid and Lamp Locations parts lists

`reviewed: true`; transcribed by the curator from 300 dpi renders and visually checked against them on 2026-10-30
`source: Instruction Manual PDF page 19 (Switch Location Diagram), PDF page 4 (Solenoid Location Diagram), PDF page 20 (Lamp Location Diagram)`

Retained renders: `external:pinmame-manuals/by-machine/williams.space-shuttle.1984/rendered-300/p-19.png`, `p-04.png`, `p-20.png` (pdftoppm 23.05.0 -r 300 -gray).

Each page is a playfield drawing with numbered balloons beside a three-column list. The lists are transcribed
in full under their literal column headings; `--` is a printed dash, `"` a printed ditto mark (it repeats the
nearest assembly printed above it) and `(blank)` an empty printed cell.
