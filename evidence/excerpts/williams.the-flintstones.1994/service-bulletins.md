# Williams The Flintstones (1994) — Service bulletins

Two one-page Williams service documents for *The Flintstones*, hosted by IPDB (machine 888). Both PDFs
are scans without a text layer; each was read from its page render. Text is transcribed as printed,
including its spelling.

## Ball Hang-up Correction Instructions, 16-9872, August 4, 1994

File `Williams_1994_The_Flintstones_Ball_Hang_up_Correction_Instructions_August_4_1994.pdf`.

Heading: `FLINTSTONES` / `BALL HANG-UP CORRECTION INSTRUCTIONS`; top right `16-9872` / `August 4, 1994`.

> To eliminate the ball hang-ups occurring near the bottom arch, remove the washers located on the top and
> bottom of the posts on the left and right sides of the bottom arch. These posts are under the clear
> portion of the bottom arch and above a purple triangle plastic piece.

1. Be sure the game is turned off and unplugged.
2. Open the coin door, slide the lever guide to the left and remove the front molding. Carefully, slide
   the playfield glass off of the game cabinet.
3. Remove the nut and washer on top of the post. Carefully, slide the bottom arch edge over the post.
   Unscrew the posts and remove the washer at the bottom.
4. Replace the post. Slide the bottom arch edge over the top of the post and replace the nut. Do Not
   replace the washers.
5. Lower the playfield, replace the glass and front molding. Close and lock the coin door.

Drawing labels: `Bottom Arch 03-9068`; `Remove (2) Flatwashers each side 4700-00011-00 (.060 x .172 x
.435)`; `Post 02-5079-4 (1.62" long)`.

## Manual Amendment, 16-9876, August 10, 1994

File `Williams_1994_The_Flintstones_Manual_Amendment_Page_2_24_Correction_August_10_1994.pdf`.

Heading: `The FLINTSTONES®` / `MANUAL AMENDMENT`; top right `16-9876` / `August 10, 1994`.

> The following drawing is from page 2-24 of the FLINTSTONES® Operators Manual. The part called out as

> Please keep this amendment with your FLINTSTONES® Operators Manual.
