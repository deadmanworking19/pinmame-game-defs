## Solenoid/Flashlamp Circuit, PDF page 109 (printed page 2-44)

Document: `Williams_1994_The_Flintstones_English_Manual.pdf` (Operations Manual 16-50029-101, July 1994), IPDB machine 888. PDF page 109, printed page number (folio) **2-44**, printed title "Solenoid/Flashlamp Circuit". The scan of this page is low resolution (blurry glyphs); every row was cross-read against the sharper copy on PDF page 119 (printed page 3-5), and the two copies agree except where listed under "Differences between copies". The table is complete on this page (the right-hand columns are not clipped).

### Differences between copies (PDF 109 vs PDF 119)

Cell-by-cell comparison of every printed cell of the solenoid, G.I. and flipper blocks:

- **No cell value differs** where both copies are legible: Function names, Solenoid Types, voltage and drive connector-pins, drive transistors, drive wires, part numbers/flashlamp types, G.I. rows and flipper rows are identical.
- Differences in what is present on the page:
  1. PDF 119 (printed 3-5) carries two lines of notes under the table that PDF 109 (printed 2-44) does not: `J1XX-X = POWER DRIVER BOARD, JX-X =8-DRIVER BOARD, J9XX-X =FLIPTRONIC II BOARD` and `PLEASE NOTE: IN THIS GAME, THE UPPER RIGHT FLIPPER CIRCUIT IS USED TO DRIVE THE LEFT & RIGHT UP/DOWN GATES.`
  2. PDF 119's table is clipped by the right edge of the scan: the Backbox sub-column of the part/flashlamp type and the Coil Colors column are cut off (the Knocker row shows only `AE-2`; backbox flashlamp cells show only `#906`; the Coil Colors cells show only `B`). These cells are complete on PDF 109 and the values above for them come from PDF 109 only.
  3. Title typography: PDF 109 title "Solenoid/Flashlamp Circuit"; PDF 119 title "SOLENOID/FLASHLAMP CIRCUIT" (upper case).
  4. Scan quality: PDF 109 is blurrier; the wire cell of row 41 reads `Grn-Wht` on PDF 109 but the sharper PDF 119 glyphs read `Gm-Wht` (the "rn" is touching; see Uncertain readings).
  5. Folios differ: 2-44 vs 3-5.

### Cross-references noted while transcribing (not differences between the two copies)

- Sol. 28 (Fred's Choice Fls.) lists drive connection `J122-4` in both copies of this table, while the Flashlamp Wiring drawing (PDF 121, printed 3-7) shows the `BLU-YEL` wire for Fred's Choice FLS. SOL. 28 on connector `J122` pin `2` (J122 has only pins 1 and 2 drawn). This is a disagreement between the table and the drawing within the same manual.
- Sol. 17 "Under Lt. Rmp Fls." has Solenoid Type `Low Power` while Sols. 18-22 and 24 are typed `Flasher` (printed that way in both copies); the row's part is `#89 (1)` / `#906 (2)`, i.e. a lamp, like the neighbouring flasher rows.
- Sol. 21 (Dino Slide Fls.) shows drive transistor `Q28`, which breaks the descending even sequence of the neighbouring rows (Q42, Q40, Q38, Q36, then Q28, Q30, Q34, Q32, Q26, ...); the digits `Q28` read identically on PDF 119.
- Sols. 41-44 show Q9, Q10, Q11, Q12 following Q16, Q15, Q14, Q13 for 37-40; these also read the same on PDF 119.
- Q10, Q12, Q14, Q16 are also listed as the G.I. drive transistors (02, 05, 03, 04) while Sols. 42, 44, 39, 37 print the same Q numbers; transcribed exactly as printed.

### Uncertain readings

- Row 41 drive wire: printed glyphs read `Gm-Wht` on PDF 119 and look like `Grn-Wht` on PDF 109; transcribed as `Grn-Wht` [?] (the "rn" pair is touching in the scan; all other green wires such as `Brn-Grn` read cleanly with a visible gap).
- Row 29-36 Sol. No. cell: the final digit is overprinted by the cell border; read as `36` [?] (confirmed as 29-36 on PDF 119).
- Sol. No. suffix mark on rows 37-44: a small dot-like `*`; read as an asterisk [?].
- PDF 109 is a blurry scan; digits 6/8 and 1/7 were individually cross-checked against PDF 119 for every row except the cells clipped on PDF 119 (Backbox part/flashlamp column and Coil Colors). For those clipped-on-119 cells only PDF 109 supports the reading: Knocker backbox `AE-23-800`; backbox types `#906 (2)` (rows 17-21) and `#906 (3)` (rows 26, 27); Coil Colors `BLUE`.
