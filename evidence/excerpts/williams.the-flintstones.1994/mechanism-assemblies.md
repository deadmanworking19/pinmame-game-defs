# Williams The Flintstones (1994) — Mechanism assembly parts lists

Source document `Williams_1994_The_Flintstones_English_Manual.pdf` (The Flintstones Operations Manual 16-50029-101, July 1994), PDF pages 82, 85, 87, 88, 89, 90, 92, 93 and 94 (read from the native 300 dpi renders), plus the one-page amendment `Williams_1994_The_Flintstones_Manual_Amendment_Page_2_24_Correction_August_10_1994.pdf`. Each section below is a literal transcription of the printed parts list for one assembly, read from the page image: item numbers, part numbers and descriptions are spelled as printed (capitalisation, punctuation, abbreviations, spacing), in printed item order, with the two-column printed lists merged in item order. Callout numbers on the drawings are not transcribed. Inch marks are tiny and degraded in the scans and are written here as `"`. Nothing was corrected or completed from a sibling assembly; apparent oddities are listed under Notes lines and under Uncertain readings.

## Uncertain readings

Method: every cell was read from the page image, enlarged and (for the heavy page 93) lightly blurred to see the closed loops of 3/6/8/9. Where more than one reading is plausible the alternatives are given. No cell was resolved by copying from another row or assembly.

PDF page 93 (A-18823, heavy blotchy face); digits 3/6/8/9 and 1/7 are the risk:
- Item 1 `11-831-50029`: middle group read `831`; alternatives `631`, `861`.
- Item 3 `4808-01175-08`: leading group read `4808` (alternative `4008`); in the description `#8` the 8 may be `#6`.
- Items 4, 6 and 8: the middle group `01168` is read with a 6 then an 8 (alternatives `01188`, `01166`); item 6's first group is read `4408` (alternative `4008`).
- Item 9 `01-12569`: read `569`; alternatives `589`, `568`.
- Item 10 `A-18942-4`: read `18942`; alternative `18642`.
- Item 11 `H-18798`: last two digits read `98`; alternatives `96`, `88`.
- Item 12 `A-18128`: last digit read `8`; alternatives `3`, `6`.
- Item 13 `A-17978`: read `17978`; alternatives `17973`, `17976`. (PDF page 94's title prints `A-17978` and was read there without difficulty.)
- Item 14 `A-18985`: read `18985`; alternatives `18983`, `18965`, `18935`.
- Item 15 `A-18369`: read `18369`; the 3 and 6 touch; alternative `18389`. (PDF page 89's title prints `A-18369` and was read there without difficulty.)
- Item 16 `01-12669`: read `12669`; alternatives `12569`, `12689`, `12668`.
- Item 17 `4106-0115-12`: the middle group has four digits (`0115`) as printed, whereas the other `4106-` part numbers on PDF page 85 have five (`01019`, `01152`); the 5 could be 3 or 6.
- Item 19 `4008-01113-12`: the second `1` of `113` has a broken stroke; read `01113`, alternative `01173`.
- Item 20 description `13/64`: the 64 could read `84`.
- Item 7 description `Socket/ Bulb Assembly` is printed with the space after the slash; the `(See p.2-29)` and `(See p.2-24)` separators read as hyphens (could be periods).

Other pages:
- PDF page 94 item 1: part number printed `A-17977.1`; the mark between `7` and `1` is a tiny dot or comma at the baseline, not a hyphen. Transcribed with a period.
- PDF page 94 item 14 description: printed `11/64x 7/16 x 16ga.` (no space before the first `x`), transcribed literally.
- PDF page 89 A-17932 item 6 `A-17986`, A-18369 item 6 `A-18368`, and PDF page 90 A-19097 item 6 `A-19096`: last digits checked at 4x and read as stated; no alternative found, listed because they differ by one digit from the neighbouring titles (`A-17932`, `A-18369`, `A-19097`).
- PDF page 85: the apparent part-number and description mismatches on items 9, 11, 13 and 14 are printed that way (see the Notes on that section); the digits themselves were read clearly.
- Inch marks throughout (`5/16"`, `3/8"`, `2.093"`, `1/4"`, ...): the glyph is a tiny raised stroke that in places looks like `'` or `*`; written as `"` everywhere. PDF page 82 (items 11 and 13) and PDF page 87 are the most degraded.
- The `t` in `Flatwasher` and the `i` in `Coil` are degraded in places (`Flalwasher`, `Coi!`); written as the normal words.
- PDF page 87 item 10 description `17/64x1/2x21ga.` and item 17 `3/16x7/16x17ga.` are printed without spaces around the `x`; transcribed literally.
- PDF page 90, A-18078: a stray speck sits before the `A` of the title; no effect on the reading.
