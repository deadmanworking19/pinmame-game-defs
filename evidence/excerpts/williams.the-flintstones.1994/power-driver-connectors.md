### Notes on apparent misprints

- `J116-1N/C` has no space between the pin number and N/C (as printed).
- `gound` appears for "ground" in the J118-3 line.
- `J120-3 ... G.I  to backbox` lacks the final period after G.I. (as printed).
- J124 lists pins 1, 2, 3 and 5; there is no line for J124-4.
- The J124 lines read "sol. 25 drive to backbox playfield" and "sol. 27 drive to backbox playfield" (sol. 25 also appears on J122-1, "playfield flashlamp").
- `J115-1` is Yellow-White (not White-...) as printed, the only J115 entry with a "Yellow-" prefix.

### Uncertain readings

- J112-3 voltage is printed as `9.8Vac` (the "8" is slightly blurred, could read 9.6) [?].
- `J112-1` has a period after "White-Green" instead of a comma (as printed).

### Notes on apparent misprints

- `J126-3` is printed `Black-Orange. sol  19` (period after the colour, double space) as shown above.
- The wire colour pattern for sol. 17 through 21 is repeated: J125 (page 3-30) lists sol. 17-21 as "backbox flashlamps" with the same colours as J126-1 to J126-5 ("playfield flashlamps"); both pages print the colours as shown.
- `J138-6` has no double space after the pin number (printed `J138-6 Yellow-Blue`).
- The connector between J135 and J138 on the board drawing (page 3-29) has no printed label there; this page's `J136` is the only listing for it.

### Uncertain readings

None beyond small-print blur; all lines above were read at 1.4x to 1.6x magnification and the pin numbers, wire colours and sol./row/column numbers were legible.
