### Notes (apparent misprints, transcribed literally)

- The list prints the item number `18` for both the `Up 3-Bnk Center Tgt.` row and the `Up 3-Bnk Right Tgt.` row; item 17 is missing from the list. The drawing carries a distinct callout `17` between `16` and `18`.
- Item 11 part number reads `20-9663-B-7`; the `B` may be an `8`.
- The list carries no item 12 part number (`Not Used`) and no part number for 57, 58 and 81 to 88.

### Uncertain readings

- Item 11 part number `20-9663-B-7`: `B` versus `8`.
- Digits 6, 8 and 9 are close in the scan; part numbers (for example 5647-12693-xx, A-18606-7, A-18605-5, A-16908, A-16909) were read at 3x and are believed correct but not independently verifiable.
- Callout 36 and callout 45 positions were read from the drawing; set membership is confident, but a callout hidden behind a graphic cannot be excluded.
