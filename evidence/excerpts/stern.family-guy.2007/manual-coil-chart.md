## Coil and flash-lamp test text (PDF pages 39 and 41)

PDF page 39: "Coils #01 – #16 are typically High Current Coils … Coils #17 – #32 are typically Low Current Coils. Flash Lamps are typically used in positions #25 – #32 (although Flash Lamps may be used in any position and will be noted). Auxiliary Coils may be used in positions #33 – #35." The Single Coil Test shows the coil name, number and "Coil or Flash Lamp Power Line / Drive Transistor Control Line wire colors"; the black SELECT button energizes the coil; the Cycling Coil Test pulses each regular coil or flash lamp in turn.

PDF page 41, "Stewie Motor (Family Guy) Test": "This Menu is provided to allow the technician a simple method of properly testing the Stewie Stepper Motor & Controller PCB Drive (Coil #20) which will not appear in the normal SINGLE COIL TEST MENU or CYCLING COIL TEST MENU." If the Stewie character is out of alignment (it does not turn towards the Stewie Mini-Pinball Machine) the test aligns it: the controller pulses the stepper enough to stop against the "raised portion" of the motor mounting bracket and then pulses it in the opposite direction.
