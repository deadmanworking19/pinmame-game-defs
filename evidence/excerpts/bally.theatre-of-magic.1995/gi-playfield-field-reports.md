# Theatre of Magic — owner reports on the playfield G.I. strings 03-05

Four repair threads by owners of physical Theatre of Magic machines, read on 2026-10-23. The pages
and photographs are retained outside the repository at
`external:pinmame-review-artifacts/theatre-of-magic-1995/field-reports/` (external evidence manifest
SHA-256 `6635a6eb0fdbbaaa157ea486e7f5d98ba1044be84f36cbeebdadf28174686ed5`) and are not redistributed
here. Quotations are verbatim, including the posters' spelling. Times are the forum's own UTC
timestamps. Pinside shows a signed-out reader the first three posts of a thread, so the retained
copies hold those posts; every quotation below is from them.

## Reading

String 04 (Green / White-Green) is the left side of the playfield, and string 05 (Violet /
White-Violet) is the right side. String 03 (Yellow / White-Yellow) is the third playfield string, but
none of these reports says which part of the playfield it lights. None of them lists the bulbs on a
string either. "Left side" and "right side" name areas, not sockets: they do not settle whether a
bulb near the centre line, at the top of the playfield or in a jet bumper belongs to string 03, 04 or
05.
