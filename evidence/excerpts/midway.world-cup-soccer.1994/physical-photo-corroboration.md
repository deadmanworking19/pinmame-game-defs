# World Cup Soccer — 2026-10-23 physical photo corroboration

Physical photographs of production machines, used to corroborate the identity, count and order of devices
whose coordinates this definition measures on the factory location drawings. The photographs supply no
coordinate: every one is a perspective view, and the signs and domes stand above the playfield.

The photographs stay external, under the working root's
`review-artifacts/world-cup-soccer/physical-photos/`, pinned by that folder's canonical manifest
(`manifest.json`, SHA-256 `5e9ba4932ae54593f7eb69c8689660d94ddd99a2b5a7ca7505a463ba7ef3e7a1`). Copyright in
each photograph stays with its photographer, and in the machine artwork with WMS Industries; rights and
license are `NOASSERTION`. This excerpt records only what the photographs show; it does not copy any of them.

## What they show

- **The two ramp signs (lamps 71, 78, 76 and 77).** IPDB picture 49215 shows both signs from the front, one
  at each ramp entrance, standing on posts above the playfield. Each carries three lamp windows, top to
  bottom LIGHT JACKPOT (a red bulb), ULTRA RAMPS and BUY TICKET, each with its own bulb. IPDB picture 43240
  shows both signs in place, the Pinside close-up `6cf8a7c6.jpg` the right one with its labels legible, and
  the Pinside photo `IMG_4166.jpeg` both lit. This matches the lamp drawing
  (printed 2-45): three bulbs on each sign, 71 at the top, 78 in the middle and 76 (left sign) or 77 (right
  sign) at the foot.
- **The side-wall flasher domes (flashers 26 and 19).** IPDB picture 43240 shows a yellow flasher dome on the
  left side wall beside the raised LOCK area, where callout 26 ends on the solenoid drawing (printed 2-49), and
  a yellow flasher dome on the right side wall below the right ramp sign, where the upper callout 19 ends.
  The Pinside close-up `6cf8a7c6.jpg` shows the right-wall dome too.
- **The top-corner domes (flasher 28).** IPDB picture 43240 shows a flasher dome in each top corner of the
  playfield, the two callouts 28 of the solenoid drawing.
- **The jet bumpers.** The stripped playfield in IPDB picture 43241 shows the three jet-bumper bases in a
  triangle at the upper right, one to the left and two to the right, one behind the other, as both location
  drawings draw them.

## Use

The factory drawings supply each measured coordinate and the callout identity; the photographs corroborate
that each measured device exists, how many bulbs it has and in what order, and on which side of the playfield
it sits. Neither replaces the other.
