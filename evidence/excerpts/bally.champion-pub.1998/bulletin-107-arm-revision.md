# Champion Pub arm revision, SB 107

Williams/Bally service bulletin 107, dated December 28, 1998, page 1.
Factual transcription and paraphrase, visually checked against the retained PDF.

| Position | Revised part | Identification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left boxer arm | 31-3065.1 | Interior B marking identifies the material change |
| Right boxer arm | 31-3066.1 | Interior B marking identifies the material change |

The bulletin reports shoulder fractures that can trap a ball or prevent arm
movement. It directs replacement with these revised arms and a small amount
of Loctite 425 intended for plastic on the mounting screw threads during
reassembly. The document prints the product name as LockTite 425.
