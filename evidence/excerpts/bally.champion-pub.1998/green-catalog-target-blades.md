# Factory stationary-target blade switches

Source: Williams Green Parts Catalog 16-9932, July 1996, hosted page 332,
printed 6-2. The retained original source is the complete 1131 × 1600 WebP
page image, SHA-256 785ab707a2dd2c761b62e0f31fe9d7be5b381ce721a339e6b799eb8f98cda549.
The full PDF was not acquired. The curator visually inspected every table row,
column and blank cell at the original image resolution.

The literal table heading is **Blade Switches Used On Stationary Target Assemblies**.
Its second header band labels the six right columns **Game and Part Quantity**.
Switch Part Number and Target Assembly Part Number are the first two columns.
The SW-1A-170-5 and SW-1A-178-4 cells each span two target rows; the continuation
cells below are left empty rather than repeating the switch number. Empty game
cells are preserved and do not assert zero. The six game headings and model
numbers are copied as printed, including the abbreviated heading India.

The Champion Pub game manual independently identifies its Half Guy contacts
55/56 with exact assembly A-17795-6. This factory catalog establishes blade
switch construction and the exact SW-1A-178-6 cross-reference for that shared
part; it supplies neither Champion Pub fitment nor a Champion Pub quantity.
No equivalence to A-18530-6 is assumed. Identity and polarity still follow the
game-specific evidence.
