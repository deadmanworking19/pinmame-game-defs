The matrix legend on page 51 duplicates the complete 1-64 functions transcribed
from Figure 2, including 40 Ball in Play and the brace on backbox 1-5. No other
functional label differs. Its cell classification nevertheless prints 6 and
45-48 BACKBOX, despite Figure 2 drawing those lamps on the playfield. Those
template location labels must not be promoted. Figure 2 is the game-specific
physical placement authority; the retained playfield photos corroborate the
return-lane/mystery/extra-ball insert positions. Lamp 40 has no Figure 2
callout and is omitted by the scripts, but PDF41 positively draws9B6 BALL IN
PLAY on COL.1/ROW6. This disputes both the NOT USED cell40 and the assignment
of6. See insert-board.md: keep40's physical routing conflicted. Runtime changes
at40 do not establish absence or fitment of a bulb.

Page 50's lamp connectors agree with the Figure 1 headers. Its harness side
maps column 8..1 to 8P2/8J2 pins 10..3 and rows 1..8 to pins 11..18, using
YEL-GRY,VIO,BLU,GRN,BLK,ORN,RED,BRN column suffixes and
RED-BRN,BLK,ORN,YEL,GRN,BLU,VIO,GRY row suffixes respectively. Its two
6.3 VAC general-illumination branches are 8J8/8P8 pin 5 YEL / pin 2 YEL-WHT,
and pin 1 VIO / pin 4 WHT. The drawing's repeated GI bulb symbols do not count
physical sockets. The bottom caption says to see the insert board wiring
diagram for backbox lamp connections.
