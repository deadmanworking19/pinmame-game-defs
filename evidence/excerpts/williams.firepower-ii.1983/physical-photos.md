the LOCK plastic/guide. Orbit Complete windows at the rear right are illuminated,

board observation, independent of the factory drawing.

Photo 50054 (behind backglass): four seven-digit score displays and a lower
central credit/match display assembly, plus incandescent backbox lamps.
The picture confirms physical backbox display placement and real GI bulbs,
but does not identify every socket's circuit or establish total GI quantity.

The inspected photos supply no exact VPX coordinate frame. They strengthen
physical classification and assembly interpretation while spatial placement
and emitter-count gates remain open.
