## IPDB 5500

https://www.ipdb.org/machine.cgi?id=5500 identifies Wild Texas as a conversion
of Firepower II, with manufacturer and date unknown. Its physical features
include a ball-diverter wireform in the upper right. The owner of the pictured
game reports Gottlieb-coded coils and Gottlieb flipper assemblies and a
transformer marked Tri Zone. These are different physical parts from the
Firepower II service-manual inventory.

## Pinside repairer's report, topic 335329

The original poster Theonlylilo describes repairing a Wild Texas with an
original Williams driver board and a distinct System 7-compatible MPU: one
27128 program EPROM, fewer/socketed ICs, no diagnostic pushbuttons, and a
three-position selector. The completed repair report says the selector chooses
six- or seven-digit displays, the ROM displays game identification 2521 1,
and Firepower II's handbook adjustments work. The author reports successful
play with the space-themed sounds despite the Western theme.

This is positive evidence of a separate physical conversion with different
MPU hardware, rather than a mere title label for identical Williams model 521
hardware. PinMAME's shared INITGAMEFULL proves routing compatibility only.
The high-tier disposition separates wldtexas into unknown-manufacturer.wild-texas,
kind physical_conversion, IPDB5500, unknown manufacturer and null year. No
OPDB identity is claimed. Its catalog clone_of remains fpwr2_l2; clone ancestry
does not merge physical records. The retained Tony Turner MPU photograph
(IPDB42932) was visually checked: one program EPROM and a changed CPU layout
are visible. It supports the attributed repair account, without proving every
Wild Texas specimen uses the same parts. No original Williams wiring inventory
has been copied into the conversion.
