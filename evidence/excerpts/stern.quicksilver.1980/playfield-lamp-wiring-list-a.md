Source: `Stern_1980_Quicksilver_Manual.pdf` (IPDB machine 1895, "English Manual [Stern Electronics]", 35 pages, a 300 dpi bilevel scan with no text layer), PDF page 21 (the right half of playfield wiring diagram sheet `12B-432-S-117`, `SHEET #2 OF 3`), first half of the typed lamp list. The list is a typed, tabular block headed `DESCRIPTION`, `WIRE COLOR`, `JACK LDA`, `PIN NO.`; dot leaders on some rows are layout only. Wire-color words are as printed: `B` is black (the blue wires are spelled `BLU`).
