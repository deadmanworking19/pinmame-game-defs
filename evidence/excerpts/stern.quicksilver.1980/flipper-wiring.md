Source: `Stern_1980_Quicksilver_Manual.pdf` (IPDB machine 1895, "English Manual [Stern Electronics]", 35 pages, a 300 dpi bilevel scan with no text layer), PDF page 7, the lower part: the generic flipper wiring figure (the jumper table above it is not cited). Transcribed from the 300 dpi render.

Labels, verbatim: `F4` (a fuse), `RECT.BRD.`, `J1-6`, `+ 43 VDC`, `BLU-W`, `LEFT FLIPPER`, `RT. FLIPPER`, `FLIPPER WIRING`, `E.O.S.` (the left flipper), `E.O.S. A` and `E.O.S. B` (the right flipper), `IN 4004 DIODE`, `G-` (left drive wire), `-O-` (right drive wire), `SDU J1-8`, `SDU J1-9`, `SDU RELAY`,
`SDU J2-2`, `SDU J2-1`, `EXTRA FLIPPER` with `BRN-W`, and the caption `Dashed Lines: Sometimes when more than 1 Flipper is used on one side, a 1-OHM 5-WATT Resistor is Used to Reduce Power.`

Circuit as drawn. The +43 VDC bus from the rectifier board connector J1 pin 6 (wire BLU-W) feeds the top of each flipper's two-winding coil. Each flipper is two windings in series, the upper one first, each with its own 1N4004 diode across it. An end-of-stroke (E.O.S.) contact, drawn with the normally-closed symbol, is wired in parallel with the upper winding. The lower end of the coil
leaves on the drive wire (left `G`, right `O`) to the Solenoid Driver (SDU) connector J1 pin 8 (left) or 9 (right) and then through the SDU relay contact to the flipper button on SDU J2 pin 2 (left) or 1 (right). With the E.O.S. contact closed (at rest) the upper winding is shorted out, so a button press energizes the lower winding alone at full current;
when the stroke ends the contact opens and the upper winding is inserted in series, which cuts the current to a holding level. The dashed extra-flipper variant, drawn beside the right flipper, is a second two-winding coil with its own 1N4004 diode across each winding; its upper winding has a dashed branch with a resistor symbol and a switch symbol across it, and its lower end (`BRN-W`) rejoins the right flipper's drive wire through the dashed contact `E.O.S. B`, while the right flipper's own contact is labelled `E.O.S. A`. Quicksilver's flipper inventory is the plain two-flipper circuit.
