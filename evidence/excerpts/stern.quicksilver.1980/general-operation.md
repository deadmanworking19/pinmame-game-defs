Source: `Stern_1980_Quicksilver_Manual.pdf` (IPDB machine 1895, "English Manual [Stern Electronics]", 35 pages, a 300 dpi bilevel scan with no text layer), PDF page 5, section `IV. GENERAL GAME OPERATION` (text page; read from the 300 dpi render, OCR-assisted and checked).

Selected statements, close paraphrase except where quoted:

- The game plays a tune on power-up; drop targets are reset; scores are reset to zero alternating with the `High Game to Date`.
- Pressing the credit button makes the outhole kicker move the ball to the shooter lane; one player is registered per press, one to four
  players; the button is inoperative after four players are registered.
- Thumper bumper scores 1000 points on 3 ball, 100 points on 5 ball. The page also prints `Scoring Bumpers advance Missle No. 1 & 3 when
  lit`, which names a feature no switch or lamp of this game's own sheets carries; that sentence is not used. Slingshots score 10 points.
- When the ball enters the outhole the bonus is added, the player-up and ball-in-play advance, the outhole kicker moves the ball to the
  shooter lane.
- At game end the `Game Over` light lights and a random `Match` number appears with the `Match` light.
- Extra ball won is played immediately after the regular ball enters the outhole.
- Tilting loses the ball in play; flippers and thumper bumpers go dead; bonus is not added. Game action becomes normal after the outhole
  kicker moves the ball to the shooter lane.
- Slamming loses the game: all feature lights go out and the game goes dead through a time-delay circuit. There is a slam switch on the
  front door, one on the tilt board and one on the playfield.
