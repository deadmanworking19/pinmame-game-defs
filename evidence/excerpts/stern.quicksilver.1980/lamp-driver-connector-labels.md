Source: `Stern_1980_Quicksilver_Lamp_Driver_Schematic.jpg` (IPDB machine 1895, "Lamp Driver Schematic", a 2330x1555 pixel grayscale JPEG scan, 300 dpi, drawing `12B-432-S-116`, `SHEET #1 OF 3`). Title block, verbatim: `STERN ELECTRONICS INC.`, `1725 DIVERSEY BLVD. CHICAGO 60614`, `LAMP DRIVER SCHEMATIC`, `FOR` with the word `CHEETAH` struck through by hand and `Quicksilver` written beside it. The sheet was drafted for a sister game and relabelled for this one by hand, so every cell below is read from the relabelled sheet, not carried from any other game. The three connector blocks `J1`, `J2` and `J3` at the right of the circuit, each pin drawn as an arrow with its pin number and the label of the SCR that drives it (a small triangle before some labels marks a MCR106-1 part; the others are 2N5060). Read from the pixel crop, three blocks side by side at twice the scan size. Some SCRs appear on more than one pin. Every row whose pin digits are legible is listed. Two further J1 rows are drawn at the end of its block, under Q24, Q17 and Q20: `Q23`, whose pin digits read `14` (a pin already used by Q9, so probably a smudged `4`), and `Q21` on pin `12`; neither pin is one a lamp list uses, so they are not transcribed as rows. The pin printed beside Q24 is smudged and reads `5`; no lamp list uses it.

Examples of one SCR reaching two connectors: Q3 is on J1 pin 26 and J2 pin 21, which are the two `SHOOT AGAIN` pins the two printed lamp lists give.

Reading note for J3 pin `18`. The label beside it is blurred: its second digit is closer to a `4` than a `6`, so it could be `Q44` or `Q46`. It is transcribed `Q46` because `Q44` is already drawn on J3 pin `19`, the lamp list prints a different lamp for each of the two pins (`TOP R.O. DIVIDERS` for 18, `BONUS 20,000` for 19), and Q46 is the only decoder output (`R46`, fourth decoder S9) that no other connector pin would otherwise carry. The J3 block holds exactly the 23 rows above; there is no separate extra Q44 row.
