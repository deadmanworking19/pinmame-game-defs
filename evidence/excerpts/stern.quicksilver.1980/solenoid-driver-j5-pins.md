Beside the J5 box a note reads: `Ul, U2 & U3 = CA3081`; `*SE9302 Substitute X44E198`; `Solenoid Test Display No. is Drive Transistor (Q) Position on SDU`. A header on the decoder reads `PINS 3, 4, 5 & 6 MOMENTARY SOLENOID DATA` and `SOLENOID BANK SELECT LOW WHEN SELECTED`. `U2` is a 74LS154.

The playfield wiring sheet prints `A3J1-5 (B-G)` for the out-hole coil and `A3J5-12 (D-O)` for the right drop-target bank; this schematic places the out-hole on J5 pin 11 and the right drop-target bank on J5 pin 10.
