Number 28 is printed `SPECIAL ROLL-OVER SWITCH`. Numbers 25-27 each end in the literal suffix `(TOP)`; the matrix sheet
(PDF page 20) labels the same three positions TOP RIGHT S.U., R. S.U. and RIGHT S.U. BOTTOM, so the repeated `(TOP)`
is a printing quirk and the order is top to bottom. The literal `(NO-SHF)` after 39 is printed exactly so.

Below the table is a small drawing: a box labelled `3-CHUTE DR.` with three leaders `SW-1`, `SW-2` and `SW-3`
pointing at its left, middle and right parts, and a leader labelled `HINGE` at the box's lower-left corner. SW-1 is
therefore the chute nearest the door hinge.
