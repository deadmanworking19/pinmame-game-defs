Source: `Stern_1980_Quicksilver_Manual.pdf` (IPDB machine 1895, "English Manual [Stern Electronics]", 35 pages, a 300 dpi bilevel scan with no text layer), PDF page 15, a full-page line drawing of the playfield outline with numbered callouts.

Heading, verbatim: `SOLENOIDS` over `QUICKSILVER`. Left-hand note, verbatim:

```
NOT ON PLAYFIELD
 3-KNOCKER
19-LOCKOUT
```
