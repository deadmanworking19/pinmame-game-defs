Source: `Stern_1980_Quicksilver_Manual.pdf` (IPDB machine 1895, "English Manual [Stern Electronics]", 35 pages, a 300 dpi bilevel scan with no text layer), PDF page 23, the right part of the cabinet and door wiring schematic `12B-432-S-121` (title block: `STERN ELECTRONICS INC.`,
`CABINET AND FRONT DOOR WIRING`, `FOR STANDARD`, `DRN. C.W.`, `DATE 10-16-79`). The drawing is titled for the
standard cabinet, not for this game by name.

Mains section, verbatim labels: `(B)` to terminal 1 and `(W)` to terminal 2 through the `POWER ON/OFF` switch, `L1`, `L2`, `GRD`,
`LINE FILTER`, `GRD. BRAID`, `120 VAC`, `120 VAC RETURN`, `SERVICE OUTLET`, and `VR1 GE # V130LA10A VARISTOR`. Three power
wires leave downward: `A2J2-6 (Y)`, `A2J2-7 (BLU-W)` and `A2J2-10 (G)`. A knocker coil is drawn at the upper left.
