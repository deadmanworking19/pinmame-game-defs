Source: `Stern_1980_Quicksilver_Manual.pdf` (IPDB machine 1895, "English Manual [Stern Electronics]", 35 pages, a 300 dpi bilevel scan with no text layer), PDF page 20, the right-hand part of the playfield wiring diagram (drawing `12B-432-S-117`, `WIRING DIAGRAM FOR QUICK SILVER`,
`SHEET #2 OF 3` on PDF page 21). The wide drawing is split across PDF pages 20 and 21 with an overlap of one coil
column; this page carries the left-hand coils and the wires that feed them. Transcribed by hand from the 300 dpi render.

Supply and the flipper section. The flipper supply bus is `A2J1-6 (BLU-W)`. The `LEFT FLIPPER` is drawn as two windings
(power and hold, each with a diode) with an end-of-stroke contact that transfers from the power to the hold winding. Its
drive wire is `A3J1-8 (G)`. A second wire `A3J1-9 (O)` runs across to the right flipper (see sheet B). A third wire
`A3J1-5 (B-G)` runs across to the out-hole coil (see sheet B).

Coils drawn on this half, with the wire printed beside the pin on the left:

| Printed coil label | Connection printed |
| --- | --- |
| LEFT THUMPER | A3J2-9 (G-O) |
| RIGHT THUMP. | A3J2-4 (G-BLU) |
| (a third thumper, drawn on sheet B as LOW THUMP.) | A3J2-10 (G-Y) |
| LEFT SLING-SHOT | A3J2-11 (G-R) |
| RIGHT SLING SHOT | A3J2-12 (R-Y) |
| (kick-out hole, drawn on sheet B) | A3J5-12 (B-Y) |
| CENTER D.T. | A3J1-5 (B-BLU) |
| RIGHT D.T. | A3J5-12 (D-O), the first letter printed like a D; read B-O |

All coils are drawn with a flyback diode across the winding. The right-hand pins of the three thumper, two sling-shot
and drop-target coils join the `(Y)` supply bus drawn on sheet B.
