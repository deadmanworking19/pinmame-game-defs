Source: `Stern_1980_Quicksilver_Solenoid_Driver_Schematic.jpg` (IPDB machine 1895, "Solenoid Driver Schematic", a 1601x1097 pixel color JPEG scan, drawing `12B-432-S-117`, `SHEET # 3 OF 3`). Title block, verbatim: `STERN ELECTRONICS INC.`, `SOLENOID DRIVER VOLTAGE REGULATOR SCHEMATIC`, `FOR QUICK SILVER`. Lower-left box headed `J2`, a table of the six momentary coils that use that connector. Pin number first, then the printed text:

| J2 pin | Printed text |
| ---: | --- |
| 9 | LEFT THUMPER (G-O) |
| 4 | RIGHT THUMPER (G-BLU) |
| 10 | LOWER THUMPER (G-Y) |
| 11 | LEFT SLING-SHOT (G-R) |
| 5 | KNOCKER (G-B) |
| 12 | RIGHT SLING-SHOT (R-Y) |

The sheet therefore prints the knocker's wire as `G-B`; the cabinet sheet prints `A3J2-5 (B-Y)` for the knocker wire; the two do not agree and neither is needed to place the device.
