## Reading

These are leads, not authority. Two owners of physical machines, comparing the manual's Lower
Playfield Parts list with what is under their playfields, do not recognise the 16-LED Skull Driver
PCB and connect it to the skull pile only by guessing; one says the page shows "which parts are not
installed on your machine". Neither says outright that their own machine lacks the board. They
corroborate Midway's development notes (`development-notes-pile-o-skulls.md`), which are the
authority for the board's absence from production.
