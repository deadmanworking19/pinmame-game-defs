# Paragon - back box game adjustments (MPU option switches S1-S32)

Transcribed from `Bally_1979_Paragon_Manual.pdf`, PDF pages 9-12 (printed pages 5, 6 and 7, "V. GAME ADJUSTMENTS" and "#1167-E PARAGON"), read from the native renders (no text layer). Tables keep every printed row and column; the page's spelling is kept, including "oder" and "col ected".

## Printed page 5

> **B. Back Box Game Adjustments:**
> Each game has thirty-two switches located on A4, the MPU module, located in the back box, that allow play to be customized to the location. See Figure III. Credits per coin, maximum credits, credit display, balls per game, match feature, high game feature, special award and melody are selectable by means of the switches. The switches are contained in four-sixteen lead packages numbered S1-8, S9-16, S17-24 and S25-32 for easy identification. The "ON" toggle position is marked on the assembly. **Turn off power before making adjustments.**
>

**BALLS PER GAME:** 5 balls - switch 16 ON; 3 balls - switch 16 OFF.

**MATCH FEATURE:** "When the Match Feature is ON, a random number appears in the Match/Ball in Play window and the word MATCH is illuminated. If the number matches the tens digit in a player's score, a free game is awarded." Match ON - switch 21 ON; OFF - switch 21 OFF.

**CREDIT DISPLAY:** credits displayed YES - switch 20 ON; NO - switch 20 OFF.
