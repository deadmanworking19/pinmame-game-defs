# Paragon - Figure V, switch and solenoid locations

Source: `Bally_1979_Paragon_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 22 (printed page 18), "#1167-E PARAGON", captioned "FIGURE V". The crop beside this file is the playfield drawing at the scan's native 300 dpi; the legend printed to the right of it is transcribed below rather than cropped.

Legend, verbatim:

> ◯ INDICATES SWITCH ASSEMBLY IDENTIFICATION NUMBERS.
> NOTE: CABINET: 07, 16
> DOOR: 06, 09, 10, 11, 16
>
> ▢ INDICATES SOLENOID IDENTIFICATION NUMBERS.
> NOTE: DOOR: 14
> BACKBOX: 15
> CABINET: 02

The numbers are the page-17 identification numbers (switch self-test numbers in circles, solenoid self-test numbers in squares), not public controller addresses. No number on the drawing has a leader line; each is printed beside or inside the part it names.
