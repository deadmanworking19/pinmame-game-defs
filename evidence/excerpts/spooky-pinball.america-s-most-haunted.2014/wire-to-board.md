# AMH wire-to-board chart (AMH-WIRE-TO-BOARD.pdf)

Transcribed from the chart's text layer (the RZ chart from its raster), every pin aligned by its tick; the coil banks, the cabinet header and the GI_1 header were spot-checked against the render at 300 dpi, not every pin. Uncertain readings are listed at the end.

Pins are listed in tick order as drawn on the chart (connector header lines run left to right as printed, vertical headers top to bottom). '(blank)' means no wire text is printed at that tick; '(unlabeled tick)' means a tick with no pin label.

## Uncertain readings

- Coil lists: the printed list contains only the coils shown; Sol Bank 0 lists only '0 - magnet - gray' (1-7 not listed at all), Bank 1 has no '12' line, Bank 2 has no '23' line. Transcribed as blank for the missing numbers, which differs from the other charts where unused coils print '7 -'.
- '9 -  right sling - orange' is printed with two spaces after the dash; kept as a leading space in the coil label.
- Sol Bank 0 connector: only pin 0 carries a wire ('gray'); pins 1-7 carry none although the list has no other coil. Sol Pwr: only the first g ('green(ground)') and first p ('red(power)') carry text; second g and second p carry none.
- Aux board: 12c SDA and 12c SCL carry no wire colour. TCBDTWN header: pins 3-7 carry no colour.
- Light Column pwr pin is printed '12v' as its wire text and Light Row pwr pin '5v'; Switch Row and Switch Column 'g' pins print 'ground' as wire text.
- Sol Bank power wires are printed as '50v pwr' for all three banks (and 'Power - orange/yellow' in the lists), while the other boards use per-bank colours.
- Switch Column pins 1 and 0 have no wire colour (matches the switch matrix page, which prints no colour for COLUMN 1 and COLUMN 0).
- '.' marker on Light Column / Switch Row / Light Row / Switch Column / Sol Bank headers is paired with the wire text 'key'.
- Leading ticks: Board Power has an unlabeled third tick; Cabinet RGB has 8 ticks without labels.
- Switch matrix: cell labels containing curly double quotes (“O”, “R”, “B” on switch cells 40/41/42; “O” “R” “B” “G” “L” “I” “R” on lamp cells 32/33/34/52/53/54/55) are kept with their quote characters.
- Lamp cell 18 is printed with an opening curly quote only: “GHOST TARGET 2 (no closing quote). Confirmed at 200 dpi.
- Switch matrix header: COLUMN 1 and COLUMN 0 carry no wire colour; COLUMN 7..2 carry GREEN GRAY, GREEN VIOLET, GREEN BLUE, GREEN BLACK, GREEN YELLOW, GREEN WHITE (shifted relative to the other three charts, where COLUMN 0 is GREEN WHITE).
- Switch cell 34 is printed over two lines 'POP PATH/' 'JUMP FAIL'; joined as 'POP PATH/ JUMP FAIL'. Cabinet cell labels carry parenthesised wire/opto notes ('(Purp / black)', '(opto #1 on aux, yellow / red)') exactly as printed, split over several lines and joined with single spaces.
- Lamp cell 35 reads 'NOT USED'.
