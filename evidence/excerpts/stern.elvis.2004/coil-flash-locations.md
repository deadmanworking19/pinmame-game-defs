# Elvis (Stern) — Coil & Flash Lamp Locations

Read from `Elvis_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 9, the front-matter drawing printed `DR. 7` ("COIL & FLASH LAMP LOCATIONS"), from
a 220 dpi render checked against the page's text layer. The left half is a playfield plan drawn with the back panel at
the top ("◀ Backpanel", "Playfield ▲") and the back panel's face above it; the right half carries typical switch, lamp
and coil wiring sketches, which are not transcribed.

Legend, verbatim: "= Coils / Flash Lamps above Playfield." (white box), "= Coils / Flash Lamps below Playfield." (black
box), "= Coils / Flash Lamps not on Playfield." (grey box), "Color = Color of Mini-Mars of Flash Lamp Bulb."
