# Elvis (Stern) — Switch Matrix Grid, Dedicated Switches & Locations

Read from `Elvis_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 6, the front-matter drawing printed `DR. 4` ("SWITCH MATRIX GRID, DEDICATED
SWITCHES & LOCATIONS"). Every cell was read from a 220 dpi render and checked against the page's text layer. Each grid
cell prints the switch number in a small black box, the mounting location to its right, the name in the middle and the
part number on the cell's own `Sw. Part Number:` line; that line is transcribed as "Part". Switch number =
(column − 1) × 8 + row.

## Location drawing and notes

The lower half of the page is a playfield plan drawn with the back panel to the left. Its legend reads "= Switches above
Playfield." (white box), "= Switches below Playfield." (black box) and "= Switches not on Playfield." (grey box). The
drawing calls out 9-28, 30-34, 36-43, 45-53 and 57-64, prints 14 and 15 twice each (two pairs of boxes at the trough
end) and 59 and 62 twice each (both slingshot contacts), and draws 54, 55, DS-1, DS-2, DS-3, DS-4 and DS-5 as grey
boxes, DS-2 and DS-4 beside the lower flippers and the others at the cabinet front and side. A black panel beside 55 reads "Optional Tournament Kit Required".
Switches 1-8, 29, 35, 44 and 56 have no callout. The individual box shading of each callout is not transcribed here.

Page notes, verbatim:

- "Switch Part Notes: ¥ Yen Coin Switch is 180-5091-00. Part Numbers which start with 515- or 500- include the bracket,
  target, and/or housing."
- "Switches 14 & 15 Part Note: Transmitter & Receiver OPTO PC Boards are used as Switches: Transmitter: 515-0173-00
  (Sw. 14 & 15); Receiver: 515-0174-00 (Sw. 14 & 15);"
- "Sw. 56 Part Note: The Switch is comprised of a Hanger Bracket (535-5319-00) and Contact Wire (535-7563-01) located in
  the Cabinet."
- "Some Switch Diodes may be located under the playfield, in the Cabinet or Backbox on Terminal Strips or Diode Boards
  and not on the assemblies."
- "DOTS: Diode On Terminal Strip, see Sec. 5, Chp.2, Playfield Wiring."

The grid and its legend print no normally-open or normally-closed marking for any matrix switch.
