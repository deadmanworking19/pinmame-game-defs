# Elvis (Stern) — Lamp Matrix Grid & Locations

Read from `Elvis_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 7, the front-matter drawing printed `DR. 5` ("LAMP MATRIX GRID & LOCATIONS").
Every cell was read from a 220 dpi render and checked against the page's text layer. Each cell prints the lamp number in
a small black box with the bulb on the same line and the name below. Lamp number = (row − 1) × 8 + column. Bracketed
letters are printed in a larger bold type. No cell is shaded and no cell carries a DOTS tag.

## Location drawing and notes

The lower half is a playfield plan with the back panel to the left ("Playfield ▲", "◀ Backpanel") and a boxed inset of
the back panel marked "(rear view)". Its legend reads "= Lamps above Playfield." (white box), "= Lamps below
Playfield." (black box) and "= Lamps not on Playfield." (grey box). As drawn:

- 1-59, 63 and 64 are black boxes on the playfield plan.
- 65-69 are white boxes in a row on the plan, and 70-72 are white boxes in a row at the plan's rear edge.
- 60-62 and 73-78 are grey boxes on the rear-view inset, which prints "Lamps 60-62 & 73-78 are located on the rear of
  the Back Panel."
- 79 and 80 are grey boxes at the cabinet front; a black panel beside 79 reads "Optional Tournament Kit Required".

Page notes, verbatim: "Lamp Part Notes: #555 Bulb Clear = 165-5002-00. #44 Bulb (Hvy. Fil.) Clear = 165-5000-44-HF. #44
Bulb Red = 165-5053-02. #44 Bulb Blue = 165-5053-05. #44 Bulb Yellow = 165-5053-06. #44 Bulb Orange = 165-5053-07 (can
sub. with Amber -03). See Section 4, Chapter 1, Parts Identification & Location, Pages 72-74 for more details on bulbs
and corresponding sockets. Some Lamp Diodes may be located under the playfield, in the Cabinet or Backbox on Terminal
Strips and not on or with the Lamp Socket. DOTS: Diode On Terminal Strip, see Sec. 5, Chapter 2, Playfield Wiring."

Above the grid the page prints the menu path `MAIN` GO TO DIAGNOSTICS MENU `MAIN` → `DIAG` GO TO LAMP MENU → `LAMP`
SINGLE LAMP TEST `LAMP`, beside "ROW & COLUMN LAMP TESTS".
