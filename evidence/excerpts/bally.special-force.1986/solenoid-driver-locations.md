# Bally Midway Special Force (game no. 0E47) — Solenoid Driver Locations

Source: `Bally_1986_Special_Force_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 45 (no printed folio), sheet headed `SPECIAL FORCE LAMP &
SOLENOID DRIVER LOCATIONS LISTING` / `M051-00E47-A012`. Transcribed by hand from a render of that page at its native
299 ppi. The `SOLENOID DRIVER LOCATIONS` table on the right of the sheet is transcribed in full, in printed order,
with its footnote and the wire colour legend beneath it. The `LAMP DRIVER LOCATIONS` tables on the same sheet are not
transcribed.
