# Harley-Davidson (Stern) — switch and lamp location drawings

Read from `Stern_Harley_Davidson_3rd_Edition_Pinball_Game_Service_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 7, the front-matter drawing
printed `DR. 5`: "SWITCH MATRIX GRID LOCATIONS" (left playfield) and "LAMP MATRIX GRID LOCATIONS" (right playfield). The
crop `switch-lamp-locations.webp` renders both playfield drawings and the legend line. Both playfields are drawn
upright, rear at the top and the flippers at the bottom. Legend under the drawings: "Legend Note: [white box] =
Switches/Lamps mounted above P/F. [black box] = Switches/Lamps mounted below the P/F. [grey box] = ...mounted in/on
Cabinet." Every box was read from a 200 dpi render and from enlarged crops of it.

Switch drawing, read box by box (black unless noted):

- Top centre: 27 (white) at the right ramp's top crossing; 44, 43, 42, 41 stacked top to bottom down the motorcycle
  trough left of centre; 35 and 36 beside them on the right (the lift).
- Top left: 33 at the left orbit; 26 (white) and 28 (white) below it at the left edge.
- Top right: 48 at the top of the right-centre lane; 45 below it at the super VUK; 34 at the right orbit; 25 (white)
  at the right ramp entrance.
- Centre left: 52 on the top bumper; 50, 49 and 51 on the other three bumpers down the left; 37 twice, above 29 and 30,
  at the motorcycle entrance; 29 and 30 at the motorcycle standup targets; 46 at the scoop below the bumpers.
- Centre right: 31 and 32 at the right-ramp standup targets.
- Drop targets: 20, 19, 18, 17 top to bottom along the left bank; 21, 22, 23, 24 top to bottom along the right bank.
- Lower playfield: 58 and 57 at the left return lane and outlane; 59 twice and 62 twice (the slingshot stacks); 61 and
  60 at the right return lane and outlane.
- Trough, lower right: 15 and 14 stacked right of the right flipper and again 14 and 15 stacked at the trough's
  right end; 13, 12, 11 stepping down toward the apron; 16 at the shooter lane.
- Cabinet (grey): 56 on the left cabinet wall; 54 lower left; 53 lower right; 08 right of the shooter lane.

  71 below 25.
- Left: 19, 18, 17 top to bottom beside the bumpers; 48 below 17; 12, 11, 10, 9 top to bottom along the left drop bank.
- Right: 13, 14, 15, 16 top to bottom along the right drop bank.
- Lower playfield: 66 and 65 at the left return lane and outlane; 67 and 68 at the right return lane and outlane; 69
  at the lower centre above the flippers; 70 twice at the two inlane ends above the flipper tips.
- Cabinet (grey): 47 at the lower right, beside the shooter lane.

Under the drawings the page prints "TYPICAL SWITCH SCHEMATIC" (column drive GRN-XXX to the N.O. terminal, blocking diode
from COM. to the row return WHT-XXX, N.C. terminal unused), "DEDICATED SWITCH SCHEMATIC" (GRY-XXX to N.O., COM. to
ground BLK) and "TYPICAL LAMP SCHEMATIC" (YEL-XXX 18V COL to the bulb, diode to RED-XXX GND ROW), and the note "All
Switch, Lamp & Coil assemblies require diodes. Some diodes are located under the playfield on Terminal Strips or Diode
Boards and not on the assemblies."
