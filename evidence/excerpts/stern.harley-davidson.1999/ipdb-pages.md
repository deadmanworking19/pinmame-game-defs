## 4455 — Harley-Davidson® (1st Edition), Stern

- "Notes: First game by Stern Pinball Inc. and the continuation of the production run of Sega's 1999 'Harley-Davidson®'

## 4664 — Harley-Davidson® (2nd Edition), Stern

- "Date Of Manufacture: November, 2002"; "Model Number: PINBALL-HD2"; "Common Abbreviations: HD2"; "MPU: Sega/Stern
  Whitestar"; "Production: 600 units (approximate)".
- "Notable Features: Flippers (2), Pop bumpers (4), 4-bank drop targets (2), Multiball (4 ball, 3 modes), Up-post between
  flippers, Shaker motor."
- "Toys: Harley motorcycle, Traffic stoplight."
- "Notes: Re-release of Stern's 1999 'Harley-Davidson® (1st Edition)' with upgraded parts such as different playfield
  motorcycles (1999 FLSTF Fat Boy® 1/10 scale, the 2000 FLSTF Fat Boy® 1/18 scale, and the 2001 FLSTS Heritage
  Springer® 1/18 scale) and chrome trim."
- Documentation: "Instruction Cards (Multilanguage)".

## 5277 — Harley-Davidson® (3rd Edition), Stern

- "Model Number: PINBALL I-0087"; "Common Abbreviations: HD3"; "MPU: Sega/Stern Whitestar"; no date of manufacture.
- "Notable Features: Flippers (2), Pop bumpers (4), Slingshots (2), 4-bank drop targets (2), Standup targets (4),
  Spinning target (1), Ramp (1), Cellar hole (1), Shaker motor, Up-post between flippers. The up-post is controlled by
  both the machine and the player."
- "Toys: Traffic light. Three Harley motorcycles, one of which does a wheelie on the playfield when it raises to kick out
  balls from underneath."
- "Notes: Re-release of Stern's 1999 'Harley-Davidson® (1st Edition)' with new art and chrome trim. Reportedly, there
  were 300 units made (this number has not been confirmed by us)."
- Documentation: "Pinball Game Service Manual" (`files/5277/Stern_Harley_Davidson_3rd_Edition_Pinball_Game_Service_Manual.pdf`).

## 4453 — Harley-Davidson®, Sega (context only)

- "Manufacturer: Sega Pinball, Incorporated, of Chicago, Illinois, USA (1994-1999)"; "Date Of Manufacture: September,
  1999"; "Model Number: 67".
- "Notes: Last game produced by Sega, production continued by Stern Pinball as Stern's 1999 'Harley-Davidson® (1st
  Edition)'."
- ROMs: "PinMAME Romset (1.03)" with a "display version 1.00" set and France, Germany, Italy and Spain sets.
