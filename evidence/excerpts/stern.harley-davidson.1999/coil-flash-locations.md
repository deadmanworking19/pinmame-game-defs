# Harley-Davidson (Stern) — coil and flash lamp location drawings

Read from `Stern_Harley_Davidson_3rd_Edition_Pinball_Game_Service_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 9, the front-matter drawing
printed `DR. 7`: "COIL LOCATIONS" (left playfield) and "FLASH LAMP LOCATIONS" (right playfield). The crop
`coil-flash-locations.webp` renders both drawings and the legend line. Both playfields are drawn upright, rear at the
top. Legend: "Legend Note: [white box] = Coils/Flashers mounted above P/F. [black box] = Coils/Flashers mounted below
the P/F. [grey box] = ...mounted in/on Cabinet." Every box was read from a 200 dpi render.

Coil drawing, read box by box (black unless noted):

- Top: 14 at the top centre between the two rear discs; 5 at the top of the motorcycle trough; 19 twice, once in a
  white diamond at the upper left and once in a white box right of the trough, joined by a thin line.
- Top right: 3 at the super VUK.
- Bumpers: 12 (top), 10 (centre), 9 (left), 11 (bottom) down the left.
- Centre: 4 at the scoop below the bumpers; 6 at the left drop bank's lower end; 7 at the top of the right drop bank.
- Lower playfield: 17 and 18 at the slingshots; 15 and 16 at the left and right flippers; 23 between the flippers;
  1 at the trough's right end; 2 at the shooter lane.
- Cabinet (grey): 13 at the lower left.
- Not drawn: 8, 20 (on the flash drawing), 21, 22 and 24.

Flash lamp drawing, read box by box (black unless noted):

- Top: F4 (white) twice, on the two rear discs at the top left and top right.
- Upper left: F2 (white) at the upper left; F5 four times, two left and two right of the motorcycle
  (upper pair and lower pair); F6 twice at the top right; F3 four times down the left, one at the upper left and three
  stacked lower down beside the top bumper.
- Left: F2 (white) a second time at the left edge level with the scoop; F1 twice below it.
- Centre: 20 below the centre bumper; F8 twice at the speedometer insert.
- Right: F7 twice down the right ramp's side.

Under the drawings the page prints "TYPICAL SWITCH, LAMP & COIL WIRING" (a switch with N.C., N.O. and COM. terminals,
WHT and GRN wires and a 1N4001 diode; a lamp socket with YEL, RED and COMMON wires and a 1N4001 diode; a coil with its
1N4004 diode, "Anode (Non-Banded Side)" to the "Drive Transistor" and "Cathode (Banded Side)" to the "Power Supply
Voltage"), and the same diode note as DR. 5.
