# Terminator 2: Judgment Day - Lamp Locations drawing

Source: `Terminator 2 Judgement Day Operations Manual.pdf` (ARC ARC retained source SHA-256 `8540d654b39c58ad3b19ece0f42eb1dfdb8460d249e9480f8906385c8ecdb16b`), PDF page 101, printed page 2-35, headed `Lamp Locations`. The crop is the playfield drawing on the right half of the page with its callouts; the lamp list on the left (lamp number, bulb, description) is outside the crop and not transcribed here, except that it prints the bulb of lamps 51 `Eyes Lower` and 52 `Eyes Upper` as `#44`.

The drawing is a top view of the playfield, rear at the top. Most lamp numbers are printed inside the outline of the insert they light (triangles, circles, rectangles and arrows). The others stand beside their insert: 17, 23, 24, 31 to 38, 41, 42, 66, 67, 76, 77, 78, 86, 87 and 88. Lamp 22 is printed twice, at the far left and on the right, for its two inserts; lamp 51 has two short arrows to two eye inserts in the middle of the playfield. At the top, `52 (in head)` is printed above the playfield with two leaders down into the skull at the upper left. Lamp 84 is printed below the drawing, outside the playfield.

Four unnumbered rectangles stand one above another on the playfield centre line, between the two diagonal rows of lamps 61-65 and 71-75 and reaching up past their tops: the CPU inserts, which carry no lamp-matrix number and are lit by the G.I. CPU string. Lamp 18 (Not Used) is not drawn. The pixel read of every callout and the control features are in `tools/seeds/williams/terminator-2-judgment-day-1991-callouts.json` (page `2-35`).
