# Terminator 2: Judgment Day - Solenoid Locations drawing

Source: `Terminator 2 Judgement Day Operations Manual.pdf` (ARC ARC retained source SHA-256 `8540d654b39c58ad3b19ece0f42eb1dfdb8460d249e9480f8906385c8ecdb16b`), PDF page 105, printed page 2-39, headed `Solenoid Locations`. The crop is the playfield drawing on the right half of the page with every balloon around it; the lists on the left half are the separate excerpt `solenoid-locations-lists.md`.

The drawing is a top view of the playfield, rear at the top. Solenoid and flashlamp numbers are printed in two ways:

- as solid black balloons printed on the part, without a leader: 3 on the outhole, 12 on the drop-target assembly at the upper left, 13, 14 and 15 on the upper-left, right-hand and lowest jet bumpers, 17 on the hot-dog insert above the apron, and 20 between the left lane and the five-target bank;
- as circled numbers with a leader to the part: 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 16, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27 and 28. 25, 26 and 27 each have two leaders, to two sockets (25's both in the left lane behind the five-target bank). 4, 8 and 9 sit at the bottom: 8's leader ends at the outlined box at the lower left, below the left outlane, 4's at the trough near the shooter lane, and 9's at the dashed outline of the shooter assembly below the playfield's front edge. 16's leader ends at the lower end of the left lane; 28's runs diagonally to the drop-target assembly, crossing 26's leader.

No balloon is printed for 1, 7 or 24. The pixel read of every callout and the control features are in `tools/seeds/williams/terminator-2-judgment-day-1991-callouts.json` (page `2-39`).
