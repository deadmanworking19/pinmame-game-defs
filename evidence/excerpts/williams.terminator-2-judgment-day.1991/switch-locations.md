# Terminator 2: Judgment Day - Switch Locations drawing

Source: `Terminator 2 Judgement Day Operations Manual.pdf` (ARC ARC retained source SHA-256 `8540d654b39c58ad3b19ece0f42eb1dfdb8460d249e9480f8906385c8ecdb16b`), PDF page 103, printed page 2-37, headed `Switch Locations`. The crop is the playfield drawing on the right half of the page with every callout around it; the switch parts list on the left half (switch number, switch assembly, description) is outside the crop and not transcribed here.

The drawing is a top view of the playfield, rear at the top, with the jet bumpers, slingshots, flippers, ramps, wireforms, lanes and inserts drawn in outline. Switch numbers are printed in four ways:

- in a circle with a leader line to the part: 25, 26, 27, 28, 31, 32, 33, 51, 53, 54, 55, 56, 57, 58, 62, 63, 64, 65, 66 and 78. 31, 32 and 33 sit on the right with leaders into the cannon mechanism; 62's leader crosses the playfield wall and ends with a tick on the left ramp's return wireform;
- with a short wedge arrow at the part: 36, 37 and 38 at the three middle stand-up targets, 46, 47 and 48 at the right target bank, and 71, 72, 73, 74 and 75 at the left five-target bank;
- printed on the part: 41, 42 and 43 inside the upper-left, right-hand and lowest jet bumper respectively, and 15, 16 and 17 along the trough below the right flipper, in that order from the outhole towards the shooter;
- printed beside the part without a leader: 44 and 45 beside the left and right slingshots, 61 beside the left ramp entrance, and 11, 12, 13 and 34 in the cabinet area outside the playfield outline (11 and 34 at the shooter end, 12 and 13 at the lower left).

No number is printed for 14, 18, 21, 22, 23, 24, 76 or 77, nor for the Not Used positions. The pixel read of every callout, the control features and the verifier's corrections are in `tools/seeds/williams/terminator-2-judgment-day-1991-callouts.json` (page `2-37`).
