# Terminator 2: Judgment Day - Lamp Locations lists

Source: `Terminator 2 Judgement Day Operations Manual.pdf` (ARC ARC retained source SHA-256 `8540d654b39c58ad3b19ece0f42eb1dfdb8460d249e9480f8906385c8ecdb16b`), PDF page 101, printed page 2-35, headed `Lamp Locations`. The page prints two lists that key the playfield drawing (the drawing is the separate excerpt `lamp-locations.md`): lamps 11-78 down the left side of the page, retained in two overlapping crops, `lamp-list-1.webp` (header and 11-45) and `lamp-list-2.webp` (44-78), and lamps 81-88 with the bulb part-number footnote below the drawing, `lamp-list-3.webp`. Transcribed manually from the image-only scan and checked against a native-resolution render of the page.
