# Terminator 2: Judgment Day - A-14615 1-bank drop target assembly

Source: `Terminator 2 Judgement Day Operations Manual.pdf` (ARC ARC retained source SHA-256 `8540d654b39c58ad3b19ece0f42eb1dfdb8460d249e9480f8906385c8ecdb16b`), PDF page 89, printed page 2-23, headed `A-14615 1-Bank Drop Target Assembly`. The crop holds the two exploded views and the whole parts list. Transcribed manually from the image-only scan and checked against a native-resolution render of the page.

The drawing shows the assembly from two sides. Balloon 5 points at the reset coil in the left view; balloon 23 points at a separate sub-assembly bolted to the right-hand side of the right view, below the target bracket.
