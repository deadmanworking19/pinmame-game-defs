# Harley-Davidson — Stern service bulletins 113, 114 and 121

Read from the three Internet Archive items `Stern_Pinball_Service_Bulletin_113`, `_114` and `_121` (files `sb113.pdf`,
`sb114.pdf`, `sb121.pdf`), from each PDF's text layer and a 70 dpi render of every page. Verbatim except where bracketed;
only the passages the record uses are quoted, and each bulletin's figures are described, not reproduced.

## Service Bulletin Nº 113 (three pages)

- "TO: Parts & Service Managers"; "DATE: December 10, 1999"; "SUBJ: H-D® Ball Guide Wire Form (OPTO Protect) and Butyrate
  -16 (Plastic) Support Plate Kit (SPI Nº 500-6408-67)". Page footers: "Stern™ Pinball, Inc. © 1999 / Technical Service
  Bulletin Nº 113", "SPI Nº 755-6408-67".
- Kit contents: "Two (2) new revised Ball Guides (5/8") with the Flanged Ends, SPI Nº 535-5356-19 (for Procedure 1 only)",
  "Support Plate with Decal, SPI Nº 515-8636-67", "#8-32 X 5/16" PPH Mach. Screw, SPI Nº 237-5613-00", "#6 X 3/8" HWH
  Screw, SPI Nº 234-5000-00", "Mini-Jewel Post (Clear), SPI Nº 550-5052-01".
- Explanation 1: "We have discovered through field testing & operator input, that some early production Harley-Davidson®
  Pinball Games have 5/8" Ball Guides that DO NOT have "stamped flange" ends (see, Fig. 1A). Constant pounding from the
  pinball can cause the Ball Guides to be hammered into the playfield (see, Fig. 1B & 1C). ... These Ball Guides are
  positioned at these locations (see Fig. 1) to protect the OPTO Switches at the Motorcycle entrance from being hit with
  the ball."
- "Games affected are within the following Serial Number range: 148598 - 149648."
- Procedure 1, Step 1: "Locate the existing two (2) Ball Guides (5/8") protecting the OPTO Switches at the left and right
  of the entrance to the Motorcycle (see Fig. 1)."
- Page 2, Fig. 1: a playfield plan with arrows to two ball guides either side of the motorcycle entrance, captioned
  "Location of the two Ball Guides protecting the OPTO Switches at the Motorcycle Entrance." Figs. 1A-1D: the guide is set
  "5/8"" from the playfield to its top, at the centre height of the ball.
- Page 3, Explanation 2: "It has also come to our attention that at some locations where game play is extreme, the
  Butyrate -16 (Plastic) cracks and breaks at the peninsula over the Right Stand-Up Target in front of the Motorcycle (see
  Fig. 2). Current production games include a Support Plate ① under the plastic to prevent possible breakage."
- "Games requiring addition are within the following Serial Nº range: 148598 - 150600."
- Fig. 2 side view labels the parts "new plate & decal over Buty. -16" and "existing support plate under Traffic Signal".

## Service Bulletin Nº 114 (two pages)

- "TO: Parts & Service Managers"; "DATE: February 11, 2000"; "SUBJ: Operator Alert #19 - Motor Relay (Harley-Davidson®
  Pinball)". Page 2 footer: "Stern™ Pinball, Inc. © 2000 / Technical Service Bulletin Nº 114".
- "Symptom: "Operator Alert #19-Motor Relay" appears in the Display." "Problem: This suggests the Motor Relay is
  inoperable; however, a number of other things can create this symptom." "Explanation: We have observed several
  different instances of mechanical problems related to the Lift (Motor) Assembly."
- Solution 1: "During Shipping, sometimes the Spring (B) will pop-off the Link (A) of the Lift (Motor) Assy. ... The Link
  (A) will then fall off, thus operation of the Lift Plate & Cycle Assy. will not move up & down."
- Solution 2: "The elongated end of the Link (A) should be bent ever-so-slightly away from the rotating Cam (C) so as not to
  bind up."
- Solution 3: "Ensure the Set Screw (D) is firmly secured tightly through the Cam (C) to the Motor Shaft on the FLAT CUT
  SIDE."
- Solution 4: "The switches are supposed to be set so the cam can stop on the Up-Switch (#35) and the Down-Switch (#36). If
  the rotating Cam (C) doesn't seem to move, you must enter Portals™ Diagnostics (Harley-Davidson Specific Test) ... and
  check for proper operation of the Motor & Switches."
- Solution 5: "We have also seen where the Motor Cam (C) catches on the motor mounting screws. Loosen the Set Screw (D) and
  pull the Cam (C) back enough so that it clears the threads of the mounting screws."
- Solution 6: "... There may be a dead spot in the motor (broken tooth on the gear inside the motor, which is unseen
  without actually opening the motor case)."
- Page 2: "Select the "RUN" Icon to automatically bring the Motor & Trough from the DOWN or UP position to the opposite
  position. Select the "PULSE" Icon to move the motor slowly UP and/or DOWN one pulse at a time. This test allows you to
  operate the Motor on this mechanism which is controlled by a Relay driven by Q19 on the I/O Power Driver Board for the
  purpose of troubleshooting." "Important: The Power Interlock Switch must be pulled out for this Test to Function."

## Service Bulletin Nº 121 (one page)

- "TO: Parts & Service Managers"; "DATE: February 05, 2001"; "SUBJ: Harley-Davidson® Plastic (Butyrate) -15 Support Plate
  Kit (SPI Nº: 502-5003-67)". Kit: "Metal Support Plate (535-8767-00)", "Mini-Jewel Post (550-5052-01)", "#6 X 3/8" HWH Scr.
  (234-5000-00)", "Service Bulletin 121 (756-5003-67)".
- Explanation: "It has come to our attention that some locations where game play is extreme, the Screened Plastic Piece
  (Butyrate) -15 (located @ the left side of motorcycle) cracks at the front (near the Right Bumper). (See Fig. 1)"
- Solution: "The plate is installed under the Screened Plastic (Butyrate) -15 to prevent it from breaking, if it already
  hasn't. Please Note: The individual Screened Plastic (Butyrate) piece is not available; The entire sheet, which contains
  pieces 01-35, must be ordered...use SPI Nº: 830-5960-XX."
