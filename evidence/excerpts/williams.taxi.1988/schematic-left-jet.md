# Taxi schematic: left jet connector disagreement

Official Williams schematics PDF 28, left portion of TAXI 79 drawing. The LEFT JET BUMPER is Sol 17, Q75, Blu-Brn, 5J3-7, downstream 5J6-7. Final manual PDF 72 instead prints 5J3-7:5J7-7. Both claims are kept here. The public output and bumper identity are corroborated, so the definition treats the downstream plug as a wiring detail: its structured wiring follows the PDF 72 wiring table, the game's own, and the device note states this drawing's 5J6-7. Neighboring Sol 19 uses Q73, Blu-Orn, 5J3-3 and 5J7-3.
