# Williams Barracora (game 510) - Parts supplement

Source: `Williams_1981_Barracora_Parts_Suppliment.pdf` (filename spelled so), the game's parts supplement 16P-510-110, October 1981, 13 PDF pages.

- PDF page 2: `R-8552-510 BACKGLASS` and `ROM SUMMARY`.
- PDF page 5: photographs captioned `3-BANK DROP TARGET (FIRST USED IN BARRACORA)`.
- PDF page 6: the 3-bank drop target parts list, and `BALL GATE LOCK ASSEMBLY (FIRST USED IN BARRACORA)` with its photograph and parts list.

Method: native page images read visually; the tables are transcribed in full. Spacing and capitals are as printed.

## Notes
