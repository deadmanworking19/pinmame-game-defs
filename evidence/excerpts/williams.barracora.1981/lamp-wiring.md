## General illumination

`6.3 VAC GENERAL ILLUMINATION` enters playfield connector 8J8/8P8 on four wires: YEL pin 5, YEL-WHT pin 2, VIO pin 1 and WHT pin 4. Under the heading `GENERAL ILLUMINATION`, two bulbs are drawn between the pin-5 and pin-2 lines and two between the pin-1 and pin-4 lines, each pair continued to the right with dashes. No bulb count is printed.

## Notes

- The missing `57` entry is an omission in the bulb list only: the matrix on the same sheet draws cell 57 PLAYFIELD, and Figure 1 of the instruction booklet prints `BONUS 4` in that cell (`lamp-matrix.md`).
- The dashes on the GI pairs mean the strings continue beyond the drawn bulbs; this sheet does not give the number of GI sockets.
