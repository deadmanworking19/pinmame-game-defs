# Gladiators (Gottlieb 1993)

Coverage: **partial**. Every controller address is enumerated and named from the ROM's own service tests, polarity is
settled for every switch, and the mechanisms are described. Playfield positions come from one retained table and stay
observed, so `coverage.missing` is `["spatial_placement"]`: no factory drawing or second, independent table checks them.
The definition, its seed and the spatial report are generated by `tools/curate_gladiators.py`.

## Identity

- Premier Technology (trade name Gottlieb), November 1993, model 737, Gottlieb System 3, 1,995 units (IPDB 1011,
  excerpt `evidence/excerpts/gottlieb.gladiators.1993/ipdb-page.md`). OPDB `G4P9q-MLnOY`.
- One PinMAME driver, `gladiatr`, with no clones. Its power-up screen reads `GLADIATORS / (Install 4 Balls) / Game #737,
  ID #000000 / GPROM CHECKSUM-FC59`; model 737 is IPDB's model number for the machine.
- Pinned `src/wpc/gts3games.c` lines 505-516 declare it `INITGAME2(gladiatr, DMD, FLIP8283, 4, SNDBRD_GTS3, 4)`: a 128x32
  DMD, flipper buttons copied into matrix switches 82 (left) and 83 (right), four balls, and `hw.lampCol = 4`, so there
  is no auxiliary driver board and lamps 120-127 do not exist on this machine.

## Evidence

- **No manual.** IPDB lists the instruction manual pages 2-5 and the coil, fuse, switch and switch-matrix diode board
  charts as "Availability limited by copyright" without a link; archive.org and other manual archives had nothing.
- **The ROM's service tests** stand in for the manual, as on Stargate. Runs of the pinned LibPinMAME on `gladiatr`
  (`evidence/runtime/gts3/gladiators-gladiatr-service-tests.json`, built by `tools/gladiators_runtime_evidence.py` from
  runs retained under `review-artifacts/gottlieb.gladiators.1993/runtime/`) step through:
  - self-test 3 **Lamp Matrix Test**, which blinks one public lamp per step and names it;
  - self-test 4 **Relay & Solenoid Test**, which numbers drivers from 0, one below the public address, and fires the
    displayed driver on the credit button;
  - self-test 5, which first lists the **Inoperative Switches** by itself (about a second per switch) and then becomes
    the **Switch Matrix Test**: `ALL SWITCHES OPEN` with every address at 0, and `SWITCH:nn` with the ROM's name while
    one address is held at 1;
  - self-test 8 **Front Door Test**, which counts coins per chute; plus a tournament and coin-door probe, one scripted
    game and a ramp-motor sensor run.
- Gladiators' menu differs from Stargate's: there is no Switch Edges test, and the Front Door Test is number 8.
- **The known-working script** is Kiwi's `Gladiators (Premier 1993) v1.1.1.vbs` in the pinned
  `jsm174/vpx-standalone-scripts` corpus (bindings in `kiwi-script-bindings.md`), with Kiwi's earlier v1.0.1 in
  `sverrewl/vpxtable_scripts`.
- **The retained table** is `Gladiators (Premier 1993) v1.1.1 VR.vpx`, a VR edit of Kiwi's v1.1.1 ("VR room added by
  MaX in 2024 with permission"), retained under the working root's
  `vpx-sources/gottlieb/gladiators-1993/kiwi-v1.1.1-vr/` with its vpxtool extraction, manifest and OBJ export. Its
  embedded script differs from the pinned v1.1.1 script only by the VR-room additions, so every binding is the same;
  its line numbers are shifted. `vpx-table-objects.md` lists each placed object, its coordinate and the lines that bind
  it.

## Controller addressing

- Switches and lamps use Gottlieb's decimal numbers: tens digit is the strobe, units digit the return or row (0-7). The
  service tests print 100-117 as A0-A7 and B0-B7.
- Public 1 is the closed contact the CPU sees (`xvia_0_a_r` complements the column and no inverted-switch mask is set).
  The Switch Matrix Test reads `ALL SWITCHES OPEN` at 0 and names the held switch at 1, so every used matrix switch is
  recorded `normally_closed: false`.
- The flipper buttons are 141 (right) and 143 (left) in PinMAME's flipper column. `core_updateSw` copies them into 83 and
  82 on every update, so a host must drive 141/143, never 82/83. With the game-over relay (32) energized it also
  fabricates the synthetic flipper outputs 45/46 (right) and 47/48 (left).
- Solenoids are public 1-32, the Relay & Solenoid Test's driver number plus one. No driver is NOT USED. 26, 31 and 32
  are the A (lightbox), T (tilt / playfield G.I.) and Q (game over) relays.
- Lamps 0-117 less 80, 90, 110 and 111 are fitted. 112-117 are the six backbox lightbox lamps and 1 is the credit-button
  lamp.
- PinMAME's `gladiatr` block in `gts3.c` (lines 708-715) types solenoids 8-9, 14-22 and 24 as #89 flashers and lamps 2-7
  (WEAPON), 12-17 (DOUBLE) and 94-97 (left billboard) as strobed LEDs. The ROM's flasher names end in 67, the #67 bulb.

## Mechanisms and recreation notes

**Ball path.**

- The machine holds four balls. A drained ball lands on OUTHOLE (23); the OUTHOLE coil (29) moves it into the trough
  (TROUGH, 33), and BALL RELEASE (28) feeds the shooter lane, where SHOOTER LANE ROLLOVER (24) sees the ball waiting
  at the plunger. The SHOOTER LANE opto (100) sits at the top of the lane (the table's trigger `sw100` is at its exit),
  so it sees the ball after the plunge.
- The PLUNGER GATE (13) is a post across the lane. The script drops it while 13 is on.
- At Start, the ROM pulsed only 13 and fired no ball release. The host supplied the shooter-lane switches itself, so
  the run does not show where a game's first ball waits or how it reaches the shooter lane.
  After a drain, the ROM reset the 3-bank (10), energized 28 for about 0.7 s, fired 29, then energized Q (32). 29 fired
  about 2.9 s after 23 rose, by which time the scenario had opened 23 again, so the run does not show whether the ROM
  waits for 23 before kicking.
- In the scripted game, Q stayed off from Start until that drain, and the flipper presses raised nothing. The run does
  not show what energizes Q during a played first ball. A recreation should follow the ROM's 32.

**Catapult.**

- The catapult is a moving rail ramp. The ball must be shot at the right moment to drop into the Catapult Hole for 50M
  (IPDB). The lamp matrix has `L HOLE "CATAPULT 50M"` (43) and `"CATAPULT"` (73), and the CATAPULT & BALL flasher is 9.
- RAMP MOTOR RELAY (B) (23) drives the ramp motor, and RAMP MOTOR (30) senses the ramp's home position.
- The script runs it as a one-solenoid reversing mechanism of 136 steps while 23 is on, with 30 closed at step 0, and
  moves the collidable ramp section with it.
- The ROM energized 23 in its ball search and at the drain. Toggling 30 never made the ROM release 23, so the ROM's own
  stop condition is not shown. A recreation should run the motor while 23 is on and report 30 at home.

**Holes.**

- The four holds are TOP HOLE (21 → coil 7), LEFT HOLE (31 → 6), CENTER UPKICKER (22 → 11; the script makes it a
  vertical upkicker) and RIGHT UPKICKER (32 → 12).
- With each switch held for 1.5 s, the ROM fired its coil twice.
- The ROM's ball search, about 13-15 s after the plunge in both scripted games (in run gameplay with playfield switches
  pulsed in between), fires 6, 7, 11, 12 and 13 and energizes 23.

**3-bank drop targets.**

- DROP TARGET #1-#3 (15, 25, 35) are reset by 3 BANK RESET (10).
- The ROM resets the bank at power-up and when it serves a ball. With all three held down during the first ball, it did
  not reset them.

**Pop bumpers and kicking targets.**

- Three pop bumpers (10/11/12 → coils 1/2/3) and two kicking targets (13/14 → 4/5) are fired by the CPU from their
  switches. A consumer drives only the switch.
- 5 was not seen in play, which PinMAME's four-frame solenoid smoothing could explain. The test fired it.
- The script models the kicking targets as the two slingshots, and the table draws a kicking-target primitive
  (`LeftKicking`/`RightKicking`, mesh `KickingT`) in each slingshot position, which the script swings on a hit. So the
  parts are kicking targets standing where slingshots usually are; no manual confirms it.

**Flippers.**

- Four flippers on two two-stage buttons (IPDB): a light press fires the lower flipper of that side, a heavy press the
  lower and upper.
- The ROM reads one switch per side (`L/R FLIPPER (SENSOR BD)`, 82/83), and PinMAME has no input for the heavy stage. A
  recreation that wants the two stages must stage the upper flipper from the button itself.
- The script fires both flippers of a side from the synthetic hold output.

**Relays and cabinet.**

- A (26) switches the backbox lightbox, T (31) the playfield G.I. (off while energized), and Q (32) enables the flippers.
- The SHAKER MOTOR (25) and KNOCKER (30) are in the cabinet. TICKET/COIN METER (27) is an optional accessory.

**Flashers.**

- The script draws 8 (VOLCANO & MOON), 9 (CATAPULT & BALL), 22 (L & R DRAGON) and 24 (SHIELD & C DRAGON) in its backbox,
  which fits the backglass-art names. No manual confirms it, so they are not classified.
- 14-21 (spot target, motor ramp dome, right ramp dome, right upkicker dome, spinner, three lightstrips) are drawn with
  the playfield flashers. 14-17 are placed at the lights under the table's flasher domes and 18 at the bulb light beside
  the spinner. The table draws the three lightstrips (19-21) only as flasher sprites, so they are not placed.

**Coin door.**

- TOURNAMENT (5) and FRONT DOOR (SERVICE) (6) are coin-door switches. Neither changed the ROM's behaviour in attract
  mode or at Start in these runs.
- Which door position closes 6 is not shown. The game took coins with 6 at either level; both games started with 6 at 0.

## Table defects (not machine facts)

- `vpmNudge.TiltSwitch = 151` (line 255) names no System 3 address. The machine's tilt is -7.
- The key handlers write 82/83 directly before `vpmKeyDown`/`vpmKeyUp`. This has no effect, because PinMAME overwrites
  them from 141/143.
- `BumpersDisable` mutes the pop bumpers while both buttons are held. That is the table's behaviour, not the machine's.

## What closes the record

The placements are observed only: they all come from one table. The instruction manual's playfield location drawings,
or a second table built independently from the machine, would validate them. The same drawings would also settle the
backbox flashers 8, 9, 22 and 24, the lightstrips 19-21 and the G.I. bulb count.
