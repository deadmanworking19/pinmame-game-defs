# Space Station (Williams, 1987)

Coverage: **partial - every controller address enumerated and named; wiring, polarity, mechanisms, both production
backbox insert boards and recreation behaviour validated; every playfield device placed from the retained table, but
spatial placement stays open because the placements are not yet checked against the factory drawings and the G.I.
comes only from the table's grouping.**

The definition is generated by `tools/curate_space_station.py`; the spatial review is
`reports/spatial/williams/space-station-1987.md`, the runtime evidence is derived by
`tools/space_station_runtime_evidence.py`, and the ROM's service-name tables are decoded by

## Identity and variants

SPACE STATION is Williams model 552 (IPDB 2261, OPDB `G5QXO-MQowd`), produced from December 7, 1987 to March 10, 1988
(3,804 units per IPDB) on a System 11B CPU board (D-11883-552) with the D-11609 alphanumeric display board, the
D-11581-552 audio board and the D-11813-552 Aux Power Driver Board. The Operations Manual is 16-552-101 (January 1988),
with a two-page Amendments and Additions sheet. Design by Barry Oursler, software by Ed Boon.

The pinned catalog has one driver, `spstn_l5` (LA-5 game ROMs, L-1 sound ROMs), catalogued 1988; the machine year is
the 1987 production start. Production machines differ in parts that do not change the controller contract:

- **Two backbox insert boards.** The manual documents the "Williams Version", with controlled lamps behind the Williams
  logo (46-48) and the shuttle flames (37 and 49 a single lamp, 57 a lamp pair) and G.I. behind the station wheel; the
  L-5 lamp test is written for the "Wheel Version", whose six station-wheel lamps are controlled (48, 47, 46, 37, 57 and
  41 are STATION WHEEL 1-6) while the logo and flame lamps are G.I., and 49 drives the big flame's lamp pair. The
  Williams Version has four extra G.I. lamps. Lamp 41 is fitted only on the Wheel Version, which is why the Lamp-Matrix
  Table prints it "Not Used" while the Lamps list prints "Dome (insert bd)". Source: an owner's photographic comparison
  (excerpt `production-variants`), consistent with the two manual pages and the ROM's names.
- **Translites** with punched, unpunched or no windows in front of the station-wheel lamps (IPDB).
- **Ramps.** The right ramp is black; owners report a clear one on an early Williams Version game.
- **Prototypes** had an opaque black mini-playfield with leaf switches and a drop target at the top of the playfield
  under the mini-playfield, removed for cost; production playfields show its unpainted footprint (IPDB). No pinned
  driver is a prototype.

## Controller facts that matter to a recreation

- **Matrices.** Switches and lamps are System 11's sequential 1-64 (column `(n-1)/8+1`, row `(n-1)%8+1`). The switch
  matrix is read uninverted, and the ROM's Switch Levels test names every fitted switch at public 1, so every matrix
  contact, the opto boards included, rests open. Unfitted matrix positions: 2, 36, 44, 49 and 51; the ROM still names 2
  "BALL TILT" and 51 "LEFT 10 PTS". 34 is the West German scoreboard switch, fitted only on West German machines, whose
  game ROM is not in the pinned catalog; the L-5 ROM names it "NOT USED".
- **A/C select relay at 12.** The eight switched drivers serve an "A" load at 1-8 while 12 is released and a "C" load at
  25-32 while it is energized; PinMAME publishes them separately. 1-4 and 6-8 are coils, 5 is unused; 25-31 are
  flasher circuits and 32 is the Left Dock Kickbig coil. Unlike later System 11 games no switch mirrors the relay.
- **Special solenoids.** 17 is the right dock kicker, which the ROM fires itself; 18-22 (left sling, right jet, right
  sling, left jet, lower jet) are mapped in PinMAME to switches 63, 61, 64, 60 and 62, so each coil rises with its own
  switch while the enable is on, as the physical special-solenoid circuits do. A consumer drives only the switch.
- **Game-on 23** rises when a game starts and drops at the third plumb-bob tilt and at game over.
- **Flippers.** `FLIP_SWNO(55,56)`: drive the buttons at 84 (left) and 82 (right). PinMAME copies them into the lane-
  change switches 55 and 56 and fabricates 45-48. Writes to 55/56 are overwritten.
- **G.I.** Three relays, PinMAME-typed as reverse AC G.I. outputs: 9 turns the playfield's white G.I. off when
  energized, 10 switches the playfield from white to green bulbs when energized (multiball), and 11 turns the backbox
  insert G.I. off. The ROM holds 9 and 11 through a tilt and flickers them in light shows.
- **Displays.** Two rows: player 1 and 2 are seven 16-segment characters each (indices 0 and 1, segment starts 1 and 9),
  player 3 and 4 seven 7-segment digits (indices 2 and 3, starts 21 and 29). The service tests print names on players 1
  and 2 and test numbers on player 3. The ROM's font draws 0 and O, and 5 and S, with the same segments.

## Mechanisms and behaviour

- **Trough and serve.** A drained ball rests on the outhole switch (10); the outhole kicker (1) moves it into the
  trough, whose switches 13 (left), 12 and 11 (right) count the balls; the shooter lane feeder (2) kicks the lead ball
  into the shooter lane (43). The ROM feeds again 2.1 s later if the trough has not changed. The plunged ball rides a
  habitrail to the mini-playfield. A docked ball makes the ROM serve a new one.
- **1-2-3 mini-playfield.** The raised blue mini-playfield (R-11780) has three rollover lanes 1-3 (14-16, lamps 14-16).
  Below it, the U-S-A lanes (38-40) are reached round the left past the Top Left Roll-Under (37).
- **Ball poppers.** The left popper (41, coil 3; the rulesheet's "centre" popper) lifts its ball to the Space Station,
  which guides it to a lock; with two balls locked it starts multiball. The right popper (42, coil 4) lifts its ball to
  the mini-playfield. The ROM fired 4 0.45 s after 42 closed.
- **Space Station.** A wheel and Rotating Ball Guide on a shaft turned by a 24 VAC, 11 rpm motor, switched by a relay
  on 16, in one direction only. A cam under the playfield interrupts OPTO 1 and OPTO 2 of the C-11872 opto positioner,
  read at 52 and 53. The guide sends a ball from the ramp or the left popper to the right lock (45) or, over the wire
  ramp past 48, to the left lock (47). At power-up the ROM runs the motor until the switches change into (52 open, 53
  closed), reached either way round in the reactive probes, and stops after about 5.3 s if they never change. In play
  it turns the station to the empty lock (rulesheet); with the switches held still it ran the motor for 90 s. The cam
  profile is not printed; the known-working VPW script models two half-turn bits in quadrature (52 closed from 180 to
  360 degrees, 53 from 90 to 270, home at 90) with the guide sending balls left at 0 and 180 degrees and right at 90 and
  270, which satisfies the ROM's homing.
- **Docks.** Right lock 45, kicked by the Right Dock Kickback (17, the Right Eject Hole mechanism, like the shooter-lane
  feeder), entry 46 on the station exit ramp; left lock 47, kicked by the Left Dock Kickbig (32), entry 48 on the wire
  ramp. "Dock W/L" (lamps 8 and 34) must be lit for a dock to hold a ball; otherwise it is ejected (rulesheet). The
  gameplay run showed DOCKED RIGHT, DOCKED LEFT and then multiball ("PILOT ONE ... LAUNCH PROBES") from the left popper.
- **Ramp and Stop 'N' Score.** A single drop target (33, reset by 8) guards the right ramp. Knocking it down starts the
  Stop 'N' Score timer; the ROM raised it about 24 s later and retried every 1.4 s while it stayed down.
- **3-bank drop targets** (57-59, reset by 6) advance the bonus multiplier (1X/2X/4X lamps 11-13) and light Extra Ball
  (lamp 5). The manual puts 57 at the upper target and 59 at the lower; the ROM's names run the other way, which changes
  nothing because the bank scores alike and resets together.
- **Left re-entry kickback** (13) fires within one poll of the left drain lane (17) closing while lamp 17 is lit. There
  are no inlanes; both drain lanes are outlanes.
- **Jets and slings.** Left (60/21), right (61/19) and lower (62/22) jet bumpers; left (63/18) and right (64/20)
  slingshots, each with a separate kicker actuating switch that has no matrix address.
- **Targets.** SHUTTLE (18-24) and STATION (25-31) standups, the CHANGE bullseye (35) that rerolls the SHUTTLE or STATION
  award on the two seven-insert ladders (50-56 and 58-64), and two 10-point rubber switches (50 top right, 54 lower right).
- **Knocker** (7) is in the backbox (B-10686-2); the table allows a ticket dispenser in its place.
- **Ball search.** With an empty trough at power-up the ROM kicks 17, 32, 4 and 3; in play it searches through 3, 4,
  13, 1, 8, 2 and the docks.

## Manual notes

- The page-69 solenoid wiring fold-out mislabels four loads (03C as Left Dock Kickbig, 05C as Top P/f, 07A as a 3-bank
  raise, 07C as Flame) and calls 21 the Center Jet Bumper; the Solenoid Table and the ROM's Coil Test agree with each
  other against it. Its supply frames give 25 V for 1, 2, 8, 9-12, 15, 16, 19, 21, 22 and the C loads, and 50 V for 3,
  4, 6, 7, 13, 17, 18 and 20.
- The switch and lamp wiring drawings label the row 2 crossing of column 7 "49" where the tables print 50.
- The All Lamps text swaps the lamp connectors (it says 1J6 columns, 1J7 rows); the table prints 1J7 columns, 1J6 rows.
- The Switches list gives the right trough switch as 5647-09938-00, the Ball Trough Switches page as A-11660 with a
  5647-12073-08; both are microswitches.
- The retained script's comments call 17 and 32 inlanes. It also binds 50 to its lower right 10-point rubber and 54 to
  its upper right one, crossed against the manual's matrix, parts list and drawing and the ROM's names (TOP RGT and
  LOW RGT 10 PTS); both score alike, so the definition places them as the machine has them and notes the table defect.

## Placement notes

- Inserts are the `lN` lights `vpmMapLights` binds; the seven insert-board lamps' lights are parked above the playfield
  and take no placement.
- Flashers 15 and 29 are five domes on the top panel at the rear edge (alternating 15, 29, 15, 29, 15, as an IPDB photo
  shows); the table's lights sit 12-13 units behind the edge and are clamped to it. Flashers 30 and 31 and the insert
  G.I. are backbox-only.
- The playfield G.I. is the table's 26 white (`GI`) and 25 green (`gi_green`) bulb lights; 9 governs both, 10 the green.
- The Space Station's optos and motor are projected onto the station's rotation centre; the outhole, feeder, popper,
  drop-reset and dock coils onto their mechanisms' objects.
