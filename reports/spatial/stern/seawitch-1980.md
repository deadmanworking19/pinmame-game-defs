## Remaining blockers

- Public lamps 29/Q15/J2-23, 41/Q42/J3-21, 51/Q51/J3-15 and 57/Q56/J3-10 have no printed load in either Seawitch list. All four fitments remain unknown; 51 changes during gameplay. Resolve by tracing an unrestored LDA harness/socket, not by another all-lamp sweep.
- The lN insert overlays do not model bulbs. Locate each real controlled-lamp socket using a bulb-level drawing or independently reconciled underside photograph; retain its measured centers and the transform. Shoot again has separate playfield/backbox loads on Q3.
- Uncontrolled GI is fitted but its emitter inventory/positions are unproved. The GI collection has 54 member occurrences, 53 distinct names and duplicate Light35; render/glow membership is not a socket count.
- Switches and coil effects have observed table geometry/projections only. The retained location diagrams have been visually reconciled for side/order/topology but no independent quantified control fit has validated coordinates. The playfield slam contact and hidden out-hole hardware remain unlocated.
